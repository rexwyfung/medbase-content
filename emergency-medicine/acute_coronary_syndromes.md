# Acute Coronary Syndromes (急性冠狀動脈綜合症)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Triage Category & Local Epidemiology

### Definition of Acute Coronary Syndromes
* **Spectrum of Ischemic Heart Disease (缺血性心臟病)** encompassing:
* **Unstable Angina (UA)**: Myocardial ischemia without necrosis.
* **Non-ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction (NSTEMI)**: Ischemia → subendocardial necrosis (+ve cardiac biomarkers).
* **ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction (STEMI)**: Transmural necrosis → full-thickness injury.

### HK Hospital Authority (HA) Triage Categories
* **Category 1 (Critical)**: Cardiac arrest, cardiogenic shock, active VF/VT, severe respiratory distress.
* **Category 2 (Emergency)**: Active typical ischemic chest pain, severe autonomic symptoms (e.g., profuse diaphoresis). Target: Door-to-ECG < 10 mins.

### Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong
* **Prevalence**: Leading cause of medical ED attendances and ICU/CCU admissions in HK.
* **Demographics**: ↑ incidence in South Asian populations and older Chinese adults.
* **Major Risk Factors**: Diabetes mellitus (糖尿病) (highly prevalent locally), hypertension (高血壓), hyperlipidemia (高血脂), smoking.

## 2. Acute Pathophysiology & Mechanism of Injury (MOI)

### Plaque Rupture & Thrombus Cascade
* **Atherosclerotic Plaque Rupture**: Fibrous cap tears → exposes thrombogenic core (collagen, tissue factor).
* **Platelet Activation**: Adhesion and aggregation → White thrombus formation.
* **Coagulation Cascade**: Thrombin generation → Fibrin mesh + RBCs → Red thrombus.

### Degrees of Coronary Occlusion
* **Partial/Transient Occlusion**: → UA or NSTEMI (subendocardial ischemia).
* **Complete/Prolonged Occlusion**: → STEMI (transmural ischemia → infarction cascade within 15-30 mins).

### Ischemic Cellular Cascade
* **Metabolic Shift**: Aerobic → anaerobic glycolysis → ↑ lactic acid → intracellular acidosis.
* **Pump Failure**: Failure of Na+/K+ ATPase → intracellular Na+ accumulation → cellular edema → apoptosis/necrosis.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (ABCDE & Secondary Survey Findings ONLY)

### Typical Symptoms
* **Chest Pain (胸痛)**: Retrosternal, crushing, pressure-like, tight.
* **Radiation**: Left arm, ulnar surface, neck, jaw, epigastrium.
* **Autonomic Symptoms**: Profuse diaphoresis (出冷汗), nausea/vomiting, pallor.
* **Precipitating Factors**: Exertion, cold, stress. Relieved by rest/nitroglycerin (historically, though unreliable).

### Atypical Presentations (Silent MI)
* **High-Risk Groups**: Elderly, diabetics, females.
* **Atypical Symptoms**: Dyspnea (呼吸困難), unexplained syncope/presyncope, isolated epigastric pain, confusion, profound fatigue.

### Physical Examination Findings
* **Vitals**: Tachycardia (sympathetic drive), bradycardia (inferior MI/AV block), hypotension (RV infarct/cardiogenic shock).
* **Cardiovascular**: S4 gallop (stiff ventricle), S3 gallop (heart failure), new systolic murmur (papillary muscle rupture/VSD), elevated JVP (RV infarction).
* **Respiratory**: Basal crepitations (pulmonary edema).
* **Extremities**: Cool, clammy peripheries, poor capillary refill.

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses (Rule-Out Life Threats)

### Major Life-Threatening Differentials (The "Big Six")
| Diagnosis | Key Differentiating Clinical Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aortic Dissection** | Tearing/ripping pain radiating to back, pulse deficits, BP asymmetry, widened mediastinum. |
| **Pulmonary Embolism** | Pleuritic chest pain, dyspnea, hypoxia, tachycardia, DVT risks. |
| **Tension Pneumothorax**| Sudden onset, unilateral ↓ breath sounds, hyperresonance, tracheal deviation. |
| **Esophageal Rupture** | Boerhaave syndrome, post-retching pain, subcutaneous emphysema, Hamman’s crunch. |
| **Cardiac Tamponade** | Beck's triad (hypotension, ↑ JVP, muffled heart sounds), pulsus paradoxus. |
| **Acute Pericarditis** | Sharp/pleuritic pain, relieved by leaning forward, diffuse PR depression, friction rub. |

## 5. Emergency Investigations (Point-of-Care to Formal)

### 12-Lead ECG Findings & Criteria
* **Target**: Complete within 10 minutes of ED arrival.
* **STEMI Criteria**:
	* **Men < 40 yrs**: ≥ 2.5 mm STE in V2-V3.
	* **Men ≥ 40 yrs**: ≥ 2.0 mm STE in V2-V3.
	* **Women (any age)**: ≥ 1.5 mm STE in V2-V3.
	* **All other leads**: ≥ 1 mm STE in ≥ 2 contiguous leads.
	* **New LBBB**: Considered STEMI equivalent in appropriate clinical context.
	* **Posterior MI**: Horizontal ST depression (V1-V3), tall R waves, upright T waves.
	* **RV Infarct**: ST elevation in right-sided leads (V4R).

### Cardiac Biomarkers
* **High-Sensitivity Troponin (hs-cTn T/I) (肌鈣蛋白)**: Rises within 1-3 hrs. Perform 0-hour and 1-hour/2-hour delta tests per HA protocol.
* **CK-MB**: Less sensitive, mainly used for detecting re-infarction (rapid clearance).

### Bedside & Radiological Imaging
* **CXR**: Evaluate for cardiomegaly, pulmonary edema (Batwing appearance, Kerley B lines), widened mediastinum.
* **POCUS (Echocardiography)**: Assess for Regional Wall Motion Abnormalities (RWMA), pericardial effusion, right ventricular strain (D-shape septum).

## 6. Acute Resuscitation & Medical Management Algorithms

### Immediate Resuscitation (Initial ED Management)
* **Oxygen Therapy**: Target SpO2 94-98%. *Avoid routine O2 if normoxic (O2 causes coronary vasoconstriction).*
* **Aspirin**: 300mg PO chewed (antiplatelet loading).
* **Nitrates**: Sublingual GTN (0.5mg) / IV Nitroglycerin infusion (vasodilation, preload ↓).
* **Analgesia**: IV Morphine (2.5-5mg aliquots) + IV Metoclopramide.

### Dual Antiplatelet Therapy (DAPT) & Anticoagulation
* **P2Y12 Inhibitors**:
	* **Ticagrelor**: 180mg PO loading (preferred in STEMI/primary PCI).
	* **Clopidogrel**: 300-600mg PO loading (if high bleeding risk or thrombolysis).
* **Anticoagulants**:
	* **Enoxaparin (LMWH)**: 1mg/kg SC (NSTEMI/UA).
	* **Heparin (UFH)**: IV bolus + infusion (STEMI before PCI).
	* **Fondaparinux**: 2.5mg SC (if high bleeding risk/conservative approach).

### STEMI Reperfusion Strategy
* **Primary PCI (緊急冠狀動脈介入治療)**: Gold standard. Target Door-to-Balloon < 90 mins.
* **Thrombolysis (溶血治療)**: Tenecteplase/Alteplase. Use if PCI unavailable within 120 mins. Target Door-to-Needle < 30 mins.
* **Rescue PCI**: Indicated if thrombolysis fails (< 50% ST resolution at 60-90 mins).

## 7. Key ED Procedures & Technical Interventions (Mechanics & Consent)

### Extended ECG Lead Placement
* **Right-Sided ECG (V4R)**: Place V4 lead at 5th intercostal space, right mid-clavicular line. Used to confirm RV infarction.
* **Posterior ECG (V7-V9)**: Place leads horizontally on the left back at the level of V6. V7 (posterior axillary line), V8 (mid-scapular line), V9 (paraspinal line).

### Transcutaneous Pacing (for Unstable Bradycardia/AV Block)
* **Mechanics**:
	* **Pad Placement**: Anteroposterior (preferred) or Anteroapical.
	* **Settings**: Set rate to 60-70 bpm. Start output at 10mA, increase by 10mA until electrical capture (wide QRS post-pacing spike), then ensure mechanical capture (femoral pulse match).
* **Consent**: Emergency implied consent if unstable; inform family immediately. Consider IV sedation (Midazolam/Fentanyl) as pacing is painful.

## 8. Clinical Decision Rules & Risk Scoring

### HEART Score (For ED Chest Pain Risk Stratification)
| Criteria | 0 Points | 1 Point | 2 Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **History** | Slightly suspicious | Moderately suspicious | Highly suspicious |
| **ECG** | Normal | Non-specific repolarization | Significant ST deviation |
| **Age** | < 45 | 45-64 | ≥ 65 |
| **Risk Factors** | None | 1-2 | ≥ 3 or history of atherosclerosis |
| **Troponin** | ≤ Normal limit | 1-3x Normal limit | > 3x Normal limit |
* **Scoring**: 0-3 = Low Risk (discharge/early OPD), 4-6 = Moderate Risk (admit/observe), 7-10 = High Risk (early invasive).

### GRACE Risk Score
* **Parameters**: Age, HR, SBP, Creatinine, Killip class, Cardiac arrest at presentation, ST deviation, Troponin elevation.
* **Utility**: Predicts 6-month mortality in NSTEMI/UA to guide timing of early invasive strategy (<24h vs <72h).

### TIMI Risk Score (UA/NSTEMI)
* **Criteria (1 pt each, max 7)**: Age ≥ 65, ≥ 3 CAD risk factors, Known CAD (stenosis ≥ 50%), ASA use in past 7 days, Severe angina (≥ 2 episodes in 24h), ECG ST changes ≥ 0.5mm, +ve Cardiac marker.

## 9. Disposition (Admit/Discharge) & Acute Complications

### Triage & Ward Disposition
* **STEMI**: Direct transfer to Cath Lab for Primary PCI → admit to CCU (心臟重症監護室).
* **High-Risk NSTEMI/UA**: Admit to CCU / High Dependency Unit (HDU).
* **Low-Risk Chest Pain (HEART ≤ 3)**: ED Observation Ward for repeat Troponin / rule out → discharge to rapid-access Chest Pain Clinic.

### Acute Mechanical & Electrical Complications
* **Arrhythmias**: VF (most common cause of pre-hospital death), VT, AF, heart blocks.
* **Cardiogenic Shock**: Large area of myocardial necrosis → pump failure.
* **Mechanical Ruptures (typically days 3-5)**:
	* **Papillary Muscle Rupture**: Severe mitral regurgitation, pulmonary edema.
	* **Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)**: Harsh holosystolic murmur, right heart failure.
	* **Free Wall Rupture**: → Cardiac tamponade → PEA arrest.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the defining ECG sign of Wellens' Syndrome? Answer: Deeply inverted or biphasic T waves in V2-V3 in a pain-free patient, indicating critical proximal LAD stenosis.
* Viva Question: What is the De Winter ECG pattern? Answer: Up-sloping ST depression leading into tall, symmetrical hyperacute T waves in precordial leads, signifying acute proximal LAD occlusion.
* Trap: Giving nitrates to a patient with an inferior STEMI. Pearl: Always obtain a right-sided ECG (V4R) first. RV infarction is preload-dependent; nitrates can cause profound, refractory hypotension.
* Pitfall: Assuming a normal initial troponin rules out ACS. Serial testing (0h and 1h/2h delta) is mandatory as early presentations may precede the biomarker leak.
* Trap: Giving Clopidogrel immediately in all suspected NSTEMI patients. Pearl: Hold P2Y12 inhibitors if the patient is a likely candidate for urgent CABG (e.g., severe multi-vessel disease), as it delays surgery due to bleeding risk.
* Pitfall: Overlooking a posterior MI because there is no ST elevation on the standard 12-lead. ST depression in V1-V3 with tall R waves is the reciprocal mirror image; immediately order V7-V9 to confirm ST elevation.
