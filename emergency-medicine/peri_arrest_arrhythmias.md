# Peri-arrest Arrhythmias (心搏驟停前心律失常)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Triage Category & Local Epidemiology

### Definition of Peri-arrest Arrhythmia
* **Peri-arrest Arrhythmia (心搏驟停前心律失常)**: Any abnormal cardiac rhythm resulting in extreme hemodynamic compromise.
* **Risk**: High probability of imminent deterioration into cardiac arrest (VF/pVT, Asystole, PEA).
* **Classification**: Broadly divided into **Tachyarrhythmias (心搏過速)** (>100 bpm, usually >150 bpm for symptoms) and **Bradyarrhythmias (心搏過緩)** (<60 bpm, usually <40 bpm for symptoms).

### HA Triage Category
* **Category 1 (Critical)**: If presenting with active cardiac arrest, profound shock, or unrecordable BP.
* **Category 2 (Emergency)**: Hemodynamically unstable but perfusing, requiring time-critical intervention (<15 mins).

### Local Epidemiology (Hong Kong)
* **Prevalence**: Very common ED presentation, highly correlated with an aging population.
* **Underlying Pathology**: Primarily driven by **Ischemic Heart Disease (缺血性心臟病)** and structural heart disease.

## 2. Acute Pathophysiology & Mechanism of Injury (MOI)

### Pathophysiology of Tachyarrhythmias
* **Re-entry Circuits (折返激動)**: Unidirectional block + slow conduction pathway (e.g., AVNRT, WPW).
* **Enhanced Automaticity (自律性增高)**: Ectopic foci firing faster than SA node (e.g., ischemia-induced).
* **Triggered Activity (觸發活動)**: Early or delayed afterdepolarizations (e.g., Torsades de Pointes).
* **Hemodynamic Consequence**: ↓ Diastolic filling time → ↓ Coronary perfusion + ↓ Cardiac Output (CO) → Ischemia + Shock.

### Pathophysiology of Bradyarrhythmias
* **SA Node Dysfunction (竇房結功能障礙)**: Degeneration (Sick Sinus Syndrome), ischemia (RCA territory).
* **AV Nodal Block (房室傳導阻滯)**: Delay or failure of conduction from atria to ventricles (Mobitz II, 3rd-degree block).
* **Escape Rhythms (逸搏心律)**: Junctional (40-60 bpm) or Ventricular (20-40 bpm) pacemakers take over when primary fails.
* **Hemodynamic Consequence**: CO = HR × SV. Extreme ↓ HR → CO plummets → Cerebral/Tissue hypoperfusion.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (ABCDE & Secondary Survey Findings ONLY)

### Adverse Features of Instability (The "Big 4")
* **Shock (休克)**: Pallor, sweating, cold extremities, prolonged CRT, hypotension (SBP < 90 mmHg).
* **Syncope (暈厥)**: Transient loss of consciousness, severely altered mental status, GCS drop.
* **Myocardial Ischemia (心肌缺血)**: Ischemic chest pain (angina).
* **Heart Failure (心力衰竭)**: Pulmonary edema, basal crepitations, raised JVP.

### Primary Survey (ABCDE) Findings
* **Airway**: Usually patent unless comatose.
* **Breathing**: Tachypnea (↑ RR), hypoxemia (↓ SpO2), frothy sputum (if LV failure).
* **Circulation**: Tachycardia/Bradycardia, weak/thready/irregular pulse, diaphoresis.
* **Disability**: Confusion, lethargy, agitation (due to cerebral hypoperfusion).

### Secondary Survey Findings
* **Symptoms**: Palpitations (心悸), dizziness, dyspnea.
* **Signs**: Cannon A-waves in JVP (Complete heart block or VT), murmurs (valvular structural disease).

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses (Rule-Out Life Threats)

### Tachyarrhythmia Classifications (The "Rule-Outs")
| Complex Type | Regularity | Key Differentials to Rule-Out |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Narrow Complex (<0.12s)** | Regular | Sinus Tachycardia, AVNRT, AVRT, Atrial Flutter |
| **Narrow Complex (<0.12s)** | Irregular | **Atrial Fibrillation (心房顫動)**, A-Flutter with variable block, MAT |
| **Broad Complex (>0.12s)** | Regular | **Ventricular Tachycardia (心室顫動) (VT)** (Assume VT until proven otherwise!), SVT with aberrancy |
| **Broad Complex (>0.12s)** | Irregular | AF with LBBB, AF with WPW, **Torsades de Pointes (尖端扭轉型室速)** |

### Bradyarrhythmia Etiologies (The 4 "I"s and 4 "H"s)
| Category | Specific Causes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ischemia** | **Inferior STEMI (下壁心肌梗塞)** (RCA supplies AV/SA nodes) |
| **Iatrogenic/Drugs** | Beta-blockers, CCBs, Digoxin toxicity, Amiodarone |
| **Ions (Electrolytes)** | Hyperkalemia (高鉀血症) |
| **Increased ICP** | Cushing's reflex (Bradycardia + Hypertension + Irregular RR) |
| **Hypothermia** | Core temp < 32°C |
| **Hypothyroidism** | Myxedema coma |
| **Hypoxia** | End-stage respiratory failure |
| **Heart Block** | Fibrotic degeneration (Lenègre-Lev disease) |

## 5. Emergency Investigations (Point-of-Care to Formal)

### Point-of-Care (POC) Testing
* **12-Lead ECG (心電圖)**: Identifies specific rhythm, rate, axis, ischemia (ST segments), QTc interval.
* **VBG/ABG (動脈/靜脈血氣)**: Rapid K+, Ca2+, pH, pO2, pCO2, lactate.
* **POCUS (Echocardiography)**: Assesses global LV/RV function, pericardial effusion (tamponade causing reflex tachycardia), regional wall motion abnormalities.
* **Bedside Glucose (血糖)**: Rule out hypoglycemia masking as sympathetic drive.

### Formal Laboratory Investigations
* **Cardiac Biomarkers**: High-sensitivity Troponin I/T (hs-Tn) to rule out primary MI.
* **Renal & Electrolytes (U&E)**: K+, Mg2+, Ca2+ (Critical for membrane stability).
* **Thyroid Function (TFTs)**: TSH/fT4 (Thyrotoxicosis triggering AF/SVT).
* **Toxicology Screen**: Digoxin level (if applicable).
* **CXR (胸部X光)**: Evaluates cardiomegaly, pulmonary edema.

## 6. Acute Resuscitation & Medical Management Algorithms

### Resuscitation Council Tachycardia Algorithm
* **If Adverse Features Present (Unstable)**:
  * Up to 3 attempts at Synchronized DC shock.
  * Follow with **Amiodarone (胺碘酮)** 300mg IV over 10-20 mins + repeat shock.
* **If No Adverse Features (Stable) + Broad Complex**:
  * Regular (Likely VT): Amiodarone 300mg IV over 20-60 mins, then 900mg over 24h.
  * Irregular: Magnesium 2g IV over 10 mins (for Torsades) or seek expert help.
* **If No Adverse Features (Stable) + Narrow Complex**:
  * Regular: Vagal maneuvers → **Adenosine (腺苷)** 6mg rapid IV push → 12mg → 12mg.
  * Irregular (Likely AF): Rate control with **Metoprolol (美托洛爾)** 2.5-5mg IV or **Diltiazem** IV. Consider anticoagulation.

### Resuscitation Council Bradycardia Algorithm
* **If Adverse Features Present (Unstable)**:
  * 1st Line: **Atropine (阿托品)** 500mcg IV, repeat to max 3mg.
  * 2nd Line: Transcutaneous Pacing (TCP) OR drug infusions.
  * Drug infusions: **Isoprenaline (異丙腎上腺素)** 5mcg/min OR **Adrenaline (腎上腺素)** 2-10mcg/min.
* **If No Adverse Features (Stable)**:
  * Assess risk of asystole (Recent asystole, Mobitz II, 3rd-degree block, broad ventricular pause).
  * High risk: Prepare for transvenous pacing; interim TCP or drugs.
  * Low risk: Observe.

### Special Antidotes & Tox-Specific Management
* **Hyperkalemia-induced Bradycardia**: **Calcium Gluconate 10% (葡萄糖酸鈣)** 10ml IV push (Cardioprotection).
* **Beta-Blocker/CCB Overdose**: **Glucagon (胰高血糖素)** 5-10mg IV bolus, High-dose Insulin Euglycemic Therapy (HIET).

## 7. Key ED Procedures & Technical Interventions (Mechanics & Consent)

### Synchronized DC Cardioversion Mechanics
* **Consent**: Emphasize risks of stroke, VF induction, skin burns, failure to convert.
* **Sedation**: Procedural sedation/anesthesia required (e.g., Propofol or Ketamine/Midazolam).
* **Preparation**: Apply defib pads in Antero-Apical or Antero-Posterior position.
* **Execution**: Press 'SYNC' button on defibrillator. Verify sync markers appear on R waves.
* **Energy Selection (Biphasic)**:
  * Broad Regular (VT): 120-150J.
  * Narrow Regular (SVT/A-Flutter): 70-120J.
  * Narrow Irregular (AF): 120-150J.
* **Action**: Charge, clear patient, press and HOLD shock button until energy delivers (sync delay).

### Transcutaneous Pacing (TCP) Mechanics
* **Consent**: Verbal emergency consent. Warn patient it is highly painful (muscle twitching).
* **Preparation**: Apply pads Antero-Posteriorly. Attach 3-lead ECG monitoring via the defib unit.
* **Execution**: Turn on Pacer mode.
* **Settings**: Set Demand Rate to 60-70 bpm. Start output at 10 mA.
* **Titration**: Dial up mA steadily until **Electrical Capture** (pacer spike followed by wide QRS/T wave) AND **Mechanical Capture** (palpable femoral pulse matching set rate). Usually captures around 50-100 mA.

### Modified Valsalva Maneuver Mechanics
* **Execution**: Patient semi-recumbent. Blow into a 10ml syringe to push plunger for 15 seconds (Generates 40mmHg pressure).
* **Modification**: Immediately lay patient flat and elevate legs to 45 degrees for 15 seconds.
* **Mechanism**: Maximizes venous return/vagal tone abruptly, breaking AV node re-entry.

## 9. Disposition (Admit/Discharge) & Acute Complications

### Disposition Criteria
* **Admit Resuscitation/ICU**: Unstable arrhythmias requiring DC cardioversion/pacing, initiation of vasopressors, or impending arrest.
* **Admit CCU/Medical Ward**: Mobitz II, 3rd-degree block (awaiting permanent pacemaker), symptomatic SVT/AF requiring rate control, new-onset ischemic arrhythmias.
* **Discharge**: Fully resolved first-episode SVT (post-adenosine/Valsalva) with normal post-reversion ECG, normal troponin, and outpatient cardiology follow-up arranged.

### Acute Complications
* **Cardiac Arrest**: Progression to VF, Asystole, or PEA.
* **Thromboembolic Stroke (中風)**: Embolization of atrial thrombus post-cardioversion of AF (>48 hours duration without anticoagulation).
* **Cardiogenic Shock**: Persistent poor CO → multi-organ failure.
* **Post-Cardioversion Arrhythmias**: Asystole or bradycardia immediately following DC shock.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: Why must you press the "SYNC" button during cardioversion? Answer: It ensures energy delivery occurs on the R wave, avoiding the relative refractory period (T wave). Shocking on the T wave can induce R-on-T phenomenon, instantly causing Ventricular Fibrillation (VF).
* Viva Question: What is the primary exception to giving Amiodarone for stable broad-complex tachycardia? Answer: Torsades de Pointes. Amiodarone prolongs the QT interval and worsens Torsades. Give IV Magnesium instead.
* Viva Question: How do you administer Adenosine properly? Answer: Use a large proximal vein (antecubital fossa), administer via rapid IV push, and immediately flush with 20ml Normal Saline while elevating the arm, due to its extremely short half-life (<10 seconds).
* Trap: Treating all Broad Complex Tachycardia (BCT) with SVT agents (e.g., Verapamil or Diltiazem) because you think it is "SVT with aberrancy". Pearl: Over 80% of BCT is Ventricular Tachycardia (VT). Giving AV node blockers to a VT patient causes catastrophic vasodilation and cardiac arrest. Assume VT and treat as VT until proven otherwise.
* Trap: Assessing "capture" during transcutaneous pacing by palpating the carotid pulse. Pearl: Pacing causes profound skeletal muscle twitching in the neck/chest, which mimics a carotid pulse. Always verify mechanical capture by palpating the femoral pulse.
* Pitfall: Giving Atropine in Mobitz Type II or 3rd-degree heart block with a wide QRS. Atropine works at the AV node. In infranodal blocks (wide QRS), Atropine is generally ineffective and can occasionally cause paradoxical slowing. Proceed directly to pacing or catecholamine infusions (Isoprenaline/Adrenaline).
