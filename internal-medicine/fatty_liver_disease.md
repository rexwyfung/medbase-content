# Fatty Liver Disease (脂肪肝)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Definition & Nomenclature of Fatty Liver Disease
* **MASLD (Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease)**: Replaces NAFLD. Requires hepatic steatosis (脂肪變性) + ≥1 cardiometabolic criteria.
* **MASH (Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis)**: Replaces NASH. Steatosis + inflammation + hepatocyte ballooning.
* **ARLD (Alcohol-Related Liver Disease/酒精性肝病)**: Steatosis secondary to excessive alcohol intake.
* **MetALD**: Overlap syndrome (MASLD criteria met + moderate alcohol consumption: 140-210g/wk for ♀, 210-280g/wk for ♂).

### Etiologies of Hepatic Steatosis
* **Primary (Metabolic)**: Insulin resistance, Metabolic Syndrome (代謝綜合症), Obesity (肥胖).
* **Toxic**: Alcohol excess.
* **Drug-Induced (DILI)**: Amiodarone, Corticosteroids, Methotrexate, Tamoxifen, Valproate.
* **Nutritional**: Starvation, Total Parenteral Nutrition (TPN).

### Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong
* **Prevalence**: MASLD affects ~25-30% of HK adults; increasing mirroring local obesity/T2DM epidemics.
* **Lean MASLD**: Important local phenotype; ~20% of HK MASLD patients are non-obese (BMI < 25 kg/m²) but have central adiposity/insulin resistance.
* **Leading Cause**: MASLD is rapidly overtaking viral hepatitis as the leading cause of HCC (肝癌) and liver transplantation in HK.

## 2. Systemic Pathophysiology & Molecular Mechanisms

### Pathophysiology of MASLD & MASH
* **First Hit (Steatosis)**: Insulin resistance (胰島素阻抗) → ↑ peripheral lipolysis → ↑ Free Fatty Acid (FFA) influx into liver + ↑ de novo lipogenesis (via SREBP-1c) → Triglyceride accumulation.
* **Second Hit (Inflammation/MASH)**: Lipotoxicity + Mitochondrial dysfunction + Oxidative stress (ROS) + Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER) stress → Hepatocyte apoptosis & ballooning.
* **Fibrogenesis**: Kupffer cell activation releases TGF-β → activates Hepatic Stellate Cells (HSC) → ↑ collagen deposition (Fibrosis/纖維化) → Cirrhosis.

### Pathophysiology of Alcohol-Related Liver Disease (ARLD)
* **Metabolic Pathway**: EtOH metabolized by Alcohol Dehydrogenase (ADH) & CYP2E1 → Acetaldehyde (highly toxic/reactive).
* **Redox Imbalance**: NAD+ depleted (↑ NADH/NAD+ ratio) → inhibits fatty acid oxidation & promotes lipogenesis → Steatosis.
* **Inflammatory Cascade**: Gut permeability ↑ → endotoxemia (LPS) → TLR4 activation on Kupffer cells → TNF-α/IL-8 release → Alcoholic Hepatitis.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Detailed History & System-Specific Physical Signs ONLY)

### Historical Clues for Fatty Liver Disease
* **Alcohol Quantitation**: Calculate weekly units (1 unit = 10g EtOH). Screen for dependence (CAGE questionnaire).
* **Metabolic Risk**: Ask re weight changes, OSA symptoms (snoring, daytime somnolence), polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) history, dietary habits (fructose/sugary drinks).
* **Symptoms**: Usually asymptomatic. May have non-specific RUQ ache (Glisson's capsule stretching) or fatigue.

### System-Specific Physical Signs
* **General**: Central obesity (measured via waist circumference: ≥90cm ♂, ≥80cm ♀ in Asians).
* **Dermatological**: Acanthosis nigricans (hyperpigmentation at neck/axillae indicating insulin resistance), skin tags.
* **Abdominal**: Hepatomegaly (smooth, firm, non-tender edge).
* **ARLD specific**: Parotid enlargement, Dupuytren's contracture, malnutrition/muscle wasting.
* **Late Signs (Avoid if excluding decompensation)**: Spider naevi, palmar erythema (often absent in early/compensated stages).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses & Discriminatory Features

### Discriminatory Features of Fatty Liver Etiologies
| Condition | Key Discriminatory Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MASLD** | Strict cardiometabolic criteria; AST:ALT ratio < 1; no significant EtOH. |
| **ARLD** | Significant EtOH Hx; AST:ALT ratio > 2; ↑ MCV; ↑ GGT. |
| **Drug-Induced** | Temporal relation to amiodarone, tamoxifen, steroids. |

### Differentiating Steatosis from Other Liver Diseases
| Differential | Discriminatory Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wilson's Disease** | Young patient, neuropsychiatric symptoms, Kayser-Fleischer rings. |
| **Autoimmune Hepatitis** | Female predominance, +ve ANA/SMA, ↑ IgG. |
| **Hemochromatosis** | Bronze diabetes, arthropathy, severely ↑ Ferritin & Transferrin Saturation. |

## 5. Tiered Investigations (Bedside, Bloods, Imaging, Biopsy & Diagnostic Criteria)

### Bedside & Basic Blood Investigations
* **Bedside**: Waist circumference measurement, BP monitoring, ECG (for silent IHD).
* **Liver Function Tests**: 
  * MASLD: ALT > AST (mild ↑ 2-3x ULN).
  * ARLD: AST > ALT (ratio >2:1). GGT markedly ↑.
* **Metabolic Panel**: Fasting glucose, HbA1c, Fasting lipid profile (↑ TG, ↓ HDL).
* **Other Bloods**: FBC (↑ MCV in ARLD, ↓ Plt in early portal HTN), Ferritin (often acutely ↑ in MASH/ARLD).

### Imaging Modalities
* **US Liver (肝臟超聲波)**: First-line. Shows hyperechoic "bright" liver compared to renal cortex. Poor sensitivity if steatosis < 20%.
* **Transient Elastography (FibroScan/肝臟纖維化掃描)**: 
  * **CAP (Controlled Attenuation Parameter)**: Quantifies steatosis.
  * **LSM (Liver Stiffness Measurement)**: Quantifies fibrosis (measured in kPa).

### Liver Biopsy & Histological Criteria
* **Role**: Gold standard for Dx, but invasive. Used when non-invasive tests are indeterminate or overlapping etiologies suspected.
* **MASH Histology**: 
  * Macrovesicular steatosis.
  * Hepatocyte ballooning (cytoskeletal injury).
  * Lobular inflammation.
  * Perisinusoidal "chicken-wire" fibrosis.
* **Alcoholic Hepatitis Histology**: Mallory-Denk bodies (eosinophilic inclusions), neutrophil infiltration, megamitochondria.

### MASLD Diagnostic Criteria
* Hepatic steatosis (imaging/biopsy) AND ≥1 of the following:
  * BMI ≥ 23 kg/m² (Asian) or waist circumference ≥ 90cm (♂) / 80cm (♀).
  * Fasting glucose ≥ 5.6 mmol/L, or Type 2 DM.
  * BP ≥ 130/85 mmHg or on anti-hypertensives.
  * Plasma TG ≥ 1.70 mmol/L or lipid-lowering Rx.
  * Plasma HDL-C ≤ 1.0 mmol/L (♂) or ≤ 1.3 mmol/L (♀).

## 6. Acute Exacerbation Management & Resuscitation (Flares/Emergencies)

### Acute Alcoholic Hepatitis (AAH) Resuscitation
* **Sepsis Screen**: Blood, urine, ascitic fluid (rule out SBP), CXR. High mortality from concurrent infections.
* **Nutrition**: Enteral feeding (NG tube if needed) targeting 35-40 kcal/kg/day & 1.2-1.5g protein/kg/day.
* **Vitamin Replacement**: IV Pabrinex (Thiamine) BEFORE glucose to prevent Wernicke's encephalopathy (韋尼克腦病).

### Pharmacological Therapy for Severe AAH
* **Indication**: Maddrey's Discriminant Function (mDF) ≥ 32.
* **First-line Rx**: Prednisolone 40mg PO daily for 28 days.
* **Review Rule**: Calculate Lille Score at Day 7. 
  * If Lille > 0.45: Steroid non-responder → STOP steroids.
  * If Lille < 0.45: Steroid responder → COMPLETE 28-day course, then taper.
* **Alternative**: Pentoxifylline 400mg TDS (historical/less favoured now, used if sepsis/steroids contraindicated to ↓ hepatorenal syndrome risk).

## 7. Chronic Management Algorithm & Disease-Modifying Therapies (Pharmacology & Lifestyle)

### Lifestyle Modifications for MASLD/MASH
* **Weight Loss Target**: 
  * > 5% total body weight loss to ↓ steatosis.
  * > 7-10% to resolve MASH inflammation and regress early fibrosis.
* **Diet**: Mediterranean diet (low saturated fats, high monounsaturated fats/omega-3, avoid fructose/sugary drinks).
* **Exercise**: 150-200 mins/week moderate-intensity aerobic + resistance training.
* **Alcohol**: Complete abstinence is optimal.

### Pharmacological Algorithm for MASLD/MASH
* **Diabetic Patients (T2DM + MASLD)**:
  * **1st Line**: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (e.g., Semaglutide/索馬魯肽) → significant weight loss & MASH resolution.
  * **Alternative/Add-on**: Pioglitazone (PPAR-γ agonist) → improves insulin sensitivity and histology (beware weight gain/heart failure).
  * SGLT2 inhibitors (e.g., Empagliflozin) → good for CV risk, mild benefit on liver fat.
* **Non-Diabetic Patients with biopsy-proven MASH**:
  * **Vitamin E (α-tocopherol)**: 800 IU/day. Antioxidant. (Contraindicated if personal/FHx of prostate cancer).
* **Bariatric Surgery**: For BMI ≥ 35 (or ≥ 30 in Asians with poorly controlled MetS).

### Cardiovascular Risk Reduction in MASLD
* **Statins (他汀類藥物)**: Safe and MUST be prescribed for CV risk reduction in compensated MASLD/cirrhosis. Does NOT worsen liver disease. (Stop only if decompensated).
* **Anti-hypertensives**: ACEi/ARB preferred for concurrent reno-protection.

### Chronic Management of ARLD
* **Abstinence**: Referral to addiction services, psychiatric support.
* **Anti-craving Agents**: Baclofen (safe in liver disease, hepatically cleared minimally), Acamprosate.
* **Long-term Nutritional Support**: Lifelong PO Thiamine, Folate, and Vitamin B complex.

## 8. Risk Stratification, Clinical Scoring Systems & Prognosis

### Non-Invasive Fibrosis Scoring (MASLD)
* **FIB-4 Index**: First-line triage tool in primary care/HA clinics.
  * Variables: Age, AST, ALT, Platelets.
  * < 1.30: Low risk of advanced fibrosis (Reassure & retest in 2-3 yrs).
  * 1.30 - 2.67: Indeterminate (Refer for FibroScan).
  * \> 2.67: High risk of advanced fibrosis (Refer Hepatology + HCC screening).
* **NAFLD Fibrosis Score (NFS)**: Uses Age, BMI, IFG/DM, AST/ALT ratio, Platelets, Albumin.

### Acute Alcoholic Hepatitis Severity Scoring
* **Maddrey's Discriminant Function (mDF)**: 
  * Formula: 4.6 x (PT patient - PT control) + Serum Bilirubin (mg/dL).
  * ≥ 32: Severe disease (indicates need for steroids, 1-month mortality ~30-50%).
* **Lille Score**: Evaluates Day 7 response to corticosteroids based on Age, Albumin, Bilirubin (Day 0 & Day 7), Creatinine, PT.

### Prognosis & Mortality
* **MASLD Leading Cause of Death**: Cardiovascular disease (CVD), NOT liver failure.
* **Fibrosis Stage**: The single most important histologic predictor of liver-related mortality in MASLD.

## 9. Multi-System Complications (End-Organ Damage & Drug Adverse Effects)

### Hepatic Complications
* **Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC)**: Can develop in MASLD *without* progressing to cirrhosis (unlike most other liver diseases).
* **Cirrhosis**: Portal hypertension, ascites, varices, hepatic encephalopathy.

### Extra-Hepatic Complications of MASLD
* **Cardiovascular**: CAD, Stroke, structural heart disease (diastolic dysfunction, AF).
* **Renal**: Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) independent of DM/HTN.
* **Endocrine/Metabolic**: Worsening T2DM, Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA).
* **Oncological**: ↑ incidence of Colorectal cancer (screen via colonoscopy), Breast cancer.

### Medication Adverse Effects
* **Vitamin E**: ↑ risk of hemorrhagic stroke, ↑ risk of prostate cancer in men.
* **Pioglitazone**: Weight gain, fluid retention, worsening heart failure, ↑ risk of osteoporotic fractures.
* **GLP-1 Agonists**: Nausea, vomiting, delayed gastric emptying, rare risk of pancreatitis.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: Why do we use the FIB-4 score before ordering a FibroScan? Answer: To triage the massive MASLD population. FIB-4 has a high negative predictive value to rule out advanced fibrosis, saving expensive and limited FibroScan resources for indeterminate/high-risk patients.
* Trap: Believing that statins are contraindicated in patients with elevated LFTs from fatty liver. Pearl: Statins are highly beneficial and safe in MASLD, even with transaminases up to 3x ULN. CVD is their leading cause of death. Only withhold in decompensated cirrhosis or if LFTs >3x ULN after initiation.
* Pitfall: Giving IV glucose to a malnourished alcoholic patient before administering thiamine. This precipitates Wernicke's encephalopathy by depleting the remaining thiamine stores required for glucose metabolism.
* Viva Question: Can MASLD patients develop HCC without having cirrhosis? Answer: Yes, ~20-30% of MASLD-related HCC occurs in non-cirrhotic livers due to direct metabolic and inflammatory oncogenesis.
* Trap: Using the Lille Score to decide if steroids should be started in alcoholic hepatitis. Pearl: Lille score is ONLY used on Day 7 of steroid therapy to assess response. Maddrey's Discriminant Function (mDF) is used on Day 0 to initiate steroids.
