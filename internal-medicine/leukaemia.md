# Leukaemia & Myeloid Neoplasms (白血病及骨髓增生性腫瘤)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Definitions of Leukaemia & Myeloid Neoplasms
* **Leukaemia (白血病):** Malignant clonal proliferation of hematopoietic stem cells in the bone marrow (BM), spilling into peripheral blood.
  * **Acute:** Block in differentiation → rapid accumulation of immature blasts.
  * **Chronic:** Uncontrolled proliferation of mature/maturing cells.
* **Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (MPNs) (骨髓增生性腫瘤):** Clonal stem cell disorders → overproduction of mature myeloid cells (erythrocytes, leukocytes, platelets) independently of normal cytokine regulation.
* **Myelodysplastic Syndromes (MDS) (骨髓發育不良症候群):** Clonal stem cell disorders → ineffective hematopoiesis, morphological dysplasia, cytopenias, and high risk of Acute Myeloid Leukaemia (AML) transformation.

### Etiologies and Risk Factors for Haematological Malignancies
* **Radiation:** Prior radiotherapy, nuclear accidents (strongly linked to AML, CML).
* **Chemicals:** Benzene exposure, prior chemotherapy (alkylating agents → loss of chr 5/7; topoisomerase II inhibitors → 11q23 MLL abnormalities).
* **Genetic Syndromes:** Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21) → markedly ↑ risk of ALL and acute megakaryoblastic leukaemia (AML M7).
* **Pre-existing BM Disorders:** MPN, MDS, Aplastic Anaemia (AA) → transformation to AML.

### Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong
* **Overall Leukaemia:** Top 10 incidence and cancer deaths in HK Hospital Authority (HA) registry.
* **AML:** Most common acute leukaemia in adults (median age ~65).
* **ALL:** Most common malignancy in childhood (peak 2-5 years).
* **CML/CLL:** Less common in Asian populations compared to Caucasians; CLL is relatively rare in HK but increasing.

## 2. Systemic Pathophysiology & Molecular Mechanisms

### Pathophysiology of Acute Myeloid Leukaemia (AML) & ALL
* **Two-Hit Hypothesis:**
  * **Hit 1 (Proliferation):** Mutations in tyrosine kinases (e.g., FLT3, c-KIT) → ligand-independent cell survival and division.
  * **Hit 2 (Differentiation block):** Mutations in transcription factors (e.g., RUNX1, CEBPA) → arrest of maturation at blast stage.
* **Result:** Blasts pack BM space → marrow failure (anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia) and tissue infiltration.

### Molecular Mechanism of Acute Promyelocytic Leukaemia (APL)
* **Translocation:** t(15;17)(q24;q21) → fuses Promyelocytic Leukaemia (PML) gene with Retinoic Acid Receptor Alpha (RARA) gene.
* **Mechanism:** PML-RARA fusion protein binds transcriptional corepressors with high affinity → blocks differentiation at promyelocyte stage.
* **Systemic Effect:** Promyelocytes contain primary granules loaded with procoagulants (tissue factor) → release triggers devastating Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC) (瀰漫性血管內凝血).

### Pathophysiology of Chronic Myeloid Leukaemia (CML)
* **Philadelphia Chromosome (Ph):** t(9;22)(q34;q11) → fusion of BCR (chr 22) and ABL1 (chr 9).
* **Mechanism:** BCR-ABL1 encodes a 210-kDa mutant protein with constitutive, unregulated tyrosine kinase activity → uncontrolled proliferation of myeloid cells (especially neutrophils/granulocytes) with preserved differentiation.

### Pathophysiology of Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL)
* **Mechanism:** Accumulation of functionally incompetent, mature-appearing monoclonal B lymphocytes (CD5+, CD23+).
* **Defect:** Primarily a disease of defective apoptosis (frequently involving BCL-2 overexpression or p53 deletion), rather than rapid proliferation.

### Molecular Mechanisms of Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (MPNs)
* **Polycythaemia Vera (PV):** >95% have JAK2 V617F mutation.
* **Essential Thrombocythaemia (ET) & Primary Myelofibrosis (PMF):** JAK2 V617F (50-60%), CALR (20-30%), MPL (5-10%).
* **Mechanism:** Mutations in JAK2/STAT pathway → hypersensitivity of hematopoietic progenitors to cytokines (EPO, TPO) → cytokine-independent overproduction.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Detailed History & System-Specific Physical Signs ONLY)

### History of Presenting Illness in Leukaemias & Myeloid Neoplasms
* **BM Failure Symptoms:** Lethargy/dyspnea (anaemia), recurrent/severe mucosal or skin infections (neutropenia), mucosal bleeding/epistaxis/menorrhagia (thrombocytopenia).
* **B Symptoms:** Unexplained fever, drenching night sweats, weight loss >10% over 6 months.
* **Hyperviscosity Symptoms (High WCC/Plt/RBC):** Headache, blurred vision, tinnitus, altered mental status, transient ischemic attacks.
* **Organ Infiltration:** Early satiety, left upper quadrant dragging pain (splenomegaly), bone pain (marrow expansion, esp. ALL).
* **MPN-Specific History:** Aquagenic pruritus (itching after hot shower → PV), erythromelalgia (burning pain in hands/feet → ET, PV).

### Physical Examination Findings in Acute Leukaemias (AML & ALL)
* **General:** Pallor, pyrexia, petechiae, ecchymoses.
* **Cutaneous:** Leukemia cutis (violaceous, raised, non-tender skin infiltrates).
* **Oral:** Gingival hypertrophy (牙齦肥厚) (hallmark of monocytic AML M4/M5).
* **ALL Specific:** Painless lymphadenopathy (cervical, axillary), marked hepatosplenomegaly, testicular enlargement (sanctuary site), cranial nerve palsies (CNS infiltration).

### Physical Examination Findings in Chronic Leukaemias (CML & CLL)
* **CML:** Massive splenomegaly (巨脾) (often crossing midline to right iliac fossa), hepatomegaly, sternal tenderness.
* **CLL:** Symmetrical, painless, rubbery lymphadenopathy (淋巴結腫大), mild-to-moderate hepatosplenomegaly.

### Physical Examination Findings in MPNs (PV, ET, PMF)
* **PV:** Plethora (ruddy cyanosis of face/hands), conjunctival suffusion, engorged retinal veins, excoriations (from pruritus).
* **ET:** Digital ischemia, livedo reticularis.
* **PMF:** Massive splenomegaly, cachexia.
* **General MPN:** Gouty tophi (from ↑ urate turnover).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses & Discriminatory Features

### Discriminatory Features of Marked Leukocytosis
| Diagnosis | Discriminatory Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CML** | Splenomegaly +ve; Basophilia/Eosinophilia +ve; Leukocyte Alkaline Phosphatase (LAP) score ↓; BCR-ABL +ve. |
| **Leukaemoid Reaction** | Severe infection/inflammation Hx; Toxic granulation/Döhle bodies +ve; LAP score ↑; BCR-ABL -ve. |
| **AML/ALL** | Rapid onset; BM failure signs; Circulating blasts; Splenomegaly mild (except ALL). |

### Discriminatory Features of Pancytopenia
| Diagnosis | Discriminatory Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MDS** | Elderly; Dysplastic changes on smear; Hypercellular marrow; Cytogenetics (e.g., del 5q). |
| **Aplastic Anaemia** | Empty/hypocellular marrow; No blasts; No dysplasia; Immune/drug/viral etiology. |
| **Aleukaemic Leukaemia** | Severe pancytopenia but BM packed with >20% blasts; blasts absent in peripheral blood. |

### Discriminatory Features of Erythrocytosis (High Hb/Hct)
* **PV (Primary Polycythaemia):** EPO level ↓; JAK2 +ve; Splenomegaly +ve.
* **Secondary Polycythaemia:** EPO level ↑; Hypoxia (COPD, cyanotic congenital heart disease, high altitude, smoking) OR EPO-secreting tumors (Renal Cell Carcinoma, Hepatocellular Carcinoma).

## 5. Tiered Investigations (Bedside, Bloods, Imaging, Biopsy & Diagnostic Criteria)

### Bedside & Peripheral Blood Investigations
* **Complete Blood Count (CBC):** Cytopenias (anemia, thrombocytopenia) + variable WCC (can be ↓, normal, or profoundly ↑).
* **Peripheral Blood Smear (PBS):**
  * **AML:** Myeloblasts with nucleoli, **Auer rods** (pink rod-like cytoplasmic inclusions, pathognomonic for myeloid lineage).
  * **APL:** Faggot cells (promyelocytes packed with bundles of Auer rods).
  * **CML:** Full spectrum of granulocytes (myeloblasts → promyelocytes → myelocytes → neutrophils), ↑ basophils/eosinophils.
  * **CLL:** Small mature lymphocytes, **Smudge cells** (fragile lymphocytes crushed during slide preparation).
  * **PMF:** **Teardrop cells** (dacrocytes), leukoerythroblastic film (nucleated RBCs, immature WBCs).
  * **MDS:** Hyposegmented neutrophils (Pelger-Huët anomaly).
* **Coagulation Profile (APL):** PT/APTT ↑, Fibrinogen profoundly ↓, D-dimer ↑ (DIC pattern).
* **Biochemistry:** LDH ↑, Urate ↑ (high cell turnover).

### Bone Marrow Aspirate and Trephine (BMAT)
* **Morphology:** Cellularity, blast percentage, presence of dysplasia, fibrosis (reticulin stain for PMF).
* **Flow Cytometry (Immunophenotyping):**
  * AML: CD13+, CD33+, CD34+, CD117+, MPO+.
  * ALL (B-cell): TdT+, CD10+, CD19+, CD22+.
  * ALL (T-cell): TdT+, CD3+, CD7+.
  * CLL: CD5+, CD19+, CD20+, CD23+, FMC7-, slg weak.
* **Cytogenetics & Molecular (Karyotype, FISH, PCR):** Detects t(15;17), t(9;22), FLT3-ITD, NPM1, CEBPA, JAK2, BCR-ABL quantification.

### WHO Diagnostic Criteria for Haematological Neoplasms
| Disease | Key Diagnostic Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AML** | **≥20% myeloid blasts** in BM or peripheral blood. (Exceptions: t(15;17), t(8;21), inv(16) are diagnostic of AML regardless of blast %). |
| **ALL** | **≥20% lymphoid blasts** in BM or peripheral blood. |
| **CML** | Persistent leukocytosis + **Ph chromosome / BCR-ABL1 +ve**. |
| **PV** | Major: 1. Hb >16.5 (M)/>16.0 (F) or Hct >49% (M)/>48% (F). 2. BM showing panmyelosis. 3. JAK2 V617F +ve. Minor: Subnormal serum EPO. |

## 6. Acute Exacerbation Management & Resuscitation (Flares/Emergencies)

### Acute Management of Leucostasis (Hyperleukocytosis)
* **Definition:** WCC > 100 x 10^9/L (usually AML/CML blast crisis) → blasts sludge in microcirculation (CNS, pulmonary).
* **Resuscitation:** Avoid blood transfusions initially (↑ viscosity).
* **Cytoreduction STAT:**
  * **Leukapheresis:** Immediate physical removal of WCC (bridging therapy).
  * **Hydroxyurea:** Rapid pharmacological WCC reduction.
  * Intensive IV hydration.

### Acute Management of APL-associated DIC
* **Rule:** If APL is suspected morphologically or clinically, **DO NOT WAIT** for molecular confirmation.
* **Targeted Agent:** **All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA) STAT** PO → forces differentiation of promyelocytes, immediately halting procoagulant release.
* **Coagulopathy Support:** Aggressive transfusion of FFP, Cryoprecipitate, and Platelets to maintain:
  * Fibrinogen > 1.5 g/L.
  * Platelets > 50 x 10^9/L.
  * PT/APTT near normal.
* **Avoid:** Invasive procedures (CVP lines, LP) until coagulopathy resolves.

## 7. Chronic Management Algorithm & Disease-Modifying Therapies (Pharmacology & Lifestyle)

### AML Treatment Algorithm
* **Induction:** "7+3 Regimen" (Cytarabine continuous IV x 7 days + Daunorubicin IV push x 3 days). Goal: Morphological complete remission (CR) (<5% blasts).
* **Consolidation (Post-Remission):** To eradicate minimal residual disease (MRD).
  * Favorable risk: High-dose Cytarabine (HiDAC) x 3-4 cycles.
  * Intermediate/Poor risk: Allogeneic Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplant (HSCT).
* **Targeted Add-ons:** Midostaurin (if FLT3+), Gemtuzumab ozogamicin (CD33+).

### APL Treatment Algorithm
* **Low/Intermediate Risk (WCC ≤ 10):** Chemo-free regimen → ATRA + Arsenic Trioxide (ATO) until remission, then consolidation with ATRA+ATO.
* **High Risk (WCC > 10):** ATRA + ATO + Anthracycline (Idarubicin).

### ALL Treatment Algorithm
* **Phases:** Induction → Consolidation → Maintenance (2-3 years of daily oral 6-MP and weekly oral MTX).
* **Regimen Example:** Hyper-CVAD (Cyclophosphamide, Vincristine, Doxorubicin, Dexamethasone alternating with high-dose MTX/Cytarabine).
* **CNS Prophylaxis (Mandatory):** Intrathecal Methotrexate/Cytarabine (ALL has high CNS relapse rate).
* **Ph+ ALL:** Add Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor (Imatinib/Dasatinib).

### CML Treatment Algorithm
* **1st Line:** Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKIs).
  * Imatinib (1st gen) OR Dasatinib/Nilotinib (2nd gen, faster/deeper response).
* **Monitoring:** Serial quantitative PCR for BCR-ABL transcripts aiming for Major Molecular Response (MMR) and Deep Molecular Response (DMR).
* **Resistance/T315I Mutation:** Switch to Ponatinib (3rd gen TKI).
* **Progression (Blast Crisis):** Treat as acute leukaemia (induction chemo) + TKI → Allogeneic HSCT.

### CLL Indications for Treatment (Watch & Wait unless criteria met)
* **Indications for Rx (IWCLL Criteria):**
  * Progressive marrow failure (worsening anemia/thrombocytopenia).
  * Massive/symptomatic splenomegaly (>6cm below margin).
  * Massive/symptomatic lymphadenopathy (>10cm).
  * Refractory B symptoms.
  * Autoimmune complications (AIHA, ITP) refractory to steroids.
* **Pharmacological Management (HK HA Formularies):**
  * **Fit pts:** FCR regimen (Fludarabine, Cyclophosphamide, Rituximab).
  * **Unfit / TP53 mutation / del(17p):** Bruton’s Tyrosine Kinase (BTK) inhibitors (Ibrutinib) OR BCL-2 inhibitors (Venetoclax).

### MPN Management Algorithm
* **Polycythaemia Vera (PV):**
  * All patients: Venesection (Phlebotomy) to target Hct < 0.45 + Low-dose Aspirin.
  * High risk (Age > 60 or prior thrombosis): Add Hydroxyurea (cytoreduction).
* **Essential Thrombocythaemia (ET):**
  * Low risk: Low-dose Aspirin.
  * High risk: Hydroxyurea + Aspirin (Anagrelide as 2nd line).
* **Primary Myelofibrosis (PMF):**
  * Symptomatic splenomegaly/B-symptoms: Ruxolitinib (JAK1/2 inhibitor).
  * Curative intent (young/fit): Allogeneic HSCT.

### MDS Management Algorithm
* **Low-Risk MDS:** Supportive care. Blood transfusions, Erythropoietin (EPO), Iron chelation (if high ferritin).
* **del(5q) MDS:** Lenalidomide (immunomodulator, reduces transfusion needs).
* **High-Risk MDS:** Hypomethylating agents (Azacitidine, Decitabine) → bridge to Allogeneic HSCT.

## 8. Risk Stratification, Clinical Scoring Systems & Prognosis

### AML Cytogenetic & Molecular Risk Stratification (ELN Criteria)
| Risk Group | Cytogenetics / Molecular Markers |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Favourable** | t(15;17) (APL); t(8;21); inv(16); mutated NPM1 (without FLT3-ITD); biallelic mutated CEBPA. |
| **Intermediate** | Normal karyotype (wild-type NPM1, wild-type FLT3); t(9;11). |
| **Adverse** | Complex karyotype (≥3 abnormalities); del(5q); del(7q); t(6;9); inv(3); FLT3-ITD (high allelic ratio); mutated TP53. |

### CML Phases of Disease
| Phase | Criteria (WHO) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Chronic Phase (CP)** | <10% blasts in blood/BM. Asymptomatic or mild symptoms. |
| **Accelerated Phase (AP)** | 10-19% blasts; persistent thrombocytopenia/thrombocytosis; increasing spleen size; clonal evolution (new genetic defects). |
| **Blast Crisis (BP)** | ≥20% blasts (myeloid or lymphoid) or extramedullary blast proliferation. Behaves like acute leukaemia. |

### CLL Clinical Staging Systems (Binet & Rai)
* **Binet Staging (Europe/HK context):**
  * **Stage A:** <3 lymphoid areas enlarged. (Prognosis: >10 years).
  * **Stage B:** ≥3 lymphoid areas enlarged. (Prognosis: 5-7 years).
  * **Stage C:** Anaemia (Hb <10) OR Thrombocytopenia (Plt <100). (Prognosis: 2-3 years).
* **Rai Staging (US):** 0 (Lymphocytosis) → I (Nodes) → II (Hepatosplenomegaly) → III (Anaemia) → IV (Thrombocytopenia).

### MDS Risk Scoring: IPSS-R (Revised International Prognostic Scoring System)
* **Variables Scored:**
  1. Cytogenetics (Very Good to Very Poor).
  2. Bone Marrow Blast percentage (0 to >10%).
  3. Depth of Cytopenias (Hb, Platelets, ANC).
* **Outcome:** Stratifies into 5 groups (Very Low to Very High) dictating likelihood of AML evolution and overall survival.

## 9. Multi-System Complications (End-Organ Damage & Drug Adverse Effects)

### Disease-Related Complications
* **Transformation:** MDS and MPNs (especially PMF) hold a high risk of transforming into secondary, highly chemo-resistant AML.
* **Autoimmunity in CLL:** Warm Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia (AIHA) and Immune Thrombocytopenia (ITP) due to immune dysregulation.
* **Hypogammaglobulinemia in CLL:** Increased susceptibility to encapsulated bacteria and recurrent sinopulmonary infections.

### Chemotherapy & TKI Adverse Effects (Pharmacotoxcity)
* **Anthracyclines (Daunorubicin, Idarubicin):** Dose-dependent, irreversible dilated cardiomyopathy.
* **Cytarabine:** Cerebellar toxicity (ataxia, nystagmus, dysarthria) at high doses; conjunctivitis (requires steroid eye drops prophylaxis).
* **Imatinib (1st gen TKI):** Periorbital edema, fluid retention, muscle cramps, GI upset.
* **Dasatinib (2nd gen TKI):** Pleural effusions, pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
* **Nilotinib (2nd gen TKI):** QTc prolongation, accelerated peripheral arterial disease (PAD), pancreatitis.
* **ATRA:** Differentiation syndrome (fever, dyspnea, pulmonary infiltrates, weight gain) → treat immediately with IV Dexamethasone.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you differentiate CML from a leukaemoid reaction secondary to severe infection? Answer: In CML, the Leukocyte Alkaline Phosphatase (LAP) score is low, basophilia is present, and molecular testing for BCR-ABL is positive. In a leukaemoid reaction, LAP score is high.
* Trap: Prescribing standard induction chemotherapy for a patient newly diagnosed with APL. Pearl: APL requires immediate administration of ATRA. Standard induction (7+3) without ATRA will exacerbate DIC and cause fatal haemorrhage.
* Pitfall: Missing Differentiation Syndrome in an APL patient on ATRA who develops dyspnea and fever. Do not assume it is just pneumonia. Promptly administer high-dose Dexamethasone without waiting for radiological confirmation.
* Viva Question: What are the three classic symptoms of symptomatic polycythaemia vera? Answer: Aquagenic pruritus (itching after hot showers), erythromelalgia (burning pain in extremities), and gout.
* Trap: Treating all patients with asymptomatic CLL just because their white count is high. Pearl: CLL is treated only when there are clear IWCLL indications (progressive cytopenias, massive organomegaly, severe B-symptoms). Early treatment does not improve overall survival.
* Pitfall: Forgetting CNS prophylaxis in ALL. ALL has a high propensity to sanctuary sites. All ALL patients must receive intrathecal methotrexate/cytarabine, regardless of baseline CSF status.
* Viva Question: What is the pathognomonic morphological finding for AML under the microscope? Answer: Auer rods (pink needle-like cytoplasmic inclusions) inside blasts.
* Trap: Using phlebotomy alone for high-risk PV (e.g., patient >60 years old). Pearl: High-risk PV patients require cytoreductive therapy (like Hydroxyurea) in addition to phlebotomy to adequately reduce thrombotic risk.
