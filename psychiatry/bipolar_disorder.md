# Bipolar Disorder (躁鬱症)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Bipolar Disorder Clinical Definition
* **Bipolar Disorder (躁鬱症)**: Chronic severe affective disorder characterized by biphasic mood episodes (mania/hypomania & depression) → significant functional impairment
* **Bipolar I (一型)**: ≥1 Manic Episode (躁狂發作) ± Depressive episodes
* **Bipolar II (二型)**: ≥1 Hypomanic Episode (輕躁狂發作) + ≥1 Major Depressive Episode (重度抑鬱發作)
* **Rapid Cycling (快速循環型)**: ≥4 mood episodes/12 months

### Bipolar Disorder Biopsychosocial Etiologies
* **Biological**: High heritability (80% monozygotic concordance) → polygenic risk (e.g., CACNA1C gene)
* **Psychological**: Maladaptive cognitive schemas, childhood trauma (童年創傷) → ↑ vulnerability
* **Social**: High Expressed Emotion (高表達情緒) in families, circadian rhythm disruptions (e.g., shift work), recent stressful life events (生活壓力事件)

### Bipolar Disorder Hong Kong Epidemiology
* **Prevalence**: ~1% lifetime prevalence in HK
* **Onset**: Peak onset age 15–25 years
* **Gender**: Bipolar I (M = F); Bipolar II (F > M)
* **Morbidity**: High risk of comorbid substance use disorder (物質使用障礙) & suicide (自殺) (10-15% lifetime risk)

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Bipolar Disorder Neurobiology and Genetics
* **Structural MRI**: ↓ Prefrontal cortex volume, enlarged lateral ventricles, ↑ amygdala reactivity
* **HPA Axis**: Chronic hyperactivation → ↑ Cortisol (皮質醇) during mood episodes
* **Circadian Rhythm**: Dysregulation of suprachiasmatic nucleus → sleep-wake cycle disruption

### Bipolar Disorder Neurotransmitter Pathways
* **Mania**: ↑ Dopamine (多巴胺) (mesolimbic pathway) → hyperlocomotion & grandiosity, ↑ Glutamate (穀氨酸), ↓ GABA (氨基丁酸)
* **Depression**: ↓ Serotonin (血清素) (5-HT), ↓ Norepinephrine (去甲腎上腺素) (NE), ↓ Dopamine

### Bipolar Disorder Psychopathology Mechanisms
* **Kindling Hypothesis (點燃假說)**: Initial episodes triggered by severe stress → subsequent episodes occur spontaneously (threshold ↓)
* **Reality Distortion**: Psychotic features (delusions/hallucinations) in severe mania driven by extreme DA dysregulation

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Bipolar Disorder Psychiatric History
* **Mania Symptoms (DIGFAST)**: **D**istractibility, **I**nsomnia (↓ need for sleep), **G**randiosity, **F**light of ideas, **A**ctivities (hyperactive/goal-directed), **S**peech (pressured), **T**houghtlessness (reckless behavior)
* **Depressive Episodes**: Pervasive low mood, anhedonia (缺乏快感), ↑/↓ appetite, suicidal ideation
* **Collateral History**: Crucial for baseline functioning & objective manic signs (patients often lack insight during mania)

### Bipolar Disorder Mental State Examination (MSE) Findings
* **Appearance & Behavior**: Disheveled or flamboyantly dressed, hyperactive, psychomotor agitation (精神運動性激動)
* **Speech**: Pressured (語速急促), loud, punning, clang associations (音韻聯想)
* **Mood & Affect**: Subjectively elated (情緒高漲) or irritable (易怒); objectively euphoric/labile
* **Thought Form**: Flight of ideas (意念飛躍), tangentiality, loosening of associations
* **Thought Content**: Grandiose delusions (誇大妄想) (e.g., possessing special powers), persecutory delusions
* **Perception**: Mood-congruent auditory hallucinations (聽覺幻覺) (rare but possible in severe mania)
* **Cognition & Insight**: ↑ distractibility, grossly impaired insight (缺乏病識感)

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Bipolar Disorder Psychiatric Differential Diagnoses
* **Schizophrenia (精神分裂症)**: Mood incongruent delusions, persistent negative symptoms, no episodic full mood recovery
* **Schizoaffective Disorder (分裂情感性障礙)**: Psychotic symptoms persist for ≥2 weeks in the ABSENCE of prominent mood symptoms
* **Borderline Personality Disorder (邊緣性人格障礙)**: Mood lability measured in hours/days (reacts to interpersonal stress), chronic emptiness, fear of abandonment
* **Major Depressive Disorder (重度抑鬱症)**: No history of mania/hypomania

### Bipolar Disorder Organic and Medical Differential Diagnoses
* **Endocrine**: Hyperthyroidism (甲狀腺機能亢進) → mimics mania (agitation, weight loss, insomnia)
* **Substance-Induced**: Cocaine/Amphetamine intoxication (安非他命中毒)
* **Iatrogenic**: Corticosteroids (類固醇), SSRI-induced manic switch (抗抑鬱藥誘發狂躁)
* **Neurological**: Frontal lobe tumors, neurosyphilis (神經性梅毒), delirium (譫妄)

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### Bipolar Disorder Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5)
| Diagnosis | Criteria Requirement | Duration | Impairment / Psychosis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manic Episode** | Abnormally elevated/irritable mood + ↑ energy + ≥3 DIGFAST symptoms (≥4 if mood is only irritable) | ≥1 week (or any duration if hospitalized) | Severe impairment OR psychotic features present |
| **Hypomanic Episode**| Same symptom criteria as Mania | ≥4 consecutive days | Observable change in function, but NO severe impairment & NO psychosis |
| **Major Depressive** | ≥5 depressive symptoms (must include low mood or anhedonia) | ≥2 weeks | Significant distress/impairment |

### Bipolar Disorder Clinical Rating Scales
* **Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS)**: 11-item clinician-rated scale to assess mania severity
* **Mood Disorder Questionnaire (MDQ)**: Self-report screening tool for bipolar spectrum (high sensitivity for Bipolar I)

### Bipolar Disorder Organic Rule-Out Investigations
* **Bloods**: TFTs (rule out thyrotoxicosis), LFTs, U&Es, Calcium, Syphilis serology (VDRL)
* **Urine**: Toxicology screen (UDS) for sympathomimetics
* **Imaging/EEG**: CT/MRI Brain (if focal neuro signs or atypical late-onset mania)

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Bipolar Disorder Acute Risk Assessment
* **Risk to Self**: Physical exhaustion (from severe hyperactivity), dehydration, suicide (post-manic crash or mixed state)
* **Risk to Others**: Aggression due to irritability/paranoia, reckless driving
* **Social/Financial Risk**: Extravagant spending, sexual disinhibition (性衝動) → ruinous consequences

### Bipolar Disorder Acute Pharmacological Management
* **First-line Mania**: Atypical Antipsychotics (非典型抗精神病藥) (e.g., Olanzapine, Quetiapine, Risperidone) OR Haloperidol
* **Agitation**: Benzodiazepines (e.g., Lorazepam) for rapid sedation
* **Acute Bipolar Depression**: Quetiapine, Lurasidone, or Olanzapine/Fluoxetine combination (AVOID SSRI monotherapy → manic switch risk)

### Bipolar Disorder Medico-Legal Framework under HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136)
* **Voluntary Admission**: Section 30
* **Compulsory Observation (Section 31/32)**: Form 1 (Application by relative/social worker) + Form 2 (Medical recommendation by registered doctor) + Form 3 (District Judge/Magistrate signature) → hold for 7 days
* **Compulsory Treatment (Section 36)**: Extension for approved treatment (requires 2 doctors, 1 must be approved under s2(2) + Judge)
* **Police Powers (Section 136)**: Police can remove a suspected mentally ill person wandering in a public place to a Place of Safety (A&E)

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Bipolar Disorder Chronic Pharmacotherapy and Mood Stabilizers
| Drug | Mechanism | Indications | Key Monitoring / Adverse Effects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lithium (鋰鹽)** | Inhibits inositol monophosphatase | 1st line prophylaxis, anti-suicidal | Narrow therapeutic index (0.6-1.0 mmol/L). Monitor TFTs, U&Es. SE: Tremor, nephrogenic DI, hypothyroidism, teratogenic (Ebstein's anomaly) |
| **Sodium Valproate (丙戊酸鈉)** | ↑ GABA, blocks Na+ channels | 1st line for mania, rapid cycling | Monitor LFTs, FBC. SE: Weight gain, alopecia, hepatotoxicity. STRICTLY CONTRAINDICATED in females of childbearing age (highly teratogenic) |
| **Lamotrigine (拉莫三嗪)**| Blocks Na+ channels, ↓ Glutamate | 1st line for Bipolar depression | Must titrate slowly. SE: Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS) (史蒂文斯-約翰遜綜合症) |

### Bipolar Disorder Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT) Indications
* **Indications**: Refractory mania, severe bipolar depression with high suicide risk, catatonia (緊張症), or life-threatening exhaustion in mania

### Bipolar Disorder Chronic Psychological and Social Management
* **Psychoeducation (心理教育)**: Teach symptom monitoring, early warning signs (e.g., sleep reduction), strict medication adherence
* **Psychological**: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) (認知行為治療) for depressive phases, Interpersonal and Social Rhythm Therapy (IPSRT) (人際與社會節律治療) to stabilize sleep/wake cycles
* **Social**: Medical Social Worker (MSW) referral for financial ruin, family therapy to ↓ High Expressed Emotion

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Bipolar Disorder Prognosis and Relapse Rates
* **Relapse Rate**: >90% of patients will have recurrent episodes; ~50% relapse within 1 year despite medication
* **Prognostic Factors**: Poor compliance, comorbid substance abuse, young onset → worse prognosis

### Bipolar Disorder Course of Illness and Rapid Cycling
* **Chronic Course**: Progressive shortening of inter-episode euthymic intervals (kindling effect) without treatment
* **Cognitive Decline**: Repeated manic episodes correlated with long-term executive function deficits

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Bipolar Disorder Psychiatric and Social Complications
* **Psychiatric**: Suicide completion (up to 15%), alcohol/stimulant use disorders (self-medication)
* **Social**: High divorce rates, bankruptcy, loss of employment, legal charges (assault/theft during mania)

### Bipolar Disorder Psychotropic Drug Side Effects
* **Lithium Toxicity (鋰中毒)**: >1.5 mmol/L → coarse tremor, ataxia, confusion, seizures. Precipitated by dehydration, NSAIDs, Thiazides
* **Atypical Antipsychotics**: Metabolic syndrome (代謝綜合症) (↑ weight, ↑ lipids, ↑ glucose) → requires routine HbA1c/lipid monitoring

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you differentiate a hypomanic episode from a manic episode clinically? Answer: Hypomania lasts ≥4 days and causes observable change, but lacks severe functional impairment, hospitalization necessity, and psychotic features, whereas mania lasts ≥1 week and causes severe impairment or has psychosis.
* Trap: Prescribing an SSRI monotherapy for a patient presenting with depression who has a past history of hypomania. Pearl: Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar disorder can precipitate a manic switch or rapid cycling; always use a mood stabilizer backbone or atypical antipsychotic.
* Pitfall: Starting a female patient of childbearing age on Sodium Valproate without contraception. Valproate is highly teratogenic (neural tube defects) and causes PCOS; use Lithium or Lamotrigine instead.
* Viva Question: What are the primary signs of Lithium toxicity and what drugs precipitate it? Answer: Coarse tremor, ataxia, dysarthria, and confusion. Precipitated by thiazide diuretics, NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, and dehydration (↓ Na+ reabsorption → ↑ Li+ retention).
* Trap: Assuming a patient with flight of ideas and grandiosity must have primary Bipolar Disorder. Pearl: Always rule out organic causes first, particularly illicit substance use (cocaine/meth) and hyperthyroidism, using UDS and TFTs.
