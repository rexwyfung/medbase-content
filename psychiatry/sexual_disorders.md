# Sexual Dysfunction & Paraphilic Disorders (性功能障礙與性偏好症)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Clinical Definitions of Sexual Disorders (性障礙臨床定義)
* **Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙):** Clinically significant disturbance in ability to respond sexually or experience sexual pleasure. Duration ≥6 mos.
* **Paraphilia (性偏好):** Intense, persistent sexual interest in atypical objects, situations, or targets.
* **Paraphilic Disorder (性偏好症):** Paraphilia causing distress/impairment OR involving non-consenting individuals/harm → psychiatric diagnosis.

### Biopsychosocial Etiology of Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙生理心理社會成因)
* **Bio:** Vascular (atherosclerosis, DM), Neuro (MS, neuropathy), Endocrine (↓ Testosterone, hyperprolactinemia, thyroid dz), Rx-induced (SSRIs, antipsychotics).
* **Psycho:** Performance anxiety (表現焦慮), past trauma/abuse, depression, poor body image.
* **Social:** Relationship discord, cultural/religious taboos, strict upbringing, lack of privacy (e.g., cramped HK living conditions).

### Etiology of Paraphilic Disorders (性偏好症成因)
* **Bio:** Frontal lobe dysfunction (↓ impulse control), temporal lobe abnormalities, hyperandrogenism.
* **Psycho:** Classical conditioning (classical pairing of atypical stimulus with early masturbation/arousal), childhood abuse/neglect, impaired intimacy capacity.
* **Social:** Social isolation, early exposure to atypical pornography.

### Hong Kong Epidemiology (香港流行病學)
* **Sexual Dysfunction:** Highly prevalent but underreported in HK due to cultural stigma (面子). Erectile Dysfunction (ED) affects ~60% of HK men aged 50-70.
* **Paraphilic Disorders:** True prevalence unknown (legal/social consequences limit disclosure). Most commonly presenting to HK forensic/psychiatric services: Voyeuristic (窺淫症), Exhibitionistic (露體症), Frotteuristic (摩擦癖). 
* **Gender Ratio:** Paraphilic disorders overwhelmingly male (>90%).

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Neurotransmitters in Normal Sexual Response (正常性反應神經傳導物質)
* **Desire (慾望):** Dopamine (DA) ↑ → pro-sexual. Serotonin (5-HT) ↓ & Prolactin ↓ → pro-sexual.
* **Arousal (喚起):** Acetylcholine (ACh) ↑, Nitric Oxide (NO) ↑ → vasocongestion/erection. Noradrenaline (NA) ↓.
* **Orgasm (高潮):** NA ↑ (sympathetic surge) → ejaculation/muscle contraction. 5-HT ↑ post-orgasm → refractory period/satiety.

### Neurobiology of Paraphilic Disorders (性偏好症神經生物學)
* **Prefrontal Cortex (前額葉皮層):** ↓ regulation/inhibitory control over impulsive sexual urges.
* **Reward Pathway (獎賞途徑):** Hyper-reactivity in nucleus accumbens / mesolimbic DA system linked to paraphilic stimuli.

### Psychopathology of Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙精神病理學)
* **Spectatoring (旁觀者效應):** Hyper-focus on sexual performance rather than experiencing pleasure → ↑ sympathetic tone → ↓ arousal/erection.
* **Anticipatory Anxiety (預期焦慮):** Fear of failure self-fulfills (e.g., premature ejaculation → anxiety → subsequent rapid ejaculation).

### Psychopathology of Paraphilias (性偏好症精神病理學)
* **Cognitive Distortions (認知扭曲):** Rationalization of harm (e.g., "the child wanted it", "women like being watched").
* **Compulsivity (強迫性):** Escalation of behaviors; need for ↑ risk/intensity to achieve same arousal.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Psychiatric History for Sexual Disorders (性障礙精神科病史)
* **HPI:** Onset (lifelong vs. acquired), Context (generalized vs. situational/partner-specific), Severity (frequency, duration). 
* **Sexual Hx (性史):** Masturbation habits (if functional solo but not with partner → psychological etiology), early sexual experiences, sexual orientation/identity.
* **Psych Hx:** Screen for Depression (抑鬱), Anxiety (焦慮), Substance misuse.
* **Relationship Hx:** Partner's attitude, conflicts, communication, domestic violence.

### Paraphilia-Specific History (性偏好症特定病史)
* **Nature of Urges:** Target (children, non-consenting adults, objects), frequency, distress caused.
* **Forensic Hx (法醫病史):** Arrests, convictions under HK Crimes Ordinance (Cap 200). 
* **Escalation:** Movement from fantasy to non-contact offenses (voyeurism) to contact offenses.

### Mental State Examination Findings (精神狀態檢查發現)
* **Appearance/Behavior:** Often normal. May show visible anxiety when discussing sexual topics.
* **Mood/Affect:** Depressed/anxious (in SD); guilt/shame OR flattened/indifferent (in ego-syntonic paraphilias).
* **Thoughts:** Preoccupations with sexual performance (SD) or paraphilic fantasies. Cognitive distortions minimizing harm to victims. 
* **Insight (洞察力):** Often poor in paraphilias (ego-syntonic), leading to ↑ reoffending risk. Better in SD (ego-dystonic).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Medical Rule-Outs for Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙醫學排除)
* **Vascular:** Atherosclerosis, HTN (major ED causes).
* **Endocrine:** Hypogonadism, hyperthyroid (Premature Ejaculation), hypothyroid (Delayed Ejaculation), DM (neuropathy/vascular).
* **Neuro:** Spinal cord injury, MS, pelvic nerve damage (post-prostatectomy).
* **Pharmacological:** SSRIs, typical antipsychotics, antihypertensives (β-blockers, thiazides), spironolactone.

### Psychiatric Differentials for Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙精神科鑑別)
* **MDD (重度抑鬱症):** ↓ libido is a core symptom; precedes sexual dysfunction.
* **Anxiety Disorders:** GAD, Panic Disorder → ↑ sympathetic tone → ↓ arousal.
* **Substance Use Disorder:** Alcohol/Opioids → acute delayed ejaculation, chronic ↓ libido/ED.

### Psychiatric Differentials for Paraphilias (性偏好症精神科鑑別)
* **OCD (強迫症):** Intrusive sexual thoughts (ego-dystonic, cause severe anxiety, NO sexual arousal).
* **Bipolar Mania:** Hypersexuality (indiscriminate, not restricted to specific paraphilic target).
* **Dementia/Organic Brain Syndrome:** Frontal lobe disinhibition causing inappropriate sexual behavior (not a true paraphilia).

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### DSM-5 Criteria for Major Sexual Dysfunctions (DSM-5 主要性功能障礙準則)
| Disorder | Core Features (Must last ≥6 months, cause distress) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Male Hypoactive Sexual Desire DO** | Persistently ↓/absent sexual thoughts/fantasies and desire. |
| **Erectile Disorder (ED)** | Difficulty obtaining/maintaining erection, or ↓ rigidity. |
| **Premature Ejaculation** | Ejaculation within ~1 min of penetration, before desired. |
| **Female Sexual Interest/Arousal DO** | Absent/↓ interest, thoughts, initiation, or physical arousal. |
| **Genito-Pelvic Pain/Penetration DO** | Dyspareunia, vaginismus, fear/anxiety of pelvic pain. |

### DSM-5 Criteria for Major Paraphilic Disorders (DSM-5 主要性偏好症準則)
| Disorder (English/Chinese) | Atypical Target / Arousal Source (Must last ≥6 months) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Voyeuristic (窺淫症)** | Observing unsuspecting person naked, disrobing, or in sexual act. |
| **Exhibitionistic (露體症)** | Exposing one's genitals to an unsuspecting person. |
| **Frotteuristic (摩擦癖)** | Touching/rubbing against a non-consenting person. |
| **Sexual Masochism (受虐症)** | Being humiliated, beaten, bound, or made to suffer. |
| **Sexual Sadism (施虐症)** | Physical or psychological suffering of another person. |
| **Pedophilic (戀童症)** | Prepubescent children (generally ≤13 yrs). Pt must be ≥16 & ≥5 yrs older. |
| **Fetishistic (戀物症)** | Nonliving objects (e.g., shoes) or highly specific non-genital body parts. |

### Rating Scales (評估量表)
* **Sexual Dysfunction:** International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF), Female Sexual Function Index (FSFI).
* **Paraphilias/Risk:** Static-99 (assesses risk of sexual recidivism).

### Organic Rule-Out Investigations (器質性排除檢查)
* **Bloods:** Fasting glucose, HbA1c, Lipid profile, early morning total/free Testosterone, Prolactin, TFTs.
* **Specialized:** Nocturnal penile tumescence (NPT) testing (differentiates psychogenic vs. organic ED: normal NPT → psychogenic).

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Acute Risk Assessment in Paraphilias (性偏好症急性風險評估)
* **Risk to Others:** HIGH if targets non-consenting victims, children, or involves sadism. 
* **Escalation Signs:** Moving from fantasy → planning → boundary probing → contact offenses.
* **Static Risk Factors:** Male, young age, prior sexual offenses, stranger victims, male victims (in pedophilia).
* **Dynamic Risk Factors:** Active substance abuse, acute psychosocial stress, non-compliance with Rx, antisocial traits.

### HK Mental Health Ordinance Cap 136 Application (香港精神健康條例第136章應用)
* **Compulsory Admission (Section 2/3):** Paraphilic disorder meets the definition of "mental disorder" under Cap 136. If a patient with severe pedophilic/sadistic disorder poses an *immediate, severe risk to others*, Section 2 (28 days) or Section 3 (6 months) CAN be applied for protection of others, though typically managed via criminal justice system if offense committed.
* **Hospital Order (Section 37):** Courts can mandate psychiatric hospital treatment post-conviction instead of prison if mental disorder requires medical Rx (often used in forensic psychiatry).

### Mandatory Reporting & Child Protection (強制通報與兒童保護)
* **Safeguarding:** If patient discloses active child abuse/pedophilic acts, confidentiality MUST be breached. Report to HK Police and Social Welfare Department (SWD) Family and Child Protective Services Unit (FCPSU).

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Pharmacotherapy for Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙藥物治療)
* **ED:** PDE-5 inhibitors (Sildenafil/Viagra). *Contraindication:* Nitrates (→ fatal hypotension).
* **Premature Ejaculation:** SSRIs (Dapoxetine - short-acting, taken PRN; or daily Paroxetine) to delay ejaculation. Topical anesthetics.
* **Hypoactive Desire:** Treat underlying MDD; Testosterone replacement ONLY if hypogonadal.

### Pharmacotherapy for Paraphilic Disorders (性偏好症藥物治療)
* **SSRIs (e.g., Fluoxetine):** High doses used off-label to ↓ sexual compulsivity, ↓ libido, and manage comorbid OCD/depression.
* **Anti-androgens (Chemical Castration - 化學閹割):** Cyproterone acetate (blocks androgen receptors), Leuprolide (GnRH agonist → ↓ LH/FSH → ↓ testosterone). Indicated for high-risk, hypersexual paraphilic disorders (e.g., severe pedophilia/sadism).

### Psychotherapy & Behavioral Interventions (心理治療與行為干預)
* **For Sexual Dysfunction:**
  * **Sensate Focus (性感集中訓練):** Masters & Johnson technique. Graduated non-genital touching → genital touching → penetration. Bans intercourse initially to ↓ performance anxiety.
  * **CBT:** Cognitive restructuring for performance anxiety.
* **For Paraphilic Disorders:**
  * **Relapse Prevention Therapy (RPT):** Identify triggers/high-risk situations, develop avoidance strategies, victim empathy training.
  * **Aversion Therapy:** Pairing paraphilic stimuli with unpleasant stimuli (historically used, now rare).

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Course of Illness in Sexual Dysfunction (性功能障礙病程)
* **Psychogenic:** Often episodic, situational, acute onset. Good prognosis with CBT/couples therapy if relationship intact.
* **Organic:** Gradual onset, progressive, generalized. Requires ongoing medical Mx (e.g., PDE-5 inhibitors).

### Prognosis of Paraphilic Disorders (性偏好症預後)
* **Course:** Chronic, lifelong, fluctuating with stress. Often ↓ in intensity after age 50 (due to natural ↓ in androgens).
* **Prognosis:** Poor. High recidivism rates, especially for pedophilia and sexual sadism. Better prognosis if ego-dystonic, high guilt, intact social support, and voluntary treatment engagement.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Psychiatric & Social Complications (精神與社會併發症)
* **SD:** Marital breakdown, infertility, secondary depression/anxiety, ↓ self-esteem.
* **Paraphilias:** Criminal conviction, imprisonment, sex offender registry (SCRC in HK), social ostracization, suicide (secondary to shame/legal consequences).

### Psychotropic Drug Side Effects on Sexual Function (精神科藥物對性功能的副作用)
| Drug Class | Mechanism | Sexual Side Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SSRIs/SNRIs** | ↑ 5-HT signaling in descending spinal pathways | Delayed ejaculation, anorgasmia, ↓ libido. |
| **Typical Antipsychotics** | D2 blockade → ↑ Prolactin | ↓ libido, ED, amenorrhea, galactorrhea. |
| **Trazodone** | α1-adrenergic blockade | Priapism (陰莖異常勃起) - Medical emergency! |
| **Bupropion / Mirtazapine** | Sparing of 5-HT, ↑ DA/NA | Minimal sexual SEs (often used to reverse SSRI-induced dysfunction). |

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you clinically differentiate psychogenic from organic erectile dysfunction? Answer: Psychogenic ED has sudden onset, is situational, and maintains morning/nocturnal erections (normal NPT). Organic ED is gradual, generalized, and lacks morning erections.
* Trap: Assuming hypersexuality in a manic patient qualifies as a Paraphilic Disorder. Pearl: Paraphilic disorders require intense sexual arousal specifically linked to an *atypical* target or situation lasting ≥6 months, not generalized hypersexuality secondary to an acute mood episode.
* Pitfall: Prescribing a PDE-5 inhibitor (Sildenafil) to a patient with ED without checking their full medication list. Always rule out concurrent nitrate use for angina, as the combination causes refractory, potentially fatal hypotension.
* Viva Question: What is the pharmacological rationale for using SSRIs in premature ejaculation? Answer: Serotonin generally inhibits the orgasmic response; SSRIs increase synaptic serotonin, thereby delaying the ejaculatory reflex.
* Trap: Diagnosing Pedophilic Disorder based solely on the victim's age. Pearl: The DSM-5 criteria strictly require the patient to be at least 16 years old AND at least 5 years older than the prepubescent child.
* Pitfall: Promising confidentiality when a patient discloses active pedophilic behavior. You must clearly state the limits of confidentiality regarding child protection and mandatory reporting to HK Police and SWD.
