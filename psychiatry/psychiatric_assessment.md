# Psychiatric Assessment (精神科評估)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong

### Definition of Psychiatric Assessment (精神科評估定義)
* **Core Function:** Systematic clinical process combining objective observation (客觀觀察) + subjective history-taking (主觀病史).
* **Primary Goal:** Syndrome identification → definitive diagnosis (診斷) → biopsychosocial formulation (生物心理社會綜合分析) → personalized management plan.
* **Dual Capacity:** Requires scientific data collection (Erklären / Explanation) + intuitive empathy (Verstehen / Understanding).

### Scope of Clinical Evaluation (臨床評估範圍)
* **Diagnostic Categorization:** Matching symptom clusters to ICD-11 (HA standard) or DSM-5 frameworks.
* **Contextualization:** Evaluating premorbid personality (病前性格), social stressors, physical comorbidities.
* **Risk Stratification:** Identifying immediate threats of harm to self (自殺傾向), harm to others (暴力傾向), or severe self-neglect (自我照顧能力喪失).

### Relevance to Hong Kong Healthcare System (香港醫療系統之相關性)
* **Hospital Authority (HA) (醫院管理局):** Triage system across AEDs (急症室), outpatient clinics (門診), and acute psychiatric wards (急症精神科病房).
* **Resource Optimization:** Brief, high-yield assessments required due to high patient volume (e.g., 10-15 min follow-ups).
* **Cultural Nuance:** High prevalence of somatization (軀體化) in HK Chinese populations; stigma (標籤效應) often masks primary affective symptoms.

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms

### Descriptive Psychopathology (描述性精神病理學) Principle
* **Phenomenology (現象學):** Objective description of morbid mental states avoiding premature theoretical assumptions.
* **Form vs Content (形式與內容):** 
  * **Form:** Type of phenomenon (e.g., Auditory Hallucination 幻聽). → *Diagnostic utility.*
  * **Content:** Specific theme (e.g., voices commanding suicide). → *Risk management utility.*

### Biopsychosocial Formulation (生物心理社會綜合分析) Mechanism
* **Biological (生物):** Genetic loading, neurochemical imbalance, organic rule-outs (e.g., Thyroid disease).
* **Psychological (心理):** Coping mechanisms, cognitive distortions, early childhood trauma.
* **Social (社會):** Housing (公屋/劏房 stress), employment, caregiver burden.
* **Chronological Matrix:** 
  * **Predisposing (預先因素):** Vulnerability (e.g., FHx, personality).
  * **Precipitating (誘發因素):** Triggers (e.g., relationship breakdown, exam stress).
  * **Perpetuating (維持因素):** Maintaining factors (e.g., substance abuse, ongoing debt).

### Empathy vs Objective Observation Dynamics
* **Therapeutic Alliance (治療聯盟):** Rapport building is diagnostic + therapeutic; facilitates disclosure of sensitive information (e.g., AH/VH, sexual dysfunction).
* **Typification Mechanism:** Rapid provisional pattern recognition by experienced clinicians within minutes, refined via systematic hypothesis testing.

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components

### The Psychiatric History (精神科病史) Framework
* **Chief Complaint (主訴):** Patient's own words.
* **History of Presenting Illness (HPI) (現病史):** Onset, duration, severity, modifiers, associated neurovegetative symptoms.
* **Past Psychiatric History (過去精神科病史):** Previous episodes, admissions, medications, compliance.
* **Past Medical History (過去醫療病史):** Organic rule-outs, operations, current meds.
* **Family History (家族史):** Psychiatric conditions, suicides, genetic loading.
* **Personal/Social History (個人及社會史):** Birth/development, schooling, occupational, marital, forensic (法醫), substance use (substance/alcohol).
* **Premorbid Personality (病前性格):** Baseline character traits, coping style.

### Mental State Examination (MSE) Components (精神狀態檢查)
| MSE Domain (領域) | Key Clinical Observations (臨床觀察重點) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Appearance & Behaviour (外表與行為)** | Self-care, psychomotor agitation/retardation, eye contact, extrapyramidal symptoms (EPS). |
| **Speech (言語)** | Rate (pressure/poverty), volume, tone, articulation (dysarthria). |
| **Mood & Affect (情緒與情感)** | Mood (subjective prevailing state), Affect (objective observed emotional responsiveness/congruence). |
| **Thought (思考)** | **Form:** Flight of ideas, loosening of associations, thought blocking.<br>**Content:** Delusions (妄想), obsessions, suicidal/homicidal ideation. |
| **Perception (知覺)** | Hallucinations (幻覺), illusions, depersonalization/derealization. |
| **Cognition (認知)** | Orientation (Time/Place/Person), attention, memory (short/long-term), visuospatial. |
| **Insight (病識感)** | Awareness of illness, attribution of symptoms, willingness to accept treatment. |

### Risk Assessment (風險評估) Matrix
* **Harm to Self (對自身危險):** Suicidal ideation, planning, lethality of intent, deliberate self-harm (DSH).
* **Harm to Others (對他人危險):** Homicidal ideation, command hallucinations, persecutory delusions targeting specific individuals, access to weapons.
* **Harm from Others (受他人傷害):** Vulnerability, abuse, exploitation.

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools

### Standardized Diagnostic Schedules
* **SCID (Structured Clinical Interview for DSM):** Gold standard for structured diagnostic evaluation.
* **SCAN (Schedules for Clinical Assessment in Neuropsychiatry):** Comprehensive observer-rated tool.

### Cognitive Assessment Instruments (認知評估工具)
| Tool | Indication | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MMSE** | General dementia screen | Max 30 pts. Tests orientation, registration, recall, language. Insensitive to mild cognitive impairment (MCI). |
| **MoCA (HK-MoCA)** | Dementia / MCI screen | Max 30 pts. Superior for executive function & visuospatial domains. Validated Cantonese version available. |

### Symptom Severity Rating Scales
* **Depression (抑鬱症):** MADRS (observer-rated), BDI / PHQ-9 (self-rated).
* **Schizophrenia (精神分裂症):** PANSS (Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale), BPRS.
* **Mania (躁狂症):** YMRS (Young Mania Rating Scale).
* **Anxiety (焦慮症):** GAD-7, HADS (Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale).

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations

### Reliability and Validity in Assessment
* **Reliability (可靠性):** Improved significantly post-introduction of operational criteria (ICD/DSM) and structured interviews.
* **Validity (有效性):** Remains limited by descriptive nature of syndromes (construct validity ↓ due to lack of distinct biological markers for most functional disorders).

### Limitations of Cross-Sectional Evaluation
* **Fluctuating Course:** Single interviews may miss cyclical symptom variations (e.g., rapid-cycling Bipolar).
* **Concealment:** Patients may deliberately hide delusions, paranoia, or suicidal intent due to fear of detention (Cap 136).
* **Recall Bias:** Depressed patients recall negative events disproportionately (mood-congruent memory bias).

### Efficacy of Multidisciplinary Collateral History
* **Requirement:** Psychiatric assessment is fundamentally incomplete without collateral history (informant history).
* **Sources:** Family, medical social workers (MSW), community psychiatric nurses (CPN), old HA records (CMS).
* **Yield:** Identifies discrepancies in functional decline, substance abuse, and personality changes.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols

### Preparation and Setting the Scene
* **Safety First:** Assess risk prior to interview. Ensure clear exit path (never let patient block the door). Duress alarms accessible.
* **Environment:** Quiet, private, well-lit.
* **Opening:** Introduce role, state purpose, outline time limits (e.g., 45-60 mins for new case), explain confidentiality limits.

### Interview Techniques and Flow
* **Open to Closed Questioning (開放至封閉式提問):** Start broad ("Tell me about your difficulties") → narrow down to specific diagnostic criteria ("Do you hear voices when alone?").
* **Facilitation:** Nodding, "tell me more".
* **Redirection:** Firm but polite interruption for tangential/garrulous patients (flight of ideas).
* **Empathy:** Validate distress without colluding with delusions (e.g., "It must be terrifying to feel followed, even though I don't see anyone").

### Emergency & A&E Department (急症室) Protocols
* **Triage Priority:** Focus strictly on immediate risks (suicide, aggression, severe agitation).
* **Organic Rule-Out:** Vital signs, GCS, tox screen, metabolic panel, CT brain (if neurological signs +ve). Differentiate delirium (譫妄) from acute psychosis.
* **Disposition:** Formulate immediate plan → Admit (Voluntary vs Involuntary) vs Discharge with early CPN follow-up.

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks (Cap 136)

### Mental Capacity Assessment Framework
* Assess patient's ability to consent/refuse medical treatment.
* **Criteria (4-Prong Test):** Patient must be able to:
  1. **Understand (理解):** Retain relevant information regarding treatment.
  2. **Retain (保留):** Hold information long enough to decide.
  3. **Weigh (權衡):** Process pros/cons to arrive at a choice.
  4. **Communicate (表達):** Convey the decision.

### Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) (精神健康條例) Application
| Section (條例) | Indication (適用情況) | Duration / Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sec 31** | Voluntary Admission (自願入院) | Consent given; standard ward admission. |
| **Sec 36** | Detention for Observation (觀察期留院) | Up to 7 days. Signed by 1 registered medical practitioner + District Judge/Magistrate. |
| **Sec 42** | Police Intervention | Police power to convey wandering mentally ill persons in public to AED (place of safety). |
| **Sec 71B** | Guardianship Order (監護令) | Gives Guardian power to require patient to reside at specific place/attend treatment. |

### Confidentiality and Duty to Warn
* **General Rule:** Strict confidentiality applied.
* **Exceptions:** 
  * Imminent risk to self (suicide).
  * Imminent risk to others (homicide/violence).
  * Statutory requirements (e.g., child abuse reporting, fitness to drive).
  * Court orders.

## 8. Special Populations (Lifespan, Intellectual Disability, Comorbidities)

### Paediatric and Adolescent Assessment
* **Triadic Interview:** Assess child alone, parents alone, and child + parents together.
* **Developmental Focus:** Milestones, academic performance, peer dynamics, bullying, family discord.
* **Safeguarding:** Mandatory assessment for non-accidental injury (NAI) or sexual abuse.

### Geriatric (Old Age) Psychiatry Adjustments
* **Pacing:** Slower interview speed; compensate for presbycusis (hearing loss) / visual impairment.
* **Collateral:** Absolute necessity to detect insidious dementia onset vs pseudodementia (depression).
* **Polypharmacy:** Thorough review for drug-induced delirium/confusion.

### Intellectual Disability (智力障礙) Modifications
* **Simplification:** Concrete language; avoid abstract concepts/metaphors. Check understanding of closed questions (e.g., check if they say 'yes' to both "Are you happy?" and "Are you sad?").
* **Behavioural Equivalents:** Assess distress via behavioural changes (e.g., ↑ head-banging = unrecognized pain or depression).

### Cross-Cultural & Language Barriers
* **Interpreters:** Use official HA interpreters, NOT family members (avoids translation bias or concealment of sensitive topics like abuse).
* **Cultural Idioms of Distress:** Recognize neurasthenia (神經衰弱) or somatic complaints as expressions of affective illness.

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges

### Ethical Dilemmas in Involuntary Assessment
* **Beneficence vs Autonomy:** Balancing the duty to protect/treat the patient (beneficence) against the infringement of their civil liberties (autonomy) under Cap 136.
* **Coercion (強迫):** Distinguishing legitimate leverage (e.g., housing support linked to compliance) from unethical threats.

### Stigma and Labelling Systemic Issues
* **Diagnostic Overshadowing:** Medical professionals attributing all new physical symptoms to the patient's existing psychiatric label (e.g., ignoring a brain tumour in a patient with Schizophrenia).
* **Self-Stigma:** Patient internalizing the psychiatric label → ↓ self-esteem, isolation.

### Transference and Countertransference Challenges
* **Transference:** Patient unconsciously projecting past relationship feelings onto the doctor.
* **Countertransference:** Doctor's emotional entanglement/reaction to the patient. Must remain aware to maintain objective assessment boundaries.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* **Viva Question:** How do you clinically differentiate a true auditory hallucination from an obsessional thought during an MSE?
* **Answer:** A true hallucination is perceived as originating from external space via sensory organs without a stimulus. An obsessional thought is recognized by the patient as originating internally from their own mind, despite being intrusive and ego-dystonic.
* **Trap:** Assuming a patient who smiles appropriately and makes good eye contact cannot be severely depressed.
* **Pearl:** "Smiling depression" is a high-risk presentation. Patients with severe suicidal intent may exhibit transient subjective brightening (affective lifting) once they have finalized their suicide plan, masking underlying profound depressive cognitions.
* **Pitfall:** Using family members as interpreters for ethnic minority or non-Cantonese speaking patients during initial assessment.
* **Explanation:** Family members may intentionally or unintentionally filter, alter, or censor sensitive information (e.g., domestic violence, psychosis, substance abuse) due to stigma or personal involvement. Always use professional HA interpreters to ensure assessment validity.
