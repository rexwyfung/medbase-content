# Ethics & Civil Law (倫理與民事法律)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong
### Definition of Psychiatric Ethics (精神科倫理定義)
* **Psychiatric Ethics** deals with moral principles governing clinical decisions, especially regarding **Autonomy (自主權)** vs **Paternalism (家長式管治)**.
* Central conflict: Duty to protect patient/public vs duty to respect patient freedom.

### Scope of Civil Law in HK Psychiatry (香港精神科民事法律範疇)
* Governs non-criminal legal relationships (e.g., negligence, contracts, wills).
* Crucial for assessing **Testamentary Capacity (立遺囑能力)**, **Power of Attorney (授權書)**, and fitness to drive/work.
* HK context heavily relies on **Mental Health Ordinance Cap 136 (精神健康條例)** for involuntary treatment and **Part IVC** for treating incapacitated adults.

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms (Psychological, Pharmacological, or Legal)
### The Four Pillars of Medical Ethics (醫學倫理四大支柱)
* **Beneficence (行善原則)**: → Acting in the patient's best interest.
* **Non-maleficence (不傷害原則)**: → Primum non nocere; minimizing harm/side effects.
* **Autonomy (自主原則)**: → Respecting patient's right to self-determination (requires capacity).
* **Justice (公義原則)**: → Fair distribution of scarce resources; treating similar cases equally.

### Utilitarian vs Duty-Based Approaches (功利主義與義務論)
* **Utilitarian (功利主義)**: Action justified if it maximizes overall benefit (e.g., breaching confidentiality to prevent public harm).
* **Duty-Based / Deontological (義務論)**: Strict adherence to moral rules regardless of outcome (e.g., never engaging in sexual relations with patients).

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components
### Framework of Mental Capacity (精神行為能力框架)
* Capacity is **Decision-Specific (特定決定)** & **Time-Specific (特定時間)**.
* 4 Components to establish capacity:
  * 1. **Understand (理解)** info relevant to the decision.
  * 2. **Retain (保留)** that info long enough to decide.
  * 3. **Weigh/Evaluate (衡量)** the info as part of the decision-making process.
  * 4. **Communicate (表達)** the decision.

### Components of Informed Consent (知情同意要素)
* **Information (資訊)**: Diagnosis, proposed treatment, alternatives, risks/benefits.
* **Voluntariness (自願性)**: Free from coercion, undue influence, or extreme leverage.
* **Competence/Capacity (能力)**: Must possess legal/mental capacity to consent.

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools
### Indications for Capacity Assessment (能力評估指引)
* Refusal of life-saving medical/psychiatric treatment.
* Making/changing a will (**Testamentary Capacity**).
* Assigning Enduring Power of Attorney (EPA).
* Decisions regarding major financial affairs, discharge planning, or marriage.

### Standardized Steps for Testamentary Capacity Assessment (立遺囑能力評估)
* Derived from the classic *Banks v Goodfellow* criteria:
  * 1. Understand the nature of making a will & its effects.
  * 2. Comprehend the extent of the property being disposed of.
  * 3. Know the identity of individuals with reasonable claims to the estate (beneficiaries).
  * 4. Free from abnormal mental states (e.g., **Delusions (妄想)**) that poison affections or distort judgements regarding the will.

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations
### Efficacy of Advance Directives (預設醫療指示效用)
* Allows patients to state preferences for future care when they lose capacity.
* ↑ Patient autonomy & therapeutic alliance.
* ↓ Family disputes during crisis.
* Limitations: May be overridden by **Cap 136** for psychiatric treatment in HK; strictly binding only for specified physical life-sustaining treatments.

### Limitations of Compulsory Treatment Evidence (強制治療證據限制)
* Community Treatment Orders (CTOs) / Conditional Discharge (**Cap 136 Section 42B**):
  * Designed to ↓ revolving-door admissions.
  * RCT evidence globally shows limited impact on readmission rates.
  * Highly effective politically for public safety assurance, but ethically debated due to prolonged coercion.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols
### Execution of Confidentiality (保密原則執行)
* Fundamental to the doctor-patient trust.
* Survives the patient's death.
* Only disclose minimum necessary information.
* Use anonymized data for research/teaching unless specific consent is obtained.

### Exceptions to Confidentiality (打破保密原則的例外)
* **Patient Consent (病人同意)**: Explicit informed consent provided.
* **Public Interest (公眾利益)**: Risk of serious harm/death to a specific third party (e.g., Tarasoff duty to warn) or public (e.g., unfit to drive).
* **Legal Requirement (法律要求)**: Court orders, statutory reporting (e.g., suspected child abuse, specific infectious diseases).
* **Best Interests (最佳利益)**: Patient lacks capacity & disclosure to carers/medical team is essential for treatment.

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks (e.g., Cap 136)
### HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) Overview (香港精神健康條例)
* Statutory framework for compulsory admission, treatment, & guardianship of persons with mental disorder in HK.
* **Mental Disorder (精神錯亂)** definition in HK: Includes mental illness, state of arrested/incomplete development of mind, psychopathic disorder, or any other disorder/disability of mind.

### Compulsory Admission Sections under Cap 136 (強制入院條例)
| Section | Purpose | Duration | Key Signatories Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sec 31** | Voluntary Admission (自願入院) | N/A | Patient consent |
| **Sec 31B** | Minor Voluntary Admission | N/A | Parent/Guardian consent |
| **Sec 32** | Observation (觀察) | Up to 7 days | 1 Medical Practitioner + District Judge/Magistrate |
| **Sec 36** | Certified Patient (Treatment) | Indefinite (Subject to review) | 2 Medical Practitioners (1 must be approved) + Judge |
| **Sec 42B** | Conditional Discharge (有條件出院) | Indefinite | Imposed by Medical Superintendent / MHRT |
| **Sec 71B** | Police Intervention | 24 hours | Police (danger in public place) → take to hospital |

### Part IVC of Cap 136 (Medical/Dental Treatment)
* Allows treatment of incapacitated adults (+18) without consent if acting in their **Best Interests (最佳利益)**.
* Applicable to physical illnesses when psychiatric patients lack capacity.

## 8. Special Populations (Lifespan, Intellectual Disability, Comorbidities)
### Minors and Consent (未成年人同意)
* Age <18: Parents/guardians provide proxy consent.
* **Gillick Competence (吉利克能力)**: If a minor has sufficient maturity/intelligence to fully understand the proposed treatment, they may consent independently (rarely used to *refuse* life-saving treatment).
* **Child Abuse (虐兒)**: Mandatory ethical breach of confidentiality to protect the child.

### Severe Intellectual Disability & Comorbidities (嚴重智力障礙)
* High risk for lacking capacity.
* Guardianship Board (**Cap 136 Part IVB**) can appoint a guardian to make decisions (residence, medical/dental treatment, work) for adults who lack capacity due to mental disorder/intellectual disability.

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges
### Coercion & The Therapeutic Alliance (強迫與治療關係)
* Informal coercion (leverage, inducements, threats) is ubiquitous in psychiatry (e.g., "Take your meds or we can't recommend housing").
* Adverse effect: ↓ Trust, ↑ treatment evasion post-discharge.
* Mitigation: Maximize **Procedural Justice (程序公義)** → ensure the patient feels heard, respected, and understands the rationale, even if disagreeing.

### Dilemmas in Dual Agency (雙重代理的困境)
* Psychiatrist acts for the patient vs acts for the State/Society (e.g., forensic assessments, fitness to work, conditional discharge).
* Must explicitly inform the patient at the start of the interview that confidentiality is limited and the report is for a third party.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls
* Viva Question: How do you assess capacity for a patient refusing a life-saving amputation? Answer: Assess the 4 domains (Understand, Retain, Weigh, Communicate). If impaired by mental illness (e.g., nihilistic delusions), treat the psychiatric illness first under Cap 136; if capacity remains absent, proceed under Part IVC in their best interests.
* Trap: Assuming compulsory psychiatric admission (Cap 136 Sec 36) allows forced treatment of co-morbid physical diseases. Pearl: Cap 136 covers psychiatric treatment only. Physical treatment for an incapacitated patient relies on Part IVC or common law doctrine of necessity.
* Pitfall: Breaching confidentiality for minor risks. Avoid by applying the "Public Interest" test strictly: only breach to prevent serious crime, imminent risk to life, or serious harm to a third party (e.g., child abuse).
* Viva Question: What are the criteria for Testamentary Capacity? Answer: Banks v Goodfellow criteria: Understand nature of a will, comprehend extent of property, know potential beneficiaries, and be free from delusions affecting the disposal of property.
* Trap: Assuming a patient with Schizophrenia automatically lacks capacity. Pearl: Capacity is decision-specific and time-specific. A patient with active hallucinations may still have full capacity to consent to appendectomy.
