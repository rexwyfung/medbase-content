# Public & Global Mental Health (公共與全球精神衞生)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong
### Definition
* **Public Mental Health (公共精神衞生)**: Promotes mental well-being, prevents disorders, and reduces population morbidity/mortality.
* Focuses on primary/secondary prevention, social determinants of health (SDOH), health equity, and stigma reduction.
* **Global Mental Health (全球精神衞生)**: Prioritizes equity, universal access, and human rights across income levels.

### Core Principles
* Aims to close the **Mental Health Treatment Gap (精神衞生治療缺口)** (>75% in LMICs, ~50-70% in HICs).
* Promotes **Task-Shifting (任務轉移)**: Training non-specialists (e.g., nurses, peers) to deliver psychological interventions.

### Local Epidemiology (Hong Kong)
* **HKMMS (香港精神健康調查)**: 13.3% past-week prevalence of **Common Mental Disorders (CMD, 常見精神障礙)** in Chinese adults (16–75).
* Most common: **Mixed Anxiety and Depressive Disorder (混合焦慮抑鬱症)** (6.8%) → **Generalized Anxiety Disorder (廣泛性焦慮症)** (4.2%).
* >70% with CMD do not seek professional help.
* **Elderly**: Disproportionate suicide clustering (rates often >20-25 per 100,000) driven by chronic illness, disability, and isolation (e.g., "hidden elderly" [隱蔽長者]).
* **Youth**: Increased self-harm post-pandemic; addressed via a three-tier school-based emergency response mechanism (三層應急機制).

### HK Healthcare System
* **Hospital Authority (HA, 醫院管理局)** provides ~90% of specialist care.
* High demand on public psychiatric SOPCs (Specialist Outpatient Clinics); routine waits can exceed 60-90 weeks.
* Manpower: ~5.5 psychiatrists per 100,000 (below WHO/OECD benchmarks of 10-15).

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms
### Social Determinants of Mental Health
* **Social Causation (社會致病論)**: Adversity and low SES (e.g., subdivided flats [劏房]) cause chronic stress (HPA-axis hyperactivity, ↑ cortisol, ↑ IL-6/TNF-$\alpha$) → ↑ MDD/anxiety vulnerability.
* **Social Drift (社會下移論)**: Severe mental illness (SMI, e.g., Schizophrenia [精神分裂症]) leads to functional impairment → descent into lower SES.

### Epidemiological Prevention
* **Primary Prevention (一級預防)**: ↓ Incidence. Universal (e.g., Joyful@HK [好心情@HK]), selective (high-risk families), indicated (Ultra-High Risk [UHR] for psychosis).
* **Secondary Prevention (二級預防)**: Early detection/intervention to ↓ duration/prevalence (e.g., **EASY [Early Assessment Service for Young People with Early Psychosis, 思覺智優計劃]**, PHQ-9 screening).
* **Tertiary Prevention (三級預防)**: ↓ Disability, promote rehabilitation (e.g., sheltered workshops [庇護工場]).

### Disease Burden Metrics
* **DALYs (殘疾調整生命年)**: $YLL + YLD$.
* **YLL (早逝壽命損失年)**: Premature mortality (e.g., suicide, SMI cardiovascular/metabolic deaths).
* **YLD (殘疾健康壽命損失年)**: Morbidity (Depressive disorders are a leading cause globally).

### Stigma
* **Public Stigma (公眾污名)**: Stereotypes → prejudice → discrimination.
* **Self-Stigma (自我污名)**: Internalization → ↓ self-esteem/efficacy, treatment avoidance.
* **Structural Stigma (體制性污名)**: Systemic under-budgeting (mental health parity gap).

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components
### WHO mhGAP-IG
* **Mental Health Gap Action Programme Intervention Guide**: Evidence-based algorithms for non-specialized settings.
* Covers priority conditions: Depression, Psychoses, Epilepsy, Child/Adolescent Disorders, Dementia, Substance Use, Self-harm/Suicide.
* Uses task-sharing protocols.

### HK HA Stepped Care Framework
* **Level 1**: Public health education, District Health Centres (DHC, 地區康健中心).
* **Level 2**: **Integrated Mental Health Programme (IMHP, 綜合精神健康計劃)** at GOPCs (Mild/moderate CMD).
* **Level 3**: **Common Mental Disorder Clinics (CMDC, 常見精神障礙門診)**, EASY Clinics, routine SOPC.
* **Level 4**: Acute inpatient, Crisis Intervention Teams.

### Community Infrastructure (SWD)
* **ICCMW (精神健康綜合社區中心)**: 24 district-based hubs by NGOs for ex-mentally ill, suspected cases, families, and residents (day training, counseling, peer support).

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools
### Screening Indications
* High-risk presentations: Unexplained somatic complaints, chronic insomnia, substance misuse, postpartum, medical multimorbidity.
* Populations: Post-disaster, isolated elderly, school refusers.

### Validated Screening Instruments
* **PHQ-9 (病人健康問卷-9)**: 9 items (DSM-5 MDD). Score $\ge 10$ = moderate (clinical cutoff).
* **GAD-7 (廣泛性焦慮量表-7)**: 7 items. Score $\ge 10$ = moderate.
* **EPDS (愛丁堡產後抑鬱量表)**: 10 items. Screened at 6-8 weeks postpartum at MCHCs (母嬰健康院). HK cutoff $\ge 10$ or +ve suicidal item.
* **GDS-15 (長者抑鬱量表)**: 15 binary items for elderly. Cutoff $\ge 8$.

### Global Assessment Scales
* **WHO-DAS 2.0**: Measures functional disability across 6 domains (ICF).
* **GAF (功能整體評估量表)**: 0-100 scale combining symptom severity and psychosocial functioning.

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations
### Early Intervention in Psychosis
* Reducing **Duration of Untreated Psychosis (DUP, 未治療精神病期)** improves outcomes: ↑ positive symptom remission, preserved cognition/gray matter, ↓ neuroleptic needs, ↓ relapse/re-hospitalization.
* HK EASY program is cost-effective, reduces early suicide, and improves vocational recovery.

### Suicide Prevention
* **Proven**: **Means Restriction (限制自殺途徑)** (e.g., MTR screen doors [月台幕門], restricted charcoal sales), school gatekeeper training, responsible media reporting (**Papageno Effect [帕帕基諾效應]** vs. **Werther Effect [維特效應]**).
* **Ineffective**: Unstructured immediate post-trauma debriefing (risks secondary traumatization), passive flyer distribution.

### GMH Limitations
* **Medicalization of Social Suffering**: Risk of framing poverty/trauma purely as neurochemical imbalances.
* **Category Fallacy (類別謬誤)**: Exporting Western diagnoses (DSM/ICD) to cultures using somatic idioms (e.g., "shenjing shuairuo" [神經衰弱]).
* **Task-Shifting Barriers**: Worker burnout, lack of supervision, psychotropic supply chain issues.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols
### HK HA Psychiatric Triage Protocol
* **Priority 1 (Urgent, 緊急)**: Wait $\le$ 1-2 weeks (high suicide intent, violent acute psychosis, severe postpartum psychosis).
* **Priority 2 (Early, 半緊急)**: Wait $\le$ 3-4 weeks (moderately severe symptoms, active psychosis w/o harm commands).
* **Priority 3 (Routine, 穩定)**: Wait months to >1 year (mild/moderate CMD, stable insomnia).

### Personalised Care Programme (PCP)
* Multidisciplinary case management for SMI (Schizophrenia, Bipolar) in the community.
* High-risk cases (ratio ~1:20-25) receive frequent home visits, crisis de-escalation; integrates with CPNS (社區精神科護理服務).

### Student Suicide Interception (Three-Tier)
* **Tier 1 (School)**: Teachers/personnel provide early support.
* **Tier 2 (Community)**: Principal refers to SWD off-site teams/NGOs.
* **Tier 3 (Specialist)**: Fast-track referral to HA child & adolescent psychiatry.

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks
### HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap. 136, 《精神健康條例》)
* Defines **Mental Disorder (精神紊亂)**: Mental illness (精神病), mental impairment (精神弱化), psychopathic disorder (精神病態性障礙), or any other disorder/disability of mind.

### Statutory Detention Forms
| Section | Purpose | Duration | Signatories | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sec 31 (Form 7/8)** | **Observation/Assessment (觀察/評估)** | Up to 7 days (ext. to 21 via Form 9) | Applicant (Form 7); 1 Judge/Magistrate + 1 Medical Rec (Form 8) | For health, safety, or protection of others. |
| **Sec 32 (Form 10)** | **Treatment (治療)** | Up to 12 months (renewable) | 1 Judge/Magistrate + 2 Medical Recs (1 by approved specialist) | Disorder requires inpatient tx; non-detained care unviable. |
| **Sec 71B (Form 1/2)** | **Voluntary Patient (自願病人)** holding | Up to 72 hours | Form 1 (MO i/c); Form 2 (Medical Superintendent) | Emergency hold for deteriorating voluntary pt attempting self-discharge. |

### Emergency Interception
* **Sec 35B (Police)**: HKPF can apprehend mentally disordered persons wandering publicly and posing danger → take to place of safety (AED).
* **Sec 35A (AED)**: Authorizes 24hr detention at AED for urgent medical assessment.

### Guardianship Board (監護委員會) (Part IVB)
* Appoints legal guardians for adults lacking capacity for personal, residential, or medical decisions.
* Guardian can specify residence (e.g., RCHD) and give proxy medical consent.

## 8. Special Populations
### Perinatal Mental Health
* Universal EPDS screening at MCHCs (6-8 wks).
* Fast-track triage for **Postpartum Psychosis (產後精神病)** (extreme risk of infanticide/suicide).
* Breastfeeding: Prefer Sertraline (舍曲林) due to low milk-plasma ratio.

### Dual Diagnosis
* High prevalence of **Ketamine (氯胺酮)** (ulcerative cystitis, dissociation), **Methamphetamine (冰毒)** (paranoid hallucinatory psychosis), and **Cocaine (可卡因)**.
* Liaison between **Substance Abuse Clinics (SAC, 物質濫用診所)** and NGOs.

### Elderly
* Rapidly aging population. High rates of Major Neurocognitive Disorders (Dementia, 認知障礙症) and **BPSD (認知障礙症行為及心理症狀)** (wandering, aggression) driving emergency admissions.
* Overwhelmed psychogeriatric outreach to RCHEs (安老院舍).

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges
### Funding & Parity
* Disproportionately low funding relative to DALY burden (mental health parity gap).
* Public psychiatry remains centralized; primary care integration is evolving.

### Community Coercion
* **Leverage/Informal Coercion (非正式強制)**: Withholding housing/benefits to enforce LAI antipsychotic adherence.
* **Lack of CTOs**: HK lacks a statutory Community Treatment Order; relies on PCP and conditional discharge (Sec 42B) for restricted patients.

### Cultural Barriers
* **Face (面子)**: Concealment ("hidden cases" [隱蔽個案]) until crisis.
* **Filial Piety (孝道)**: Extreme caregiver burden delaying professional care.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls
* Viva Question: What is the most prevalent mental disorder in the HKMMS? Answer: Mixed Anxiety and Depressive Disorder (MADD) at 6.8%, then GAD at 4.2%.
* Viva Question: What are the criteria and duration for Section 31 (Cap 136)? Answer: Detention for observation of mental disorder warranting hospital care for health/safety/protection; up to 7 days, extendable to 21 days by a judge.
* Viva Question: What is the most evidence-based population suicide intervention? Answer: Means restriction (e.g., MTR screen doors, charcoal regulations).
* Viva Question: How does prolonged DUP affect schizophrenia? Answer: Poorer tx response, lower remission, worse cognitive/functional deficits, higher relapse.
* Trap: Assuming suicide rates are highest in youth. Pearl: Highest rates are in the elderly ($\ge 65$), especially isolated men with chronic illness.
* Trap: Thinking a single psychiatrist can sign a Section 32 treatment order. Pearl: Section 32 requires two medical recommendations (one specialist) AND authorization by a District Judge/Magistrate.
* Trap: Believing task-shifting replaces psychiatrists entirely. Pearl: Non-specialists deliver front-line/low-intensity tx; specialists handle complex cases and clinical governance.
* Pitfall: Recommending immediate generic psychological debriefing post-disaster. Routine debriefing is ineffective and may worsen PTSD; prioritize basic safety and stepped-care screening.
* Pitfall: Confusing ICCMWs with HA SOPCs. ICCMWs are NGO-run social rehabilitation centers; HA provides medical/psychiatric treatment and Cap 136 assessments.
