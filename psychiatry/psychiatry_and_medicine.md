# Psychiatry & Medicine (精神病學與醫學)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong

### Definition of Consultation-Liaison Psychiatry (會診聯絡精神病學)
* **Consultation-Liaison (C-L) Psychiatry (會診聯絡精神病學)**: Subspecialty focusing on psychiatric morbidity in general medical/surgical settings.
* **Core Mandate**: Bridges gap between physical & psychological care → addresses complex mind-body interactions.
* **Consultation (會診)**: Expert psychiatric assessment/advice upon request by primary medical team.
* **Liaison (聯絡)**: Ongoing collaborative integration with medical teams → proactive education & systemic support.

### Scope & Clinical Relevance in Hospital Authority (HA) 
* **High Prevalence**: ~30-40% of general hospital inpatients have comorbid psychiatric conditions.
* **Morbidity & Mortality (發病率與死亡率)**: Untreated psychiatric comorbidities ↑ length of stay (LOS), ↑ readmission rates, ↑ overall HA burden.
* **Local HA Service Model (醫院管理局服務模式)**: C-L teams (psychiatrists + specialized CPNs) deployed across all acute regional hospitals (e.g., QMH, PWH).
* **Common C-L Triggers**: Delirium (譫妄), deliberate self-harm (DSH/蓄意傷害自己), unexplained somatic symptoms, capacity assessments.

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms

### The Biopsychosocial Interface (生物心理社會介面)
* **Biological (生物)**: Direct physiological effects of medical illness on brain (e.g., neuroinflammation, metabolic derangement) → structural/functional psychiatric manifestations.
* **Psychological (心理)**: Maladaptive coping, personality structure, past trauma → shapes illness behavior & perception of physical symptoms.
* **Social (社會)**: Stigma, familial dynamic shifts, financial stress from chronic illness → exacerbates psychiatric vulnerability.

### Mechanisms of Psychiatric-Medical Comorbidity
* **Direct Causation (直接致病)**: Medical illness directly causes psychiatric syndrome (e.g., hyperthyroidism → anxiety).
* **Psychological Reaction (心理反應)**: Adjustment disorder 2° to severe medical diagnosis (e.g., malignancy).
* **Somatic Presentation (軀體化表現)**: Primary psychiatric illness presenting with physical symptoms (e.g., depression presenting as chronic pain).
* **latrogenic (醫源性)**: Psychiatric symptoms 2° to medical treatments (e.g., steroid-induced psychosis/類固醇引發的精神病).

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components

### Classification of Somatic Symptom & Related Disorders (軀體症狀及相關障礙)
| Disorder (English) | 障礙 (Chinese) | Core Clinical Feature (DSM-5) | Insight / Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Somatic Symptom Disorder** | 軀體症狀障礙 | ≥1 distressing somatic symptom + excessive thoughts/behaviors | Unconscious |
| **Illness Anxiety Disorder** | 疾病焦慮障礙 | High health anxiety; minimal/no somatic symptoms | Unconscious |
| **Conversion Disorder** | 轉化症 | Altered voluntary motor/sensory function incompatible with neuro dz | Unconscious |
| **Factitious Disorder** | 做作性障礙 | Falsification of signs/symptoms to assume sick role | Conscious deception (internal motive) |
| **Malingering** (V-code) | 詐病 | Falsification of signs/symptoms for secondary gain (e.g., money) | Conscious deception (external motive) |

### Framework for Organic vs. Functional Illness
* **Organic (器質性)**: Identifiable structural, metabolic, or toxic etiology.
* **Functional (功能性)**: No identifiable structural cause; relates to physiological dysfunction or psychogenic origins.
* **Diagnostic Shift**: Modern C-L psychiatry moves away from strict organic/functional dichotomy → embraces integrated neurobiological models.

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools

### High-Yield Indications for C-L Referral
* **Altered Mental Status**: Delirium screening & management.
* **Risk Assessment**: Post-DSH evaluation, acute suicidality on medical wards.
* **Diagnostic Diagnostic**: Unexplained medical symptoms (MUS).
* **Treatment Refusal**: Capacity assessments for life-saving interventions.
* **Behavioral Disturbance**: Agitation in dementia (BPSD), severe substance withdrawal.

### Standardized Screening & Assessment Tools
* **Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE/簡短智能測驗)**: Baseline cognitive screening (Score <24 suggests impairment; education-adjusted).
* **Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA/蒙特利爾認知評估)**: Superior for mild cognitive impairment & executive dysfunction.
* **Confusion Assessment Method (CAM/譫妄評估方法)**: Diagnostic tool for Delirium (requires acute onset/fluctuating course + inattention + disorganized thinking OR altered level of consciousness).
* **Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS/醫院焦慮抑鬱量表)**: Validated for medically ill patients (excludes somatic symptoms of depression).

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations

### Efficacy of C-L Interventions
* **Clinical Outcomes**: Early C-L intervention ↓ psychological distress, ↑ adherence to medical regimes.
* **Economic Outcomes**: Significant ↓ in LOS & overall healthcare utilization.
* **Proactive vs. Reactive Liaison**: Proactive screening (e.g., pre-op psych evaluation) yields superior outcomes compared to reactive post-crisis consultation.

### Limitations of C-L Psychiatry
* **Time Constraints**: Acute medical wards prioritize rapid discharge → insufficient time for complex psychodynamic interventions.
* **Stigma (標籤效應)**: Patients often reject psychiatric input, perceiving it as medical dismissal ("it's all in my head").
* **Diagnostic Overshadowing (診斷掩蔽)**: Medical teams misattributing genuine physical symptoms to known psychiatric history.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols

### The C-L Assessment Protocol in HA
* **Step 1: Chart Review (翻查病歷)**: Verify medical status, recent investigations, meds, and primary team's specific referral question.
* **Step 2: Stakeholder Interview**: Collateral history from ward nurses & primary medical officers (MOs).
* **Step 3: Patient Assessment**: Focused bedside psychiatric history & Mental State Examination (MSE/精神狀態檢查).
* **Step 4: Physical Review**: Brief targeted neuro/physical exam (if primary team omitted/missed psych-relevant signs).
* **Step 5: Formulation & Documentation**: Clear, action-oriented recommendations in CMS (Clinical Management System).

### Local Guidelines for Behavioral Emergencies
* **Agitation Protocol**: Non-pharmacological de-escalation first.
* **Chemical Restraint (化學約束)**: Low-dose haloperidol (氟哌啶醇) (0.5-1mg PO/IM) OR Lorazepam (勞拉西泮) (0.5-1mg PO/IM) per HA guidelines. Avoid benzos in delirium unless alcohol/sedative withdrawal.

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks

### Mental Capacity Assessment (精神行為能力評估)
* **Criteria for Capacity**: Patient must be able to:
  1. **Understand** information relevant to the decision.
  2. **Retain** that information.
  3. **Weigh** the information to arrive at a choice.
  4. **Communicate** the decision.
* **Time & Decision-Specific**: Capacity is fluid; assessed for the *specific* intervention at the *specific* time.

### Mental Health Ordinance (MHO) Cap 136 (精神健康條例 第136章) in General Hospitals
* **Section 2**: Inapplicable for purely physical treatments. MHO only covers treatment for *mental disorder*.
* **Treating Physical Illness w/o Capacity**: Governed by common law principle of **Necessity (Best Interests)**, NOT the MHO.
* **Section 36**: Allows urgent treatment of mental disorder to save life or prevent severe deterioration.

## 8. Special Populations

### The Elderly Medical Inpatient (長者病人)
* **Polypharmacy (多重用藥)**: High risk of iatrogenic delirium & anticholinergic burden.
* **Dementia Comorbidity**: Frequent BPSD (Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia) exacerbated by unfamiliar ward environment.
* **Frailty Syndrome**: Altered pharmacokinetics → requires "start low, go slow" psychotropic dosing.

### Perinatal Populations (圍產期)
* **C-L on O&G Wards**: Screening for postpartum depression (PPD/產後抑鬱) & postpartum psychosis (PPP/產後精神病).
* **Risk/Benefit Analysis**: Psychotropic use during pregnancy/lactation requires balancing teratogenic risks vs. risks of untreated severe maternal mental illness.

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges

### Iatrogenic Psychiatric Syndromes
* **Steroid-induced Psychosis (類固醇引發的精神病)**: Risk correlates with dose (>40mg/day prednisone equivalent). Presents with mania/psychosis.
* **Interferon-induced Depression**: Seen in HCV/oncology treatments.
* **Anticholinergic Delirium**: Triggered by antihistamines, TCAs, antispasmodics.

### Ethical Dilemmas (倫理困境)
* **End-of-Life (EOL) Decisions**: Distinguishing rational desire to hasten death in terminal illness vs. treatable clinical depression.
* **Covert Medication (隱蔽用藥)**: Administering psychotropics in food/drink to non-capacitous patients → strictly regulated, requires best-interests documentation & multidisciplinary agreement.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you clinically differentiate delirium from dementia at the bedside? Answer: Delirium has an acute onset, fluctuating course, and prominent inattention/altered consciousness; dementia is chronic, progressive, with clear consciousness until late stages.
* Trap: Assuming a patient with schizophrenia who refuses a life-saving amputation lacks capacity. Pearl: Capacity is decision-specific; a patient with schizophrenia can possess full capacity to refuse surgery if their reasoning is not driven by psychotic delusions.
* Pitfall: Prescribing typical antipsychotics (e.g., haloperidol) for delirium in a patient with Parkinson's disease or Lewy Body Dementia. Exacerbates extrapyramidal symptoms and can cause severe rigidity; use low-dose quetiapine or avoid antipsychotics altogether.
* Viva Question: What is the defining difference between Factitious Disorder and Malingering? Answer: Factitious disorder is driven by an internal psychological need to assume the sick role; malingering is driven by external, tangible secondary gain (e.g., financial compensation, avoiding jail).
* Trap: Using the Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) to force a non-compliant psychiatric patient to undergo hemodialysis. Pearl: The MHO strictly covers treatment for mental disorders; physical treatments for non-capacitous patients fall under the common law principle of acting in the patient's best interests (Necessity).
