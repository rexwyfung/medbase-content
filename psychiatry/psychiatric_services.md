# Psychiatric Services (精神科服務)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong
### Definition of Psychiatric Services
* **Psychiatric Services** (**精神科服務**) → A structured, multidisciplinary continuum of care for mental disorders, spanning inpatient, outpatient, and community-based settings.
* Primary goal → Assessment, treatment, rehabilitation, and risk management of mental illness to restore functioning and ↓ disability.

### Scope and Relevance in Hong Kong
* In HK, services are heavily dominated by the public sector via the **Hospital Authority (HA)** (**醫院管理局**), which manages >90% of severe psychiatric cases.
* **HK Service Model** → Highly centralized, characterized by high patient volume, transitioning progressively from an asylum-based model to a **Community Care** (**社區照顧**) model.

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms
### Deinstitutionalization & Community Care
* **Deinstitutionalization** (**去院舍化**) → Shift of care from isolated psychiatric asylums to mainstream hospitals and community integration.
* Core Principle: **Least Restrictive Environment** (**最少限制環境**) → Treat patients with the maximum degree of freedom and autonomy safely possible.

### Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) Mechanism
* **MDT** (**跨專業團隊**) → Collaborative, non-hierarchical approach to holistic biopsychosocial care.
* Core Members:
 * **Psychiatrists** (**精神科醫生**) → Diagnosis, pharmacotherapy, risk assessment, MHO Cap 136 execution.
 * **Community Psychiatric Nurses (CPN)** (**社區精神科護士**) → Symptom monitoring, depot injections, compliance checks, crisis intervention.
 * **Medical Social Workers (MSW)** (**醫務社工**) → Financial aid (CSSA), housing, family support, statutory MHO duties.
 * **Clinical Psychologists (CP)** (**臨床心理學家**) → Psychometric testing, psychotherapy (CBT, etc.).
 * **Occupational Therapists (OT)** (**職業治療師**) → Functional assessment, ADL training, vocational rehabilitation.

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components
### Stepped-Care Tiers of Psychiatric Care Framework
| Tier | Setting | Target Population | Main Interventions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | GOPCs, Private GPs | Mild anxiety, mild depression, insomnia | Psychoeducation, basic SSRIs, brief counseling |
| **Secondary** | **SOPD** (**專科門診**) | Moderate-severe mood disorders, stable psychosis | Specialist MDT care, diagnostic clarification |
| **Tertiary** | Acute Wards, Specialized Units | Acute psychosis, high risk of self-harm/violence | Inpatient admission, ECT, intensive observation |

### Specialized HA Community Services Components
* **EASY (Early Assessment Service for Young People with Early Psychosis)** (**思覺失調服務**) → Targets ages 15-64 with first-episode psychosis → ↓ **DUP** (Duration of Untreated Psychosis).
* **ICT (Intensive Care Team)** (**深切治療小組**) → Rapid community intervention for severe, relapsing patients to ↓ readmissions.
* **CPS (Community Psychiatric Services)** (**社區精神科服務**) → Post-discharge outreach, psychoeducation, and relapse prevention.

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools
### Indications for Triage & Inpatient Admission
* **SOPD Triage** → Urgent (seen in <2 weeks), Semi-urgent (<8 weeks), Routine.
* **Inpatient Admission Indications** →
 * Acute high risk of suicide or severe self-harm.
 * Acute high risk of violence/harm to others (command AH, severe persecutory delusions).
 * Severe self-neglect leading to physical compromise (e.g., severe anorexia nervosa, depressive stupor).
 * Need for rapid medication titration or **ECT** (**腦電盪治療**).

### Standardized Needs & Outcome Assessment Tools
* **Camberwell Assessment of Need (CAN)** (**坎伯韋爾需求評估**) → Rates 22 domains (e.g., accommodation, self-care, psychotic symptoms) as absent, present, or modified. Identifies unmet social/clinical needs.
* **Health of the Nation Outcome Scales (HoNOS)** (**國民健康結果量表**) → 12-item scale rating clinical problems (symptoms) & social functioning. Routinely used to measure progress/efficacy of interventions.

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations
### Efficacy of Community Interventions
* **Assertive Community Treatment (ACT)** (**啟導性社區治療**) → Evidence-based model for **SMI** (Severe Mental Illness).
 * Efficacy: ↓ psychiatric inpatient days, ↑ housing stability, ↑ patient satisfaction.
 * Mechanism: Small caseloads, 24/7 availability, direct provision of services in the community.
* **Early Intervention Services** → Strong evidence for ↓ negative symptoms and ↑ functional recovery in the first 2-3 years post-onset.

### Limitations of Psychiatric Services
* **"Revolving Door" Phenomenon** (**旋轉門效應**) → Frequent readmissions due to medication non-adherence, substance misuse, or poor social support.
* **Resource Constraints** → High case-to-staff ratios in HA SOPDs → brief consultation times, ↑ risk of missing subtle clinical deterioration.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols
### Care Programme Approach (CPA)
* **CPA** (**護理計劃方針**) → Standardized protocol for coordinating care for patients requiring complex, prolonged secondary care (especially schizophrenia, severe bipolar).
* **Key Elements of CPA**:
 * Designation of a **Key Worker / Care Coordinator** (**專責人員**).
 * Comprehensive, documented multidisciplinary needs assessment.
 * Individualized, written care plan (shared with patient).
 * Regular, formal MDT reviews.

### Discharge Planning Protocol
* Formulated upon admission.
* Ensure clear follow-up (SOPD, CPNS).
* **Crisis Plan** → Clear instructions on early warning signs and emergency contacts provided to patient & carers.
* Post-discharge period (first 1-2 weeks) = Highest risk for suicide → mandates early CPNS contact.

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks
### Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) in Psychiatric Services
* **MHO Cap 136** (**《精神健康條例》第136章**) governs psychiatric services for involuntary patients in HK.
* **Conditional Discharge (CD)** (**有條件釋放**) → Applies to patients with a history of criminal violence or high-risk behavior.
 * Conditions typically include: Mandatory SOPD attendance, residing at a specified address, taking prescribed medication.
 * Breach of CD → Immediate recall to hospital.

### Medico-Legal Community Care Mandates
* **Community Treatment Orders (CTO)** (**社區治療令**) → Used in UK/Australia to enforce community treatment (allows recall for non-compliance). *Note: HK does NOT have CTOs; relies on CD for restricted patients.*
* **Guardianship Board** (**監護委員會**) → Appoints legal guardians for financial/welfare decisions for adults lacking mental capacity (e.g., severe intellectual disability, advanced dementia).

## 8. Special Populations (Lifespan, Intellectual Disability, Comorbidities)
### Child & Adolescent Mental Health Services (CAMHS)
* Focus: Neurodevelopmental (ADHD, ASD), emotional disorders, eating disorders.
* Protocol: Strong liaison with schools, educational psychologists, and family therapy.

### Psychogeriatric Services (Old Age Psychiatry)
* Focus: Dementia with BPSD (Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia), late-onset depression/psychosis.
* **CGAT (Community Geriatric Assessment Team)** (**社區老人評估小組**) → Crucial outreach service to **RCHEs** (Residential Care Homes for the Elderly) (**安老院舍**) to ↓ unnecessary acute hospital admissions.

### Intellectual Disability (ID) Services
* Specialized HA teams managing behavioral phenotypes, severe aggression, and comorbid psychiatric illnesses (diagnostic overshadowing risk).

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges
### Systemic Challenges & Service Gaps
* **Stigma & Discrimination** (**標籤效應**) → Leads to delayed presentation, poor employment prospects, and social isolation.
* Caregiver Burden → Lack of respite care and adequate community support centers leads to severe carer burnout.

### Ethical Dilemmas in Community Care
* **Autonomy vs. Beneficence/Paternalism** → Balancing a patient's right to refuse medication in the community against the MDT's duty to prevent relapse and protect society.
* **Informal Coercion / Leverage** (**非正式強制**) → Tying social benefits, housing, or child access to medication adherence. Ethically contested but clinically prevalent to ensure compliance.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls
* Viva Question: What is the primary purpose of the Care Programme Approach (CPA) in psychiatric services? Answer: To provide structured, coordinated multidisciplinary care, designate a single key worker, and ensure regular review for patients with severe and enduring mental illness.
* Trap: Assuming Hong Kong utilizes Community Treatment Orders (CTOs) to force patients to take medication at home. Pearl: HK does not have CTOs; community compulsion relies on Conditional Discharge (CD), which allows recall to hospital for treatment if conditions are breached, but forced medication can only occur within the inpatient setting.
* Pitfall: Discharging an acutely suicidal patient without a clear follow-up and crisis plan. Always ensure an urgent SOPD appointment, early CPNS outreach, and provide direct crisis contact numbers to the family before discharge.
