# Physical Treatments: ECT, TMS & Psychosurgery (物理治療：腦電盪治療、經顱磁刺激及精神外科手術)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong

### Definition of Somatic Therapies
* **Core Concept**: Non-pharmacological, device-based or surgical interventions directly altering neurobiology
* **Target**: Severe, treatment-resistant, or acutely life-threatening psychiatric disorders

### Local Relevance within Hong Kong Hospital Authority (HKHA)
* **Electroconvulsive Therapy [ECT] (腦電盪治療)**: 
  * Mainstay for acute psychiatric emergencies
  * Available in all major HKHA psychiatric units
* **Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation [TMS] (經顱磁刺激)**: 
  * Emerging outpatient modality
  * Primarily utilized in private practice and specific HKHA research/tertiary clinics
* **Psychosurgery (精神外科手術)**: 
  * Historically utilized (e.g., lobotomy)
  * Currently virtually obsolete in HK 
  * Replaced globally by reversible neuromodulation (e.g., Deep Brain Stimulation [DBS])

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms

### Neurobiological Mechanism of ECT
* **Seizure Induction**: Electrical current intentionally provokes generalized tonic-clonic cerebral seizure
* **Neurotransmitter Surge**: Massive acute release of DA, 5-HT, and NE
* **Receptor Modulation**: Post-ictal downregulation of β-adrenergic receptors
* **Neurogenesis**: ↑ Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) → hippocampal neuroplasticity

### Physical Mechanism of TMS
* **Electromagnetic Induction**: Pulsed magnetic fields pass through scalp/skull without resistance
* **Focal Depolarization**: Induces highly localized secondary electrical currents in targeted superficial cortex (e.g., Left Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex [DLPFC])
* **Neuroplasticity**: Modulates cortical excitability without triggering generalized seizure

### Mechanism of Psychosurgery & DBS
* **Ablative Surgery**: Permanent stereotactic destruction of hyperactive limbic-frontal circuits
* **DBS**: Reversible, high-frequency electrical pulses delivered via implanted electrodes → focal inhibition of abnormal target nuclei

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components

### Modality Comparison Table
| Modality (療法) | Anatomical Target | Seizure Required? | Anesthesia Required? | Reversibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bitemporal ECT** | Bilateral frontotemporal cortex | Yes | Yes (General) | Fully reversible |
| **Unilateral ECT** | Right non-dominant hemisphere | Yes | Yes (General) | Fully reversible |
| **rTMS** | Left/Right DLPFC | No | No (Awake) | Fully reversible |
| **Ablative Surgery** | Anterior cingulate/capsule | No | Yes (General) | Irreversible |
| **DBS** | Subcallosal cingulate (e.g., CG25) | No | Yes (General) | Reversible |

### ECT Pulse Waveforms
* **Brief Pulse**: Standard standard 0.5-1.5 ms width → balances efficacy and cognitive side effects
* **Ultra-Brief Pulse**: <0.3 ms width → ↓ cognitive impairment → requires longer seizure duration for efficacy

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools

### First-Line Indications for ECT
* **High Suicide Risk** (高自殺風險)
* **Catatonia** (緊張症)
* **Refusal of Food/Fluids** (拒絕飲食致生命危險)
* **Severe Psychotic Depression** (伴隨精神病徵狀的重度抑鬱)
* **Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia** (難治性精神分裂症)

### Indications for rTMS
* **Treatment-Resistant Major Depressive Disorder** (難治性抑鬱症)
  * Failed ≥1 adequate antidepressant trial
* **Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder [OCD]** (強迫症)

### Pre-ECT Standardized Assessments
* **Medical Clearance**: 
  * ECG (心電圖)
  * Chest X-ray (胸部X光)
  * Blood tests: CBC, U&E, LFT, TFT
* **Anesthetic Assessment**: ASA grading
* **Cognitive Baseline**: MMSE / MoCA

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations

### Efficacy Profile of ECT
* **Remission Rates**: 70-80% for severe MDD (superior to all pharmacotherapy)
* **Onset**: Rapid (improvements often seen post 2-3 sessions)
* **Major Limitation**: High relapse rate (up to 50% at 6 months) without maintenance pharmacotherapy or maintenance ECT (mECT)

### Efficacy Profile of rTMS
* **Remission Rates**: 30-40% for treatment-resistant MDD
* **Advantage**: Zero systemic cognitive impairment
* **Limitations**: 
  * Depth of penetration limited to ~2 cm
  * High logistical burden (daily sessions)
  * Out-of-pocket costs high in HK private sector

### Evidence for Psychosurgery
* **Efficacy**: 30-50% response in ultra-refractory OCD (capsulotomy)
* **Limitation**: Lack of large-scale RCTs → relegated to strict last-resort status globally

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols

### HKHA ECT Administration Protocol
* **Preparation**: Nil by mouth (NPO) x 6 hours
* **Team Required**: 
  * Psychiatrist (administers stimulus)
  * Anesthetist (manages airway/drugs)
  * Psychiatric Nurse (monitoring)
* **Pharmacotherapy Sequence**:
  * Anticholinergic (Atropine) → ↓ secretions / ↓ vagal bradycardia
  * Short-acting IV Anesthetic (Propofol / Methohexital) → Unconsciousness
  * Muscle Relaxant (Succinylcholine) → Prevents musculoskeletal trauma
* **Stimulus Delivery**: Bilateral electrodes applied → stimulus dosed via seizure threshold titration
* **Monitoring**: 
  * EEG → confirms cerebral seizure (target >25 seconds)
  * Visual monitoring of isolated limb (tourniquet technique) → confirms motor seizure
* **Course**: 2-3 sessions/week → Total 6-12 sessions

### rTMS Execution Protocol
* **Setup**: Outpatient setting → patient fully awake
* **Mapping**: Motor threshold (MT) determined via stimulation of primary motor cortex observing thumb twitch
* **Treatment**: Coil positioned over DLPFC → 20-30 min session → patient drives home immediately post-session

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks (e.g., Cap 136)

### Consent Framework for ECT (HK Mental Health Ordinance Cap 136 Part IVC)
| Patient Status | Capacity | Legal Requirement for ECT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Voluntary** | Capable | Standard written informed consent |
| **Voluntary** | Incapable | Relatives CANNOT consent. Must use common law (Best Interests) or specific HA forms |
| **Involuntary (e.g., Sec 3)** | Capable & Consenting | Standard written consent |
| **Involuntary (e.g., Sec 3)** | Incapable OR Refusing | Requires **SOAD** (Second Opinion Appointed Doctor) via MHO Form |
| **Emergency (Life-saving)** | Any | Urgent treatment permitted immediately (Documented necessity) |

### Legal Framework for Psychosurgery
* **Strict Governance**: Regulated under MHO Cap 136 Section 59A
* **Mandatory Panel**: Requires approval from SOAD **plus** two other non-medical persons appointed by the Hospital Authority
* **Absolute Rule**: Patient MUST be capable of giving valid consent → involuntary psychosurgery is strictly prohibited

## 8. Special Populations (Lifespan, Intellectual Disability, Comorbidities)

### Pregnancy (孕婦)
* **ECT**: Highly safe & preferred over many psychotropics for acute severe maternal illness
* **Modifications**: Fetal monitoring required → elevation of right hip to prevent aortocaval compression

### Elderly (長者)
* **ECT**: First-line for severe psychotic depression or catatonia 
* **Advantage**: Avoids polypharmacy / severe anticholinergic toxicity of TCAs
* **Risk**: ↑ Risk of prolonged post-ictal confusion

### Neurological Comorbidities
* **Absolute Contraindication to ECT**: Raised Intracranial Pressure (ICP) (e.g., space-occupying lesion) → risk of brain herniation due to transient ↑ cerebral blood flow during seizure
* **Relative Contraindications**: 
  * Recent Myocardial Infarction (<3 months)
  * Recent CVA / Aneurysm
  * Retinal detachment

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges

### ECT Adverse Effects Profile
* **Acute (Post-Ictal)**:
  * Headache & jaw ache
  * Myalgia (due to succinylcholine fasciculations)
  * Nausea
  * Transient arrhythmias / hypertension
* **Cognitive**:
  * Anterograde amnesia (resolves weeks post-treatment)
  * Retrograde amnesia (spotty memory loss for events immediately preceding treatment; can occasionally be permanent)

### TMS Adverse Effects Profile
* **Common**: Scalp discomfort / local pain at coil site
* **Neurological**: Transient tension-type headache
* **Severe**: Seizure induction (extremely rare, <0.1%)

### Systemic & Ethical Challenges
* **Historical Stigma**: Media portrayal creates immense public fear and treatment refusal
* **Coercion Concerns**: Use of ECT via SOAD overriding patient refusal raises intense bioethical conflict between *Beneficence* (saving life) and *Autonomy* (bodily integrity)
* **Resource Allocation**: Lack of widespread public HKHA funding for rTMS restricts access to affluent patients

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the only absolute contraindication for ECT? Answer: Raised intracranial pressure.
* Trap: Assuming ECT is contraindicated in pregnancy due to risks to the fetus. Pearl: ECT is considered safe in all trimesters and is often a first-line treatment for highly suicidal or psychotic pregnant women.
* Pitfall: Forgetting to administer the muscle relaxant (succinylcholine) before the electrical stimulus during ECT. This leads to massive, unprotected tonic-clonic muscle contractions causing severe musculoskeletal injuries and bone fractures.
* Viva Question: How do you confirm an adequate seizure during ECT if the patient is fully paralyzed by succinylcholine? Answer: By inflating a BP cuff on one limb above systolic pressure before injecting the muscle relaxant, isolating that limb to visually observe the motor seizure, alongside continuous EEG monitoring.
