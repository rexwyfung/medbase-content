# Misuse of Alcohol (酒精濫用)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Clinical Definition of Alcohol Misuse (酒精濫用臨床定義)
* **Definition**: Maladaptive pattern of alcohol consumption → clinically significant impairment/distress, categorized by dependence vs. harmful use as detailed in the Shorted Oxford Psychiatry Textbook.pdf.
* **Harmful Use (有害使用)**: Pattern of drinking → physical/psychological damage (e.g., liver disease, depressive episodes) independent of dependence.
* **Alcohol Dependence (酒精依賴)**: Cluster of behavioral, cognitive, physiological phenomena developing after repeated use (compulsion, tolerance, withdrawal).

### Biopsychosocial Etiologies (生理心理社會病因)
* **Biological (生理)**: ↑ Genetic heritability (40-60%); specific variants (e.g., ALDH2 deficiency common in Asian/HK populations → flushing, ↓ dependence risk).
* **Psychological (心理)**: +ve reinforcement (euphoria) & -ve reinforcement (anxiety relief); comorbid personality traits (impulsivity, antisocial traits).
* **Social (社會)**: Peer pressure, occupational stress, cultural acceptance, easy accessibility (e.g., HK 24/7 convenience stores), lack of alcohol tax (HK abolished wine/beer duty in 2008).

### Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong (香港本地流行病學)
* **Prevalence**: ~1.5-2% lifetime prevalence for alcohol dependence in HK.
* **Demographics**: ↑ in males, peak age 20-40, increasing trend among young females.
* **HK Hospital Authority (HA) Burden**: Significant cause of acute A&E attendances for trauma, acute pancreatitis, and psychiatric dual-diagnoses.

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Neurobiology & Neurotransmitter Pathways (神經生物學與神經傳導途徑)
* **GABAergic System (GABA系統)**: Acute alcohol ↑ GABA-A receptor activity (inhibitory) → sedation, anxiolysis. Chronic use → ↓ GABA receptor sensitivity (downregulation).
* **Glutamatergic System (谷氨酸系統)**: Acute alcohol ↓ NMDA receptor activity (excitatory). Chronic use → ↑ NMDA receptors (upregulation).
* **Withdrawal (戒斷)**: Abrupt cessation → GABA/Glutamate imbalance (↓ inhibition, ↑ excitation) → hyperactivity, seizures, Delirium Tremens (震顫性譫妄).
* **Dopaminergic Mesolimbic Pathway (多巴胺中腦邊緣途徑)**: Alcohol stimulates Ventral Tegmental Area (VTA) → ↑ Dopamine (DA) in Nucleus Accumbens (NAc) → reward/craving.

### Psychopathology of Addiction (成癮心理病理學)
* **Craving (渴求)**: Intense, overwhelming desire to consume alcohol triggered by environmental cues.
* **Salience (顯著性)**: Alcohol-seeking behavior overrides previously prioritized activities/responsibilities.
* **Loss of Control (失控)**: Inability to limit consumption despite awareness of harmful consequences.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Psychiatric History for Alcohol Misuse (酒精濫用精神病史)
* **Drinking Profile**: Exact quantity/frequency (units/week), duration, type of alcohol, pattern (binge vs. continuous).
* **Dependence Features**: Tolerance (↑ doses needed), withdrawal symptoms (morning shakes, sweats), relief drinking (eye-openers).
* **Impact & Consequences**: Occupational decline, relationship breakdowns, legal issues (DUI), history of falls/trauma.
* **Past Treatment**: Previous detox attempts, rehabilitation, longest period of abstinence, triggers for relapse.

### Mental State Examination Findings (精神狀態檢查結果)
* **Appearance & Behavior**: Disheveled, poor hygiene, psychomotor agitation (if in withdrawal), flushed facies, tremors, prominent odor of alcohol.
* **Speech**: Slurred (intoxicated) vs. rapid/anxious (withdrawal).
* **Mood & Affect**: Labile, frequently irritable, underlying depressed mood or anxiety.
* **Thought Content**: Denial or minimization of drinking severity, guilt, suicidal ideation (↑ risk).
* **Perception**: Alcoholic hallucinosis (auditory/visual hallucinations with clear sensorium) or illusions (e.g., in Delirium Tremens).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Psychiatric Differential Diagnoses (精神科鑑別診斷)
* **Primary Depressive Disorder (原發性抑鬱症)**: Assess chronology (did low mood precede alcohol misuse, or is it alcohol-induced?).
* **Anxiety Disorders (焦慮症)**: Alcohol used as self-medication (e.g., Social Anxiety Disorder).
* **Bipolar Disorder (雙相情緒障礙症)**: Binge drinking secondary to manic disinhibition/impulsivity.
* **Personality Disorders (人格障礙)**: Antisocial (ASPD) or Borderline (BPD) featuring impulsivity and substance misuse.

### Organic & Medical Rule-Outs (器質性與內科排除診斷)
* **Hepatic Encephalopathy (肝性腦病)**: Confusion, asterixis, ↑ ammonia secondary to alcoholic cirrhosis; mimics psychiatric delirium.
* **Wernicke's Encephalopathy (韋尼克氏腦病)**: Confusion, ataxia, ophthalmoplegia due to Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) deficiency.
* **Head Trauma/Subdural Hematoma (頭部創傷/硬腦膜下血腫)**: Common in falls during intoxication; presents with altered mental status, fluctuating consciousness.

### Clinical Rating Scales (臨床評估量表)
* **CAGE Questionnaire (CAGE問卷)**: 4 items (Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye-opener). Score ≥2 indicates high suspicion.
* **FAST Questionnaire (FAST問卷)**: 4 items screening for hazardous drinking.
* **CIWA-Ar (臨床酒精戒斷評估量表)**: 10-item scale measuring withdrawal severity (guides benzodiazepine dosing).

### Organic Rule-Out Investigations (器質性排除檢查)
* **Bloods**: ↑ MCV, ↑ GGT, ↑ AST/ALT (AST:ALT ratio > 2:1), ↓ platelets, ↓ Mg, ↓ K, abnormal clotting (↑ PT/INR).
* **Imaging**: CT Brain (rule out subdural hematoma/atrophy), USG Abdomen (fatty liver/cirrhosis).
* **Tox Screen**: Urine/blood toxicology for comorbid substance misuse.

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Acute Intoxication & Withdrawal Management (急性中毒與戒斷治療)
* **Airway/Breathing/Circulation (ABC)**: Triage, prevent aspiration in severe intoxication.
* **Thiamine Replacement (硫胺素補充)**: Pabrinex (High-dose IV/IM Thiamine) BEFORE glucose → prevents Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome.
* **Pharmacological Detoxification**: Symptom-triggered Benzodiazepines (e.g., Diazepam, Chlordiazepoxide) via CIWA-Ar protocol; Lorazepam preferred in severe liver failure.
* **Seizure Management**: IV Lorazepam/Diazepam for acute withdrawal seizures.

### Risk Assessment (風險評估)
* **Risk to Self**: High risk of accidental trauma (falls, RTAs), suicidal ideation (↑ impulsivity), self-neglect.
* **Risk to Others**: Domestic violence, aggressive behavior during intoxication.
* **Medical Risk**: Delirium Tremens (medical emergency, 15-20% mortality if untreated), aspiration pneumonia.

### Medico-Legal Considerations via HK Cap 136 (香港第136章精神健康條例醫療法律考量)
* **Sectioning**: Alcohol dependence *alone* is generally explicitly excluded from grounds for compulsory admission under typical mental health legislation.
* **Cap 136 Application**: Can be applied if acute intoxication/withdrawal → secondary psychotic symptoms, delirium, or suicidal behavior that constitutes a "mental disorder" warranting detention for health/safety/protection of others.

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Pharmacotherapy for Relapse Prevention (預防復發藥物治療)
| Medication (藥物) | Mechanism of Action (作用機制) | Clinical Application & Side Effects (臨床應用與副作用) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acamprosate (阿坎酸)** | NMDA receptor antagonist / GABA agonist | ↓ Cravings. Safe in liver disease. Renal excretion (adjust in CKD). SE: Diarrhea. |
| **Naltrexone (納曲酮)** | Mu-opioid receptor antagonist | ↓ Reward/euphoria of drinking. Contraindicated in acute opioid use/liver failure. SE: Nausea, hepatotoxicity. |
| **Disulfiram (雙硫侖)** | ALDH inhibitor → ↑ Acetaldehyde | Aversive therapy. Ingestion → flushing, nausea, tachycardia, palpitations. Requires highly motivated patient. |

### Psychotherapy & Psychosocial Interventions (心理治療與社會心理介入)
* **Motivational Interviewing (動機式訪談法)**: Resolve ambivalence, progress through Stages of Change (Prochaska & DiClemente).
* **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) (認知行為治療)**: Identify triggers, develop coping strategies, relapse prevention planning.
* **Social Support (社會支援)**: Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) (12-step mutual help groups), family therapy, supported employment/housing via HA Medical Social Workers (MSW).

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Course of Illness (病程)
* **Relapsing-Remitting**: Chronic trajectory with periods of abstinence punctuated by relapses.
* **Progression**: Early hazardous use → tolerance → physical dependence → end-organ damage.

### Prognostic Factors (預後因素)
* **Good Prognosis (+ve)**: Strong social support, stable employment, absence of ASPD, older age of onset, high motivation to change, intact cognitive function.
* **Poor Prognosis (-ve)**: Early onset (<20 years), comorbid psychiatric disorders (dual diagnosis), polysubstance abuse, severe liver disease/cognitive impairment, homeless/poor social network.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Psychiatric & Neurological Complications (精神與神經併發症)
* **Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome (韋尼克-高沙可夫症候群)**: Acute Wernicke's (ataxia, confusion, ophthalmoplegia) → chronic Korsakoff's (anterograde amnesia, confabulation) due to mammillary body damage.
* **Alcoholic Dementia/Neurocognitive Disorder (酒精性失智症)**: Global cognitive decline, cerebral atrophy.
* **Alcoholic Hallucinosis (酒精性幻覺症)**: Auditory hallucinations without autonomic instability.

### Medical & Social Complications (內科與社會併發症)
* **Medical**: Liver cirrhosis, acute/chronic pancreatitis, cardiomyopathy, ↑ risk of GI cancers (esophageal, hepatocellular), peripheral neuropathy.
* **Social**: Job loss, marital breakdown, domestic abuse, financial ruin, criminality (DUI).

### Pharmacotherapy Side Effects (藥物副作用)
* **Benzodiazepines (Detox)**: Sedation, respiratory depression (especially if combined with residual alcohol), paradoxical disinhibition.
* **Disulfiram**: Severe Disulfiram-Ethanol Reaction (DER) can be fatal (arrhythmias, cardiovascular collapse).

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* **Viva Question**: What is the mechanism of Wernicke's Encephalopathy? Answer: Acute Thiamine (Vitamin B1) deficiency leading to petechial hemorrhages in the mammillary bodies, thalamus, and periaqueductal gray matter.
* **Viva Question**: How does Delirium Tremens present? Answer: Onset 48-72 hours post-cessation, featuring severe agitation, visual/tactile hallucinations (formication), coarse tremor, and profound autonomic instability (tachycardia, hypertension, diaphoresis).
* **Trap**: Prescribing glucose IV before Thiamine in a malnourished alcoholic. Pearl: Always administer Pabrinex (Thiamine) BEFORE glucose; glucose metabolism rapidly depletes residual thiamine stores, precipitating acute Wernicke's Encephalopathy.
* **Pitfall**: Using Disulfiram in a patient with poor insight or cognitive impairment. Avoid by ensuring the patient fully comprehends the lethal risks of the Disulfiram-Ethanol reaction and has intact cognitive capacity.
* **Pitfall**: Attempting rapid outpatient detoxification for a patient with a history of Delirium Tremens or withdrawal seizures. Always admit for inpatient, medically supervised detoxification using a standardized CIWA-Ar protocol.
