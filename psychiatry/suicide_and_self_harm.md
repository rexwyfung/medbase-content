# Suicide & Self-Harm (自殺與蓄意自殘)

## 1. Topic Definition, Scope & Relevance in Hong Kong

### Definition of Suicidal Behaviors
* **Suicide (自殺):** Fatal self-injurious act with explicit/inferred intent to die.
* **Deliberate Self-Harm (DSH) (蓄意自殘):** Intentional self-poisoning or self-injury irrespective of suicidal intent.
* **Non-Suicidal Self-Injury (NSSI) (非自殺性自殘):** Direct, deliberate destruction of body tissue without lethal intent (e.g., cutting) → often for emotional regulation.

### Local Epidemiology & Scope in HK (香港流行病學)
* **Overall Rate:** ~12-14 per 100,000 population.
* **Peak Demographics:** ↑ rates in elderly males (長者男性); concerning ↑ trends in youth/students.
* **Common Lethal Methods (HK context):** Jumping from height (高處墮下) > Charcoal burning (燒炭) > Hanging (縊首).
* **Parasuicide/DSH Methods:** Psychotropic/paracetamol overdose (藥物過量), wrist-cutting (割腕).

## 2. Core Theoretical Principles & Mechanisms (Psychological, Pharmacological, or Legal)

### Neurobiological Mechanisms
* ↓ **Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) 5-HIAA** → correlates with ↑ impulsivity, aggression, and violent suicidal acts.
* **HPA Axis Dysregulation** → ↑ cortisol, hyperactive stress response.
* **Genetics:** +ve family Hx of suicide ↑ risk independent of primary psychiatric Dx.

### Psychological & Cognitive Theories
* **Beck’s Cognitive Triad:** **Hopelessness (絕望)** → strongest cognitive predictor of completed suicide (stronger than depression severity).
* **Joiner’s Interpersonal-Psychological Theory:**
  * Thwarted belongingness (lack of connection).
  * Perceived burdensomeness (feeling like a liability).
  * Acquired capability for suicide (habituation to pain/fear via prior DSH).

## 3. Key Classifications, Frameworks & Components

### Classification of Suicidal Intent (自殺意圖分類)
* **High Intent:** Meticulous planning, precautions against discovery, final acts (writing a will, sorting finances), highly lethal method, no expectation of rescue.
* **Low Intent:** Impulsive act, carried out in presence of others, immediate reporting to seek help, secondary gain/cry for help.

### Typology of DSH Motivations
* **Affect Regulation:** Relief from intolerable tension/numbness.
* **Interpersonal Influence:** Coercion, communicating distress.
* **Self-Punishment:** Driven by guilt/worthlessness (common in psychotic depression).

## 4. Clinical Indications & Standardized Assessment Tools

### Comprehensive Risk Assessment Framework (風險評估)
* **Ideation (意念):** Active vs. passive. Frequency, intensity.
* **Plan (計劃):** Formulation, timing, lethality.
* **Means (工具):** Access to charcoal, high-rise balconies, hoarded medications.
* **Protective Factors (保護因素):** Religious beliefs, children at home, strong therapeutic alliance.

### Standardized Assessment Tools & Factors
| Assessment Domain | High-Yield Risk Factors |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SAD PERSONS Scale** | **S**ex (Male), **A**ge (<19 or >45), **D**epression, **P**revious attempt, **E**thanol abuse, **R**ational thinking loss (Psychosis), **S**ocial support lacking, **O**rganized plan, **N**o spouse, **S**ickness (chronic illness). |
| **Psychiatric Hx** | Recent inpatient discharge (< 1 month), severe mood disorder, Schizophrenia, Borderline Personality Disorder. |
| **Current MSE** | Agitation, command hallucinations, profound hopelessness, severe insomnia, global insomnia. |

## 5. Evidence-Base, Efficacy & Limitations

### Efficacy of Pharmacological & Somatic Interventions
* **Lithium (鋰鹽):** Proven independent anti-suicidal properties in Bipolar Affective Disorder.
* **Clozapine (氯氮平):** Only FDA-approved antipsychotic specifically for ↓ suicidal behavior in Schizophrenia.
* **Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT) (腦電盪治療):** Rapid relief of suicidal drive in severe/psychotic depression.

### Limitations of Risk Prediction
* **Actuarial Failure:** Standardized scales yield high false +ve rates → poor Positive Predictive Value (PPV) for rare events like suicide.
* **Dynamic Nature:** Risk fluctuates hour-by-hour; scales cannot replace continuous clinical judgment and MSE.

## 6. Practical Execution, Guidelines & Local Protocols

### Acute Hospital Authority (HA) Management Protocol
* **Triage & Stabilization:** Medical resuscitation in A&E first (e.g., NAC for paracetamol, gastric lavage, surgical repair).
* **Observation Levels (觀察級別):**
  * **Category 1 (Constant Observation):** 1-to-1 nursing within arm's reach (一對一密切監察) for imminent risk.
  * **Category 2 (Close Observation):** Checks every 15 minutes.
* **Environmental Safety:** Removal of sharps, ligatures, cords; use of anti-ligature fixtures in psychiatric wards.

### Post-Discharge & Community Follow-up
* MDT Approach: Psychiatric Medical Social Worker (MSW) (醫務社工), Community Psychiatric Nurse (CPN) (社區精神科護士).
* **Early Follow-up:** First outpatient review strictly < 7 days post-discharge (highest risk period).

## 7. Medico-Legal Considerations & HK Statutory Frameworks (e.g., Cap 136)

### HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) (香港法例第136章)
* **Section 31:** Voluntary admission (自願入院). Always the first-line preferred option.
* **Section 36:** Observation order (觀察期). Detention up to 7 days. Indicated if patient poses immediate risk to self, has a suspected mental disorder, and refuses voluntary admission. Requires 1 medical recommendation (usually A&E/Psych MO) + District Judge/Magistrate signature.
* **Section 71:** Police power (警察權力). Police can convey a person wandering in a public place (e.g., bridge, rooftop) to a place of safety (A&E) if they appear mentally disordered and pose a risk to self/others.

### Medico-Legal Duty of Care (謹慎責任)
* Failure to assess, document, or act upon foreseeable suicide risk constitutes medical negligence.
* Detailed documentation of the risk formulation and justification for the management plan is legally mandatory.

## 8. Special Populations (Lifespan, Intellectual Disability, Comorbidities)

### High-Risk Psychiatric Comorbidities
* **Depression (重度抑鬱症):** Accounts for the highest absolute number of suicides.
* **Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD) (邊緣性人格障礙):** Chronic DSH, recurrent parasuicide. 10% lifetime completed suicide risk.
* **Substance Misuse (濫用藥物):** Alcohol/drugs ↑ impulsivity, ↓ cortical inhibition.

### Lifespan Vulnerabilities
* **Elderly (長者):** Extremely high lethality, few warning signs, strong association with chronic pain, physical frailty, and bereavement.
* **Adolescents (青少年):** Highly impulsive, susceptible to contagion effects (cluster suicides/media glamorization), cyberbullying.

## 9. Adverse Effects, Ethical Dilemmas & Systemic Challenges

### Ethical Dilemmas in Involuntary Interventions
* **Autonomy (自主權) vs. Beneficence (行善原則):** Paternalistic intervention breaches autonomy to preserve life.
* **Mental Capacity Act / Common Law:** Assessing if severe depression/psychosis impairs the patient's capacity to refuse life-saving medical treatment (e.g., refusing antidote for overdose).
* **Confidentiality:** Breaching confidentiality to inform relatives/police if the patient absconds with active lethal intent.

### Systemic Challenges in Hong Kong
* A&E and ward overcrowding leading to premature discharge pressures.
* Extreme stigma (標籤效應) regarding psychiatric care delays help-seeking until crisis point.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the most robust pharmacological intervention to reduce suicide risk in a patient with treatment-resistant Schizophrenia? Answer: Clozapine.
* Trap: Assuming that a patient who self-harms by superficial cutting is "just seeking attention" and is not at risk of suicide. Pearl: A history of recurrent Non-Suicidal Self-Injury (NSSI) is one of the strongest statistical predictors for future completed suicide due to pain habituation.
* Pitfall: Discharging a severely depressed patient immediately after they start an SSRI and report feeling "a bit more energetic." Pearl: The psychomotor retardation often lifts before the cognitive hopelessness resolves. This creates a highly dangerous window where the patient now has the physical energy to execute a suicide plan.
* Viva Question: Under Cap 136, can you use Section 36 to force a patient to take medical treatment for a paracetamol overdose? Answer: No. Cap 136 only permits compulsory treatment for the *mental disorder*. Treatment for the physical consequence of the overdose must rely on Common Law principles of necessity/capacity.
* Trap: Relying solely on a "No-Suicide Contract" to manage a high-risk outpatient. Pearl: No-suicide contracts have zero legal standing and no proven efficacy in preventing suicide. You must rely on a comprehensive risk formulation and safety plan.
