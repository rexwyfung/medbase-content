# Personality & Personality Disorder (人格與人格障礙)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Clinical Definition of Personality Disorder
* **Personality (人格)**: Enduring, predictable patterns of thinking, feeling, & behaving.
* **Personality Disorder (PD) (人格障礙)**: Inflexible, maladaptive traits causing significant subjective distress or functional impairment (social/occupational). 
* Onset in adolescence/early adulthood; persists across situations.
* Ego-syntonic (自我和諧) (patient perceives traits as normal), leading to poor insight.

### Biopsychosocial Etiologies
* **Biological**: ↑ Genetic heritability (esp. Antisocial & Schizotypal).
* **Psychological**: Maladaptive defense mechanisms (e.g., splitting, projective identification) secondary to childhood trauma (童年創傷), abuse, or neglect. Pathological attachment styles.
* **Social**: Invalidating early environments, family dysfunction, institutionalization.

### Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong
* Global prevalence: ~10%.
* HK: Borderline (邊緣型) & Obsessive-Compulsive (強迫型) are most commonly seen in psychiatric clinics.
* Antisocial PD heavily overrepresented in HK correctional facilities.
* ↑ Comorbidity with substance misuse & major depressive disorder (MDD).

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Neurobiological Correlates
* **Borderline PD**: Amygdala (杏仁核) hyperreactivity → emotional dysregulation; Prefrontal cortex (PFC) (前額葉皮層) hypofunction → ↓ impulse control.
* **Antisocial PD**: ↓ Autonomic arousal (chronic under-arousal), reduced grey matter volume in PFC.
* **Schizotypal PD**: Dopaminergic dysregulation structurally similar to schizophrenia spectrum (ventricular enlargement).

### Psychopathology & Defense Mechanisms
* **Splitting (分裂)**: Inability to integrate positive/negative qualities of self/others (idealization vs. devaluation).
* **Projective Identification (投射認同)**: Projecting intolerable internal feelings onto another, subtly coercing them to act out those feelings.
* **Acting Out (付諸行動)**: Expressing unconscious emotional conflicts via actions rather than words (e.g., self-harm).

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Psychiatric History Focus
* Collateral history is **MANDATORY** (patients often lack insight).
* Trace lifelong patterns of instability (jobs, relationships, housing).
* Detailed childhood/developmental history (abuse, neglect, school truancy, conduct disorder before age 15).
* Explore chronicity (traits exist outside of acute Axis I episodes).

### Mental State Examination (MSE) Findings
* **Appearance/Behavior**: May show self-harm scars (BPD), dramatic attire (Histrionic), or guardedness (Paranoid).
* **Speech**: Often normal form; Cluster A may be vague/circumstantial; Cluster B may be highly emotive/pressured.
* **Mood/Affect**: High lability, intense anger, chronic feelings of emptiness.
* **Thoughts**: Absence of true delusions (unless transient micro-psychotic episodes in BPD under stress).
* **Cognition**: Globally intact.
* **Insight**: Typically poor. Patient externalizes blame (others are the problem).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Psychiatric Differentials
* **Bipolar Disorder (躁鬱症)**: Mood shifts last days/weeks (not hours/minutes like BPD). PDs lack true manic episodes.
* **Schizophrenia (精神分裂症)**: True, persistent psychosis vs. transient/stress-related paranoia in Schizotypal/Borderline.
* **Anxiety Disorders / MDD**: Acute onset, ego-dystonic vs. chronic, ego-syntonic PD traits.
* **Substance Use Disorder (物質濫用)**: Intoxication/withdrawal mimicking impulsivity/mood lability.

### Organic & Medical Rule-Outs
* **Frontal Lobe Syndrome (額葉綜合症)**: Traumatic brain injury (TBI), tumor, or frontotemporal dementia causing personality change.
* **Temporal Lobe Epilepsy (顳葉癲癇)**: Can present with aggressive outbursts.
* **Endocrinopathies**: Hyperthyroidism (irritability), neurosyphilis.

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### DSM-5 Cluster Classification & Criteria
| Cluster (群組) | Theme | Disorders & Core Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A (Odd/Eccentric)** | Weird | **Paranoid (偏執型)**: Suspicious, unforgiving.<br>**Schizoid (類精神分裂型)**: Solitary, restricted affect, indifferent.<br>**Schizotypal (精神分裂型)**: Magical thinking, eccentric, micro-psychosis. |
| **B (Dramatic/Erratic)** | Wild | **Antisocial (反社會型)**: Disregard for rights, deceitful, lack of remorse.<br>**Borderline (邊緣型)**: Unstable relationships, self-harm, fear of abandonment.<br>**Histrionic (表演型)**: Attention-seeking, provocative.<br>**Narcissistic (自戀型)**: Grandiosity, lack of empathy, needs admiration. |
| **C (Anxious/Fearful)** | Worried | **Avoidant (逃避型)**: Fears rejection, socially inhibited.<br>**Dependent (依賴型)**: Clinging, needs to be cared for.<br>**Obsessive-Compulsive (強迫型)**: Rigid, perfectionistic, control-obsessed (Anankastic). |

### ICD-11 Dimensional Approach
* Discards specific categorical types (except 'Borderline pattern').
* Classifies by **Severity**: Mild, Moderate, Severe.
* Classifies by **Trait Domains**: Negative affectivity, Detachment, Dissociality, Disinhibition, Anankastia.

### Standardized Rating Scales
* **IPDE** (International Personality Disorder Examination).
* **PDQ-4** (Personality Diagnostic Questionnaire).

### Organic Rule-Out Investigations
* Bloods: TFTs, VDRL, U-Tox (urine toxicology screen).
* Neuroimaging: CT/MRI Brain if personality change is late-onset (>30 years old) or sudden.

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Acute Crisis Management
* Goal: Stabilization & harm minimization.
* Avoid prolonged hospital admissions → ↑ risk of regression & institutional dependency.
* Establish clear, firm boundaries. Ensure consistent team approach to prevent splitting (staff division).

### Risk Assessment
* **Self-Harm (自我傷害)**: High lethality risk in BPD. Assess intent vs. tension-relief mechanisms.
* **Violence**: High risk of hetero-directed violence in Antisocial PD. Assess forensic history.

### Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance - Cap 136)
* PD alone rarely justifies involuntary admission (Section 71/73) unless co-morbid with acute psychotic/severe affective episode.
* "Psychopathic disorder" (abnormally aggressive/seriously irresponsible conduct) is a distinct legal category in Cap 136, but high threshold for compulsory treatment requires it to be "treatable" (treatability test).

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Psychotherapy (First-Line Treatment)
* **Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) (辯證行為治療)**: Gold standard for BPD. Focuses on distress tolerance, emotion regulation, interpersonal effectiveness, mindfulness.
* **Mentalization-Based Therapy (MBT)**: ↑ Capacity to understand own and others' mental states.
* **Schema Therapy**: Addresses maladaptive lifelong core beliefs.

### Pharmacotherapy (Symptom-Targeted)
* *No drug cures PD.* Use strictly for targeted symptoms; avoid polypharmacy.
* **Cognitive/Perceptual Symptoms** (paranoia/dissociation): Low-dose atypical antipsychotics (e.g., Quetiapine, Aripiprazole).
* **Affective Dysregulation/Impulsivity**: SSRIs (e.g., Fluoxetine) or mood stabilizers (e.g., Lamotrigine, Valproate).
* Avoid benzodiazepines → ↑ risk of behavioral disinhibition & dependency.

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Illness Trajectory
* Chronic, lifelong pattern.
* Symptoms peak in early adulthood.
* **Cluster B "Burn-out"**: Impulsivity and dramatic behaviors often attenuate significantly by middle age (40s-50s).

### Prognostic Indicators
* **Good Prognosis**: High intelligence, ability to maintain employment, cluster C traits.
* **Poor Prognosis**: Cluster B (Antisocial), comorbid substance abuse, severe childhood trauma, absence of social support.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Psychiatric & Social Complications
* **Psychiatric**: ↑ Lifetime risk of completed suicide (up to 10% in BPD). Comorbid MDD, eating disorders.
* **Social**: Severe interpersonal chaos, divorce, chronic unemployment, homelessness, incarceration.

### Iatrogenic Complications
* Polypharmacy: Side-effect burden (metabolic syndrome from off-label antipsychotics).
* Prescription drug dependence (benzodiazepines, analgesics).
* Staff boundary violations (inappropriate relationships due to extreme idealization/manipulation).

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you differentiate Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder (OCPD) from OCD? Answer: OCPD is ego-syntonic (patient likes their rigid perfectionism) and lacks true repetitive obsessions/compulsions, whereas OCD is ego-dystonic and causes marked distress.
* Trap: Admitting a patient with Borderline PD for a month to "cure" their chronic suicidality. Pearl: Prolonged admission often exacerbates regression and self-harm; aim for brief, goal-directed crisis admissions (48-72 hours) and rapid discharge to community therapies.
* Pitfall: Arguing with a patient who is splitting the ward staff. Do not take devaluation personally; use a unified multidisciplinary team approach with strictly enforced, pre-agreed behavioral boundaries.
