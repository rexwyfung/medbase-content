# Paranoid Symptoms & Syndromes (偏執症狀與綜合症)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Definition of Paranoid Symptoms (偏執症狀定義)
* **Paranoia (偏執)**: Encompasses persecutory, grandiose, jealous, religious, or erotomanic themes
* **Delusion (妄想)**: Firmly held belief on inadequate grounds → impervious to rational argument → out of keeping with cultural/educational background
* **Overvalued Idea (超價觀念)**: Comprehensible idea pursued beyond reason → retains marginal insight

### Biopsychosocial Etiologies (生理心理社會病因)
* **Biological (生理)**: ↑ DA activity in mesolimbic pathway, +ve family history of Schizophrenia (精神分裂症) / Delusional Disorder (妄想症), advanced age, sensory impairment (e.g., deafness → ↑ paranoia)
* **Psychological (心理)**: Projection (投射) defense mechanism, cognitive biases (jumping to conclusions, externalizing attributional bias)
* **Social (社會)**: Social isolation (社會孤立), migrant status, severe childhood adversity, HK high-density living stressors

### Local Epidemiology (本地流行病學)
* **Delusional Disorder**: Rare (lifetime prevalence ~0.2%) → onset typically middle-to-late adult life (>40 yrs)
* **Schizophrenia**: ~1% lifetime prevalence in HK
* **Subtypes**: Persecutory (被迫害) most common → Morbid Jealousy (病態嫉妒) more common in males → Erotomania (色情狂) more common in females

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Neurotransmitter Pathways (神經傳導物質路徑)
* **Dopamine (多巴胺) Hypothesis**: ↑ DA in mesolimbic tract → +ve symptoms (delusions, AH)
* **Serotonin (血清素)**: 5-HT2A receptor hyper-modulation → alters perception & cognitive flexibility
* **Glutamate (谷氨酸)**: NMDA receptor hypofunction → downstream DA dysregulation

### Psychopathology of Paranoid Delusions (偏執妄想的精神病理學)
* **Primary Delusion (原發性妄想)**: Autochthonous (sudden onset) → heavily weighted for Schizophrenia
* **Secondary Delusion (繼發性妄想)**: Arises from preceding morbid experience (e.g., AH derogatory voices → belief of persecution)
* **Systematization (系統化)**: Highly organized/complex delusional network → hallmark of Delusional Disorder (妄想症)

### Specific Delusional Syndromes (特定妄想綜合症)
* **Othello Syndrome (奧賽羅綜合症)**: Morbid/pathological jealousy (病態嫉妒) → dangerousness to partner
* **De Clérambault's Syndrome (德克勒朗波綜合症)**: Erotomania (色情狂) → belief that a person of higher status is in love with them
* **Capgras Syndrome (卡普格拉綜合症)**: Delusional misidentification → imposter replaced loved one
* **Cotard's Syndrome (科塔爾綜合症)**: Nihilistic delusions (虛無妄想) → belief one is dead / rotting (often in severe psychotic depression)

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Psychiatric History Focus (精神科病史重點)
* **HPC**: Onset (sudden vs insidious), themes (persecutory, jealous, grandiose), fixity (complete vs partial)
* **Impact**: Behavioral response to delusions (e.g., barricading doors, seeking legal action)
* **Substance Hx**: Methamphetamine (冰毒) / Cocaine (可卡因) / Cannabis (大麻) → crucial in HK context
* **Collateral Hx (第三者病史)**: Essential → patients often lack insight / conceal beliefs

### Mental State Examination (MSE) Findings (精神狀態檢查發現)
* **Appearance & Behavior**: Suspicious, guarded, hostile, averting gaze, restless
* **Speech**: Evasive, circumstantial, formal thought disorder (formal TD) absent in pure Delusional Disorder
* **Mood & Affect**: Irritable, angry, mood-congruent vs incongruent (suggests Schizophrenia)
* **Thoughts**: Persecutory (被害), grandiose (誇大), ideas of reference (牽連觀念) → assess thought insertion/withdrawal/broadcasting (Schizophrenia First-Rank Symptoms)
* **Perceptions**: Hallucinations usually absent in Delusional Disorder → if present (tactile/olfactory), must be related to delusional theme
* **Insight**: Markedly impaired (reality testing deficient)

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Primary Psychiatric Differentials (原發性精神科鑑別診斷)
* **Schizophrenia (精神分裂症)**: +ve bizarre delusions, prominent AH, formal TD, -ve symptoms
* **Delusional Disorder (妄想症)**: Non-bizarre (or bizarre) encapsulated delusion >1 month, functioning largely preserved, no prominent hallucinations
* **Bipolar I Disorder, Manic Episode (躁鬱症躁狂發作)**: Grandiose/persecutory delusions + ↑ mood, ↑ energy, ↓ sleep
* **Major Depressive Disorder with Psychotic Features (重度抑鬱症伴精神病性特徵)**: Nihilistic/guilt/persecutory delusions + severe ↓ mood
* **Substance-Induced Psychotic Disorder (物質誘發精神病)**: Secondary to stimulants (Methamphetamine/Cocaine)

### Organic/Medical Rule-Outs (器質性/內科排除診斷)
* **Dementia/Neurocognitive Disorders (認知障礙症)**: Alzheimer's, Lewy Body (VH prominent)
* **Neurological (神經科)**: Temporal Lobe Epilepsy (TLE), CNS tumors, Huntington's
* **Endocrine/Metabolic (內分泌/代謝)**: Thyroid dysfunction, Cushing's, hypercalcemia
* **Infectious (感染)**: Neurosyphilis, HIV encephalopathy, HSV encephalitis

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### Diagnostic Criteria (診斷標準)
| Disorder | Core DSM-5 Criteria Highlights |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Delusional Disorder (妄想症)** | 1+ delusions for $\ge$ 1 month. Criterion A for Schizophrenia NOT met. Functioning not markedly impaired (apart from delusion impact). Brief mood episodes allowed. |
| **Schizophrenia (精神分裂症)** | 2+ symptoms for 1 month (must include delusions, hallucinations, or disorganized speech). Continuous signs $\ge$ 6 months. |
| **Substance-Induced Psychosis** | Delusions/Hallucinations developed during/soon after substance intoxication/withdrawal. |

### Rating Scales (評估量表)
* **PANSS (陽性與陰性症狀量表)**: Assesses severity of +ve/-ve symptoms
* **BPRS (簡明精神病評定量表)**: Rapid baseline symptom severity
* **MacArthur Competence Assessment Tool**: Evaluates capacity in resistant patients

### Organic Rule-Out Investigations (器質性排除檢查)
* **Bedside**: MMSE / MoCA (rule out cognitive impairment)
* **Bloods**: CBC, LFT, RFT, TFT, VDRL/HIV, Ca2+, Vitamin B12/Folate
* **Toxicology**: Urine drug screen (UDS) (尿液毒理化驗) → vital for sympathomimetics
* **Imaging/EEG**: CT/MRI Brain (if new onset >50 yrs, atypical presentation, neurological signs)

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Acute Risk Assessment (急性風險評估)
* **Risk to Others (對他人的風險)**: High in Morbid Jealousy (Othello) → risk of domestic violence / homicide towards partner or perceived rival
* **Risk to Self (對自己的風險)**: Depression secondary to paranoia → suicide risk
* **Absconding/Vulnerability**: Barricading self, starvation (fear of poisoning)

### Acute Pharmacological Management (急性藥物治療)
* **De-escalation**: Low-stimulus environment, empathetic but non-colluding approach
* **Rapid Tranquilization (快速鎮靜)**: IM Haloperidol (氟哌啶醇) 2-5mg + IM Promethazine (異丙嗪) 25-50mg OR IM Lorazepam (勞拉西泮) 1-2mg

### Medico-Legal: HK Mental Health Ordinance Cap 136 (香港精神健康條例第136章)
| Section (條例) | Purpose & Duration | Criteria in Paranoid States |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 31 (第31條)** | Voluntary Admission (自願入院) | Patient retains insight/capacity to consent. |
| **Section 32 (第32條)** | Observation (觀察) $\le$ 7 days | Suspected mental disorder + risk to self/others. |
| **Section 36 (第36條)** | Treatment (治療) $\le$ 28 days | Confirmed mental disorder + requires inpatient Tx + risk to self/others. |
| **Section 42B (第42B條)** | Conditional Discharge (有條件出院) | Used for chronic non-compliant patients to mandate community follow-up. |

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Pharmacotherapy (藥物治療)
* **First-line**: Second-Generation Antipsychotics (SGAs) → Risperidone (利培酮), Olanzapine (奧氮平), Aripiprazole (阿立哌唑)
* **Refractory/Non-compliant**: Long-Acting Injectables (LAI) (長效針劑) → e.g., Paliperidone Palmitate (帕利哌酮)
* **Treatment-Resistant**: Clozapine (氯氮平)

| Antipsychotic (抗精神病藥) | Common / Severe Side Effects |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Olanzapine (奧氮平)** | ↑ Weight, Metabolic Syndrome, sedation |
| **Risperidone (利培酮)** | ↑ Prolactin (Hyperprolactinemia), Extrapyramidal Symptoms (EPS) |
| **Clozapine (氯氮平)** | Agranulocytosis (致命性白血球缺乏症), Myocarditis, ↓ Seizure threshold, severe constipation |

### Psychological Interventions (心理介入治療)
* **CBT for Psychosis (CBTp)**: Target cognitive biases → gently challenge evidence without direct confrontation → focus on reducing distress rather than eradicating the delusion
* **Supportive Psychotherapy (支持性心理治療)**: Build therapeutic alliance, validate distress (not the belief)

### Social Support & Rehabilitation (社會支援與復康)
* **Community Psychiatric Nurse (CPN) (社區精神科護士)**: Monitor medication compliance (crucial in Delusional Disorder)
* **Medical Social Worker (MSW) (醫務社工)**: Housing, financial aid, carer support, domestic violence prevention (especially in morbid jealousy)

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Illness Trajectory (疾病軌跡)
* **Delusional Disorder**: Chronic, unremitting, but encapsulated → patient maintains employment/social life outside the delusional theme. Compliance is universally poor.
* **Substance-Induced**: Resolves with abstinence, though chronic Methamphetamine use can cause permanent paranoid sensitization.

### Prognostic Factors (預後因素)
* **Good Prognosis (+ve)**: Acute onset, identifiable precipitant, female sex, good premorbid functioning, early treatment compliance.
* **Poor Prognosis (-ve)**: Insidious onset, prominent -ve symptoms, early age of onset, substance abuse, poor insight, lack of social support.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Psychiatric & Social Complications (精神與社會併發症)
* **Forensic/Legal**: Stalking (Erotomania), assault/murder (Morbid Jealousy), litigious behavior (Querulant paranoia)
* **Social**: Severe isolation, marital breakdown, unemployment
* **Comorbidities**: High rates of secondary Major Depressive Disorder (重度抑鬱症) and substance use (self-medication)

### Psychotropic Adverse Effects (精神科藥物不良反應)
* **Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS) (神經阻滯劑惡性綜合症)**: Medical emergency → fever, rigidity, altered mental status, ↑ CK
* **Tardive Dyskinesia (TD) (遲發性運動障礙)**: Chronic D2 blockade → involuntary oro-facial movements
* **Metabolic Syndrome (代謝綜合症)**: ↑ central obesity, ↑ lipids, ↑ glucose → high cardiovascular morbidity in chronic psychiatric populations

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you differentiate a delusion from an overvalued idea in a clinical interview? Answer: A delusion is held with absolute subjective certainty and is impervious to logical counter-argument, whereas an overvalued idea is comprehensible and pursued beyond reason, but the patient may retain a small degree of insight and entertain alternative views temporarily.
* Trap: Assuming that because a belief is highly improbable, it must be false and therefore delusional. Pearl: Falsity is not the absolute defining criterion; pathological jealousy (Othello Syndrome) can occasionally be factually true (a partner may actually be unfaithful), but the belief is delusional because of the pathological, groundless logic used to arrive at the conclusion (e.g., claiming a displaced towel proves infidelity).
* Pitfall: Directly challenging or arguing with a patient's paranoid delusion. This destroys rapport and entrenches the belief. Validate the distress and fear the belief causes ("It must be terrifying to feel followed") without colluding with the belief itself ("I do not see the agents, but I believe you are frightened").
* Viva Question: What specific risk assessment is mandatory in a patient presenting with Morbid Jealousy? Answer: You must aggressively assess the risk of domestic violence, assault, or homicide towards the partner and the perceived rival, including the presence of weapons, stalking behavior, and specific threats.
* Trap: Using the term "paranoid" solely to mean "persecutory". Pearl: In descriptive psychopathology, "paranoid" is a broader term encompassing grandiose, erotic, jealous, and religious delusions, not just persecutory themes.
