# Psychiatry of the Elderly (老年精神病學)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### 1.1 Clinical Definition of Old Age Psychiatry (老年精神病學定義)
* **Scope**: Psychiatric disorders presenting in patients aged ≥65 years.
* **Core Triad (The 3 Ds)**: Dementia (認知障礙症), Delirium (譫妄症), Depression (抑鬱症).
* **Late-Onset Psychosis**: Very Late-Onset Schizophrenia-Like Psychosis (VLOSLP) / Late-Onset Paraphrenia (晚發性妄想症).

### 1.2 Local Epidemiology in Hong Kong (香港流行病學)
* **Demographics**: HK has rapidly ↑ aging population; ~20% aged ≥65.
* **Dementia Prevalence**: ~10% in ≥65 yrs; ↑ to ~30% in ≥85 yrs.
* **Depression**: ~10% community prevalence; high suicide rate in elderly HK males (highest demographic risk group).

### 1.3 Biopsychosocial Etiologies (生理心理社會病因)
* **Biological**: Neurodegeneration (amyloid/tau), cerebrovascular disease (小血管疾病), sensory impairments (deafness/blindness → ↑ psychosis/delirium risk), polypharmacy.
* **Psychological**: Loss of independence, grief/bereavement, loss of cognitive reserve.
* **Social**: Retirement (loss of status), social isolation, financial strain, elder abuse, caregiver burnout.

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### 2.1 Neurobiology of Aging & Dementia (大腦老化與神經生物學)
* **Structural**: Global cortical atrophy, ventricular enlargement, ↓ hippocampal volume.
* **Neurotransmitters**: ↓ ACh (memory loss), ↓ DA (parkinsonism, apathy), altered 5-HT/NE (depression).
* **Vascular**: White matter hyperintensities (WMHs), microinfarcts → vascular depression/dementia.

### 2.2 Psychopathology of Late-Life Depression (晚年抑鬱症心理病理學)
* **Presentation**: ↑ somatic complaints (hypochondriasis), prominent agitation/anxiety, severe insomnia.
* **Cognitive**: "Pseudodementia" (假性認知障礙) → cognitive deficits secondary to depression.
* **Psychosis**: Cotard's syndrome (nihilistic delusions) (虛無妄想), delusions of guilt/poverty.

### 2.3 Psychopathology of Late-Onset Psychosis (晚發性精神病)
* **Features**: Formed visual hallucinations (Charles Bonnet syndrome) (查理斯·邦納症候群) or auditory hallucinations.
* **Delusions**: Partition delusions (e.g., people living in the attic/walls), persecutory, theft.
* **Negative Symptoms**: Usually absent or mild compared to early-onset schizophrenia.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### 3.1 Psychiatric History: Essential Geriatric Modifications (病史採集調整)
* **Collateral History (CRITICAL)**: Essential for establishing baseline cognition, chronology of decline, and personality changes.
* **Functional Assessment**: 
  * **ADLs (基本日常生活活動)**: Bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting.
  * **IADLs (工具性日常生活活動)**: Managing finances, medications, cooking, transport.
* **Pharmacological Hx**: Strict reconciliation for polypharmacy & anticholinergic burden.

### 3.2 Mental State Examination (MSE) in the Elderly (老年精神狀態檢查)
* **Appearance & Behaviour**: Self-neglect, sensory aids (glasses/hearing aids), psychomotor retardation/agitation, wandering.
* **Speech**: Dysphasia (expressive/receptive) vs. poverty of speech (depression/schizophrenia).
* **Mood & Affect**: Apathy vs. true depressed mood, emotional lability (pseudobulbar affect).
* **Thought**: Persecutory themes (often related to memory loss, e.g., "my caregiver stole my purse").
* **Perception**: Illusion vs. true hallucination; check visual/hearing deficits.
* **Cognition**: Bedside screening (orientation, registration, attention, recall).

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### 4.1 The 3 Ds: Dementia vs. Delirium vs. Depression (認知障礙症 vs 譫妄症 vs 抑鬱症)

| Feature | Delirium (譫妄症) | Dementia (認知障礙症) | Depression (Pseudodementia) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Onset** | Acute (hours/days) | Insidious (months/years) | Subacute (weeks/months) |
| **Course** | Fluctuating (worse at night) | Slowly progressive | Diurnal variation (worse AM) |
| **Consciousness** | Clouded / Impaired | Clear (until late stages) | Clear |
| **Attention** | Severely impaired | Preserved (until late stages) | Poor concentration |
| **Memory Deficit** | Recent memory (inattention) | Recent memory (storage failure) | Variable ("I don't know" answers) |
| **Reversibility** | Usually reversible | Irreversible | Reversible with treatment |

### 4.2 Psychiatric vs. Organic Rule-Outs (精神病 vs 腦部器質性疾病)
* **Medical Rule-Outs for Delirium (PINCH ME)**: **P**ain, **I**nfection, **N**utrition, **C**onstipation, **H**ydration, **M**edication, **E**nvironment.
* **Medical Rule-Outs for Late-Life Psychosis**: Parkinson's disease (DA agonists), Lewy Body Dementia (LBD), UTI, stroke, subdural hematoma.
* **Medical Rule-Outs for Late-Life Depression**: Hypothyroidism, Vitamin B12/Folate deficiency, cerebrovascular disease, pancreatic cancer.

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### 5.1 DSM-5 Major Neurocognitive Disorder (重度認知神經障礙)
* **Criterion A**: Significant cognitive decline in ≥1 domain (complex attention, executive function, learning/memory, language, perceptual-motor, social cognition) based on concern + objective testing.
* **Criterion B**: Deficits interfere with independence in everyday activities (IADLs).
* **Criterion C**: Not exclusively in the context of a delirium.
* **Criterion D**: Not better explained by another mental disorder (e.g., MDD, schizophrenia).

### 5.2 Cognitive Rating Scales (認知評估量表)
* **MMSE (簡短智能測驗)**: Score <24/30 suggests impairment; biased by education level/language.
* **MoCA (蒙特利爾認知評估)**: Score <26/30; superior for detecting Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) & executive dysfunction.
* **HK-MoCA / HK-MMSE**: Validated Cantonese versions localized for HK populations.

### 5.3 Organic Rule-Out Investigations (器質性疾病排除檢查)
* **Bloods**: FBC, U&E, LFT, TFT, Vitamin B12, Folate, Ca2+, HbA1c, VDRL/HIV (if indicated).
* **Urine**: MSU (rule out subclinical UTI).
* **Imaging**: CT/MRI Brain (rule out SOL, stroke, NPH; assess medial temporal lobe atrophy [MTA]).
* **ECG**: Baseline before commencing psychotropics (QTC prolongation).

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### 6.1 Acute Management of BPSD (行為及心理症狀急性處理)
* **De-escalation**: Low-stimulation environment, gentle reorientation, ensure hearing/vision aids are functional.
* **Pharmacological (Last Resort)**:
  * **First-line**: Haloperidol 0.5mg PO/IM (AVOID in Parkinson's/LBD).
  * **Alternative**: Lorazepam 0.5mg PO/IM (use cautiously → paradoxically worsens delirium/falls).

### 6.2 Risk Assessment in the Elderly (長者風險評估)
* **Risk to Self**: High suicide risk (especially isolated males with chronic pain/depression), self-neglect, severe malnutrition, wandering/getting lost, fire hazards (leaving stove on).
* **Risk to Others**: Aggression due to persecutory delusions, unsafe driving.
* **Vulnerability**: Financial exploitation, physical abuse by caregivers.

### 6.3 HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) & Statutory Frameworks (香港精神健康條例)
* **Part IVC**: Consent to medical/dental treatment for mentally incapacitated persons (MIP). Doctor can treat in patient's best interest without consent if urgently required.
* **Guardianship Board (監護委員會)**: Appoints legal guardian for accommodation, care, and treatment decisions for adults lacking capacity.
* **Enduring Power of Attorney (EPA) (持久授權書)**: Created while competent, takes effect upon loss of capacity (financial matters only in HK).

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### 7.1 Pharmacotherapy: Cognitive Enhancers (認知促進藥物)
* **Acetylcholinesterase Inhibitors (AChEIs)**: Donepezil (多奈哌齊), Rivastigmine, Galantamine (for mild-moderate Alzheimer's/LBD).
* **NMDA Receptor Antagonist**: Memantine (美金剛) (for moderate-severe Alzheimer's).

### 7.2 Pharmacotherapy: Psychotropics in the Elderly (精神科藥物)
* **Rule**: "Start low, go slow" (↓ renal/hepatic clearance).
* **Antidepressants**: SSRIs (Sertraline, Citalopram). Avoid TCAs (anticholinergic/cardiotoxic). Monitor for hyponatremia (SIADH).
* **Antipsychotics**: Quetiapine, Risperidone for severe BPSD. ↑ Risk of CVA and all-cause mortality in dementia (Black Box Warning).

### 7.3 Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT) (腦電盪療法)
* **Indications**: Severe psychotic depression, high suicide risk, food/fluid refusal (life-threatening), intolerance to pharmacotherapy.
* **Efficacy**: Highly effective and safe in elderly; requires careful cardiovascular clearance.

### 7.4 Psychosocial & Environmental Interventions (心理社會介入)
* **Psychological**: Reminiscence therapy (懷緬治療), Reality orientation (現實導向), Validation therapy (確認治療).
* **Social**: Caregiver support/respite care (CGAS in HK), Day Care Centres for the Elderly (DECC), environmental adaptations (calendars, clocks, clear signage, bright lighting).

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### 8.1 Dementia Trajectory (認知障礙症病程)
* **Alzheimer's**: Gradual, smooth decline; average survival 4-8 years post-diagnosis.
* **Vascular Dementia**: Step-wise decline, corresponding to new ischemic events.
* **Lewy Body Dementia**: Rapidly fluctuating cognition, early visual hallucinations, extreme sensitivity to neuroleptics.

### 8.2 Late-Life Depression Trajectory (晚年抑鬱症病程)
* **Prognosis**: Good response to treatment, but high relapse rate.
* **Vascular Depression**: Worse prognosis, poorer response to SSRIs, ↑ risk of progression to overt dementia.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### 9.1 Psychiatric & Medical Complications (精神與生理併發症)
* **Frailty Syndrome**: Muscle wasting, falls, hip fractures, prolonged hospitalizations.
* **Infections**: Aspiration pneumonia (due to late-stage dysphagia), recurrent UTIs.
* **Mortality**: ↑ Suicide rate (completed, violent methods in elderly males).

### 9.2 Social Complications (社會併發症)
* **Caregiver Burden**: High rates of clinical depression and physical exhaustion in spouses/children.
* **Institutionalization**: Loss of independence, placement into Care and Attention (C&A) homes.

### 9.3 Psychotropic Drug Side Effects in the Elderly (藥物副作用)

| Drug Class | Specific Elderly Risks & Complications |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Antipsychotics** | Parkinsonism, Tardive Dyskinesia, Stroke/TIA, ↑ Mortality, QTc prolongation, Orthostatic hypotension (falls). |
| **SSRIs** | Hyponatremia (SIADH), GI bleeding, osteoporosis/falls, insomnia. |
| **AChEIs** | Bradycardia, syncope, severe nausea/diarrhoea, urinary incontinence. |
| **Benzodiazepines** | Paradoxical disinhibition, respiratory depression, falls/hip fractures, delirium. |

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the most reliable cognitive screening tool for a highly educated elderly patient with suspected mild cognitive impairment? Answer: The MoCA, as the MMSE suffers from a ceiling effect in highly educated individuals and lacks sensitivity for executive dysfunction.
* Trap: Assuming memory loss in an elderly patient is always early Alzheimer's Disease. Pearl: Always rule out "pseudodementia" caused by late-life depression, which presents with "I don't know" answers, prominent distress about memory loss, and a subacute onset.
* Pitfall: Prescribing Haloperidol for agitation in a patient with fluctuating cognition and visual hallucinations. This risks severe, potentially fatal neuroleptic sensitivity reactions in Lewy Body Dementia; use behavioral strategies or low-dose Quetiapine instead.
* Viva Question: How do you differentiate the cognitive deficits of normal aging from Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI)? Answer: Normal aging involves mild processing speed and recall delay but ADLs/IADLs are fully intact; MCI involves objective cognitive deficits on testing (>1.5 SD below mean) but ADLs remain preserved, without meeting full dementia criteria.
* Trap: Discharging an elderly male with treated depression once mood improves without assessing social isolation. Pearl: Elderly males in Hong Kong have the highest completed suicide rates; rigorous psychosocial support and follow-up are mandatory even after symptomatic remission.
* Pitfall: Using tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs) like Amitriptyline for sleep or neuropathic pain in the elderly. TCAs cause high anticholinergic burden, leading to acute delirium, urinary retention, constipation, and dangerous orthostatic hypotension resulting in falls.
