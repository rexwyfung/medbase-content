# Dementia, Delirium & Other Neuropsychiatric Disorders (認知障礙症、譫妄及其他神經精神疾病)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Biopsychosocial Etiologies & Local Epidemiology

### Definition of Major Neurocognitive Disorder / Dementia (認知障礙症定義)
* **Dementia (認知障礙症)**: Chronic, global, usually irreversible deterioration of cognition in clear sensorium. 
* **Core Deficits**: Memory + ≥1 other domain (aphasia, apraxia, agnosia, executive dysfunction) → functional impairment.

### Definition of Delirium (譫妄定義)
* **Delirium (譫妄)**: Acute, transient, fluctuating global cognitive impairment with clouded consciousness.
* **Core Triad**: Inattention + altered consciousness + fluctuating course.

### Biopsychosocial Etiologies of Neuropsychiatric Disorders (生物心理社會病因)
* **Bio (生物)**: Neurodegeneration (Alzheimer's), vascular disease, metabolic derangements (UTI, hypoxia, polypharmacy).
* **Psycho (心理)**: Pre-morbid cognitive reserve, baseline psychiatric illness, sensory deprivation (hearing/vision loss).
* **Social (社會)**: Relocation trauma, social isolation, loss of primary caregiver.

### Hong Kong Epidemiology (香港流行病學)
* **Prevalence (患病率)**: ~10% of HK population aged ≥70; ↑ incidence with rapidly aging HK demographics.
* **Delirium in HK Hospitals (香港醫院譫妄數據)**: Occurs in up to 30% of acute medical/surgical elderly admissions (Hospital Authority data).
* **Most Common Subtypes**: Alzheimer's Disease (AD) (60-70%) > Vascular Dementia (VaD) (20-30%).

## 2. Neurobiology, Psychopathology & Neurotransmitter Pathways

### Alzheimer’s Disease Neurobiology (阿茲海默症神經生物學)
* **Pathology**: Extracellular β-amyloid plaques (Aβ42) + intracellular neurofibrillary tangles (hyperphosphorylated tau).
* **Neurotransmitters**: ↓ ACh (nucleus basalis of Meynert), ↑ Glutamate (excitotoxicity).
* **Atrophy**: Medial temporal lobe (hippocampus) → parietal → frontal.

### Vascular Dementia Neurobiology (血管性認知障礙症神經生物學)
* **Pathology**: Micro-infarcts, strategic single infarcts, or chronic subcortical ischemia (Binswanger's disease).
* **Mechanism**: Hypoxia-ischemia → white matter lesions (leukoaraiosis) → disruption of fronto-subcortical circuits.

### Lewy Body Dementia (LBD) & Parkinson’s Disease Dementia (PDD) (路易氏體及柏金遜症認知障礙)
* **Pathology**: Intracellular Lewy bodies (α-synuclein).
* **Pathways**: ↓ Dopamine (substantia nigra) → Parkinsonism; ↓ ACh → visual hallucinations/fluctuations.

### Delirium Neurotransmitter Imbalance (譫妄神經傳導物質失衡)
* **Core Mechanism**: Global cortical dysfunction due to oxidative stress & neuroinflammation (cytokine storm).
* **Pathways**: ↓ ACh (anticholinergic drugs/infection) + ↑ Dopamine (dopaminergic excess).

## 3. Clinical Assessment (Psychiatric History & Mental State Examination [MSE] ONLY)

### Psychiatric & Collateral History (精神及提供者病史)
* **Collateral Source**: Essential. Patient usually lacks insight (anosognosia).
* **Timeline**: Acute/hours (Delirium) vs Step-wise (VaD) vs Gradual/years (AD).
* **Functional Decline**: Assess ADLs (Activities of Daily Living) & IADLs (Instrumental ADLs).

### MSE: Dementia Findings (認知障礙症精神狀態檢查)
* **Appearance/Behavior**: Apraxia (dressing difficulty), wandering, perseveration.
* **Speech**: Expressive aphasia, nominal aphasia (word-finding difficulties), echolalia.
* **Mood/Affect**: Labile, apathy, secondary depression.
* **Thought**: Poverty of thought, paranoid delusions (e.g., "caregiver stealing money").
* **Sensorium**: CLEAR consciousness (distinguishes from delirium).

### MSE: Delirium Findings (譫妄精神狀態檢查)
* **Appearance/Behavior**: Hyperactive (agitated, plucking at clothes) or Hypoactive (lethargic, easily missed).
* **Speech**: Rambling, incoherent, dysarthric.
* **Perception**: Visual hallucinations (VH) (e.g., seeing insects/intruders), illusions.
* **Sensorium**: CLOUDED consciousness, fluctuating level of arousal (worse at night - "sundowning"), extreme inattention.

## 4. Differential Diagnoses (Psychiatric vs. Organic/Medical Rule-Outs)

### Psychiatric Differentials (精神科鑑別診斷)
* **Pseudodementia (Depression in Elderly / 老年抑鬱)**: Prominent "I don't know" answers, diurnal mood variation, highlights failures, intact attention.
* **Late-Onset Schizophrenia (晚發性精神分裂症)**: Prominent AH/delusions, clear consciousness, less severe memory impairment initially.
* **Bipolar Disorder (Manic Episode / 躁狂發作)**: Hyperactivity, pressured speech, but intact orientation unless severe.

### Organic / Medical Rule-Outs (器質性/內科疾病排除)
* **Endocrine**: Hypothyroidism, Hypoglycemia.
* **Infectious**: UTI, Pneumonia, Neurosyphilis, HIV encephalopathy.
* **Neurological**: Subdural hematoma, Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus (NPH) (Triad: wobbly, wacky, wet).
* **Toxic/Metabolic**: Uremia, Hepatic encephalopathy, Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome (Vit B1 deficiency).

## 5. Diagnostic Criteria (DSM-5/ICD-11), Rating Scales & Organic Rule-Out Investigations

### DSM-5 Diagnostic Criteria Comparison (DSM-5 診斷標準比較)
| Feature (特徵) | Delirium (譫妄) | Major Neurocognitive Disorder (認知障礙症) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Onset** | Acute (hours/days) | Insidious (months/years) |
| **Attention** | Profoundly impaired | Usually intact until severe stages |
| **Consciousness** | Fluctuating / Clouded | Clear / Alert |
| **Course** | Reversible (usually) | Progressive / Irreversible |
| **Hallucinations** | Common (mostly Visual) | Rare (except in LBD) |

### Cognitive Rating Scales (認知評估量表)
* **HK-MoCA (香港版蒙特利爾認知評估)**: Sensitized for mild cognitive impairment (MCI). Adjusted for education level.
* **MMSE (簡短智能測驗)**: Max 30. <24 suggests impairment. Less sensitive for frontal/executive dysfunction.
* **AMT (Abbreviated Mental Test)**: Quick 10-item screen used in HK acute medical wards.

### Baseline Organic Rule-Out Investigations (基本器質性排除檢查)
* **Bloods (血液檢查)**: CBC, U&E, LFT, TFT, Vit B12, Folate, VDRL/Syphilis, Ca2+, Glucose.
* **Imaging (影像學)**: CT Brain (rule out SOL, bleed, hydrocephalus) / MRI Brain (MTA score for hippocampal atrophy, Fazekas scale for white matter changes).
* **Others (其他)**: MSU (Mid-stream urine), CXR, ECG.

## 6. Acute Management, Risk Assessment & Medico-Legal (HK Mental Health Ordinance)

### Acute Delirium & Agitation Management (急性譫妄及躁動處理)
* **Non-Pharm**: De-escalation, reorientation clocks, hearing aids/glasses, optimized sleep-wake cycle, family presence.
* **Pharm**: Treat underlying cause (e.g., Abx for UTI). If severe agitation/risk to self/others → Haloperidol (0.5mg-1mg PO/IM) or Quetiapine (12.5-25mg PO).
* **Contraindication**: Avoid BZDs (worsens delirium) UNLESS alcohol withdrawal (Delirium Tremens). Avoid Haloperidol in LBD/PDD.

### HK Mental Health Ordinance (Cap 136) Considerations (香港精神健康條例考量)
* **Part IVC (第IVC部)**: Medical/dental treatment for incapacitated adults. Allows emergency treatment in patient's best interest if they lack capacity.
* **Guardianship Board (監護委員會)**: Appoints legal guardian for welfare/accommodation decisions for adults lacking mental capacity (e.g., placement into Care and Attention Home).
* **Section 2**: Dementia falls under the legal definition of "Mental Disorder" in HK.

### Risk Assessment (風險評估)
* **Self-Harm / Accidental Harm**: Wandering, forgetting to turn off stove, medication mismanagement.
* **Harm to Others**: Aggressive outbursts (BPSD), unsafe driving.
* **Vulnerability**: Elder abuse (financial, physical, neglect) by exhausted caregivers.

## 7. Chronic Biopsychosocial Management (Pharmacotherapy, ECT & Psychotherapy)

### Pharmacotherapy (藥物治療)
* **AChE Inhibitors (乙酰膽鹼酯酶抑制劑)**: Donepezil, Rivastigmine, Galantamine. Indication: Mild-to-moderate AD/LBD. Side effects: GI upset, bradycardia, syncope.
* **NMDA Receptor Antagonist (NMDA受體拮抗劑)**: Memantine. Indication: Moderate-to-severe AD.
* **BPSD Management**: SSRIs (Sertraline, Citalopram) for depression/anxiety. Antipsychotics ONLY if severe risk/distress (black box warning).

### Psychological Interventions (心理治療)
* **Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST) (認知刺激治療)**: Evidence-based group therapy for mild-to-moderate dementia.
* **Reality Orientation (現實導向)**: Calendars, clear signage.
* **Reminiscence Therapy (懷舊治療)**: Using photos/music to evoke long-term memories and ↑ mood.

### Social Support & HK Systems (社會支援及香港醫療系統)
* **HK HA CGAT (社區老人評估小組)**: Multi-disciplinary outreach to Residential Care Homes for the Elderly (RCHEs).
* **Caregiver Support**: Psychoeducation, respite care, day care centers (DECCs/NECs).
* **Advanced Care Planning (預設醫療指示)**: Establish directives while capacity is intact.

## 8. Prognosis & Course of Illness

### Prognosis of Delirium (譫妄預後)
* **Short-term**: Usually resolves in days-weeks once underlying cause is treated.
* **Long-term**: ↑ 1-year mortality rate. ↑ Risk of future delirium. May unmask underlying undiagnosed dementia.
* **Persistent**: 20% of elderly have prolonged cognitive deficits post-delirium.

### Prognosis of Dementia (認知障礙症預後)
* **Course**: Irreversible, progressive global decline.
* **Survival**: Median survival 4-8 years post-diagnosis (depends on age/comorbidities).
* **Terminal Phase**: Dysphagia → aspiration pneumonia (leading cause of death), bed-bound state, pressure ulcers.

## 9. Complications (Psychiatric, Social & Psychotropic Drug Side Effects)

### Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia (BPSD) (認知障礙症伴發的精神行為症狀)
* **Psychiatric**: Psychosis (delusions of theft/infidelity), depression, apathy.
* **Behavioral**: Aggression, wandering, hoarding, inappropriate sexual behavior, shadowing.

### Social & Caregiver Complications (社會及照顧者併發症)
* **Caregiver Burnout**: High rates of clinical depression/anxiety in primary caregivers.
* **Financial Burden**: Cost of home-helpers, residential care, loss of income.

### Psychotropic Drug Side Effects in Elderly (精神科藥物在長者的副作用)
| Drug Class (藥物類別) | Key Side Effects in Dementia/Elderly (主要副作用) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Atypical Antipsychotics** | ↑ Risk of stroke (CVA), ↑ all-cause mortality, Parkinsonism, QTc prolongation, orthostatic hypotension. |
| **Typical Antipsychotics** | Severe Extrapyramidal Symptoms (EPS), Tardive Dyskinesia, falls. |
| **Benzodiazepines** | Paradoxical agitation, ↑ risk of falls/hip fractures, worsening of cognitive impairment, respiratory depression. |
| **Antidepressants (SSRIs)** | Hyponatremia (SIADH), GI bleeding risk, falls. |

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: How do you clinically differentiate Lewy Body Dementia from Alzheimer's? Answer: LBD presents with early visual hallucinations, fluctuating cognition, REM sleep behavior disorder, and spontaneous Parkinsonism BEFORE or within 1 year of cognitive decline.
* Trap: Prescribing Haloperidol for a delirious patient with Lewy Body Dementia or Parkinson's Disease. Pearl: Typical antipsychotics can cause severe, irreversible Parkinsonism and neuroleptic malignant syndrome in LBD. Use Quetiapine or minimize meds.
* Pitfall: Mistaking hypoactive delirium for depression during Group C clinical exams. Pearl: Always test attention (e.g., serial 7s, months backward). A depressed patient will be slow but accurate; a delirious patient will lose track entirely.
* Viva Question: What is the defining feature of vascular dementia on history? Answer: A step-wise deterioration in cognition temporarily associated with cerebrovascular events (strokes/TIAs), accompanied by focal neurological signs.
* Trap: Treating asymptomatic bacteriuria in an elderly patient with advanced dementia just because a dipstick is positive. Pearl: Only treat if there are systemic signs of infection or acute delirium, to avoid antibiotic resistance and C. diff.
* Pitfall: Assuming a patient lacks capacity for ALL decisions just because they have a diagnosis of dementia. Pearl: Capacity is decision-specific and time-specific. A patient with mild AD may lack capacity to manage complex finances but retain capacity to choose their daily meals or appoint a proxy.
