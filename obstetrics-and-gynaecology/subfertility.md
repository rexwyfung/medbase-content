# Subfertility (不孕症)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology

### Clinical Definition
* **Primary Subfertility (原發性不孕):** Failure to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse in a woman with no prior pregnancies.
* **Secondary Subfertility (繼發性不孕):** Failure to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse in a woman with $\ge1$ previous conception.

### Local Epidemiology & Demographics
* **Prevalence:** Affects ~1 in 6 couples in Hong Kong.
* **Age Factor:** ↑ maternal age exponentially ↓ natural fecundity due to ↓ oocyte quantity and quality.
* **Aetiology Breakdown:**
	* Male factor (~30%).
	* Ovulatory dysfunction (~25%).
	* Tubal pathology (~20%).
	* Unexplained subfertility (~25%).

## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy (Maternal-Fetal Physiology)

### Ovulatory Dysfunction (排卵障礙)
* **Hypothalamic-Pituitary Axis:** ↓ GnRH pulsatility → ↓ FSH/LH secretion → follicular arrest and anovulation.
* **Ovarian Axis:** Hyperandrogenism combined with insulin resistance → peripheral conversion of androgens → disrupted follicular maturation [e.g., **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome** (多囊卵巢綜合症)].
* **Ovarian Reserve:** Accelerated apoptosis of primordial follicles → ↑ FSH and ↓ AMH [e.g., **Premature Ovarian Insufficiency** (早發性卵巢功能不全)].

### Tubal & Pelvic Pathophysiology
* **Infectious Damage:** Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID) → mucosal destruction → loss of ciliated epithelium → hydrosalpinx or tubal occlusion.
* **Endometriosis (子宮內膜異位症):** Ectopic endometrium → chronic pelvic inflammation → distorted pelvic anatomy and dense adhesions → impaired ovum pickup.

### Uterine & Cervical Factors
* **Implantation Failure:** Submucosal fibroids or polyps physically distort the uterine cavity and alter endometrial receptivity.
* **Asherman Syndrome:** Intrauterine synechiae from previous curettage → destruction of stratum basalis → amenorrhoea and impaired implantation.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (History, Abdominal, Speculum & Bimanual Exam Findings ONLY)

### Maternal History
* **Menstrual:** LMP, cycle length, cycle regularity, severity of dysmenorrhoea.
* **Sexual:** Frequency of intercourse, dyspareunia, libido.
* **Obstetric:** G/P, history of miscarriages, terminations, or ectopic pregnancies.
* **Medical/Surgical:** Previous PID, pelvic surgeries (e.g., appendicectomy, cystectomy), known STI history.

### Paternal History
* **Sexual/Medical:** Erectile dysfunction, ejaculatory failure, prior paternity, undescended testes, mumps orchitis.
* **Lifestyle:** Smoking, excessive alcohol, use of tight underwear, occupational heat exposure.

### Abdominal Examination
* **General:** BMI assessment (obesity associated with PCOS, low BMI with hypothalamic amenorrhoea).
* **Dermatological:** Hirsutism, severe acne, alopecia, acanthosis nigricans.
* **Palpation:** Surgical scars, lower abdominal tenderness, palpable pelvic masses (fibroids or large cysts).

### Speculum Examination
* **Cervix:**
	* Cervicitis.
	* Visible polyps.
	* Anatomical defects.
	* Severe ectropion.
* **Vagina:**
	* Abnormal discharge (+ve/-ve).
	* Structural anomalies (e.g., transverse vaginal septum).

### Bimanual Examination
* **Uterus:** Size, shape, mobility, version (a fixed retroverted uterus suggests severe endometriosis).
* **Adnexa:** Palpable masses, unilateral or bilateral tenderness.
* **Pouch of Douglas:** Nodularity or tenderness (highly indicative of deep infiltrating endometriosis).

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging

### WHO Classification of Ovulatory Disorders
| Class | Description | Hormone Profile | Clinical Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WHO I** | Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism | ↓ FSH, ↓ LH, ↓ Oestradiol | Anorexia, Kallmann syndrome |
| **WHO II** | Normogonadotropic normoestrogenic | Normal FSH/LH/Oestradiol | **PCOS** (多囊卵巢綜合症) |
| **WHO III** | Hypergonadotropic hypoestrogenic | ↑ FSH, ↑ LH, ↓ Oestradiol | **POI** (早發性卵巢功能不全) |

### ASRM Endometriosis Staging
| Stage | Severity | Morphological Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage I** | Minimal | Isolated superficial implants, no significant adhesions |
| **Stage II** | Mild | Superficial implants <5cm in aggregate, no dense adhesions |
| **Stage III** | Moderate | Deep implants, small endometriomas, filmy adhesions |
| **Stage IV** | Severe | Deep implants, large endometriomas, dense adhesions, cul-de-sac obliteration |

## 5. Key Investigations (Labs, Specific Ultrasound Criteria & CTG Tracings)

### Female Baseline Hormonal Labs
* **Day 2-3 FSH, LH & Oestradiol:** Assess baseline ovarian function.
* **Anti-Mullerian Hormone (AMH):** Marker of ovarian reserve (independent of menstrual cycle day).
* **Day 21 Progesterone:** Confirms ovulation (>30 nmol/L = ovulatory in a 28-day cycle).
* **Ancillary Labs:** TFTs, Prolactin, Rubella IgG status.

### Tubal & Uterine Investigations
* **Hysterosalpingogram (HSG):** Fluoroscopic contrast imaging → assesses tubal patency and gross uterine cavity contour.
* **HyCoSy:** Ultrasound contrast imaging → assesses tubal patency dynamically.
* **Diagnostic Laparoscopy & Dye Test:** Gold standard → direct visualization of endometriosis, pelvic adhesions, and direct +ve/-ve tubal spill.

### Specific Ultrasound Criteria (Pelvic USG)
* **Antral Follicle Count (AFC):** Transvaginal USG on Day 2-3. Normal reserve = 10-20 total follicles.
* **PCOS Criteria:** $\ge20$ follicles (2-9mm) per ovary OR ovarian volume >10mL.
* **Structural Anomalies:** Identifies hydrosalpinx, endometriomas (ground-glass echogenicity), and submucosal fibroids.

### Male Factor Investigations (WHO 2021 Semen Analysis Criteria)
* **Volume:** $\ge1.4$ mL.
* **Concentration:** $\ge16$ million/mL.
* **Motility:** $\ge30\%$ progressive motility.
* **Morphology:** $\ge4\%$ normal forms.

## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization (Pharmacology, Steroids, Resuscitation)

### Ovulation Induction (OI) - First Line
* **Letrozole:**
	* Aromatase inhibitor.
	* 1st line for PCOS. 2.5mg PO daily D2-D6.
	* ↓ peripheral oestrogen → ↑ endogenous FSH release.
* **Clomiphene Citrate:**
	* Selective Oestrogen Receptor Modulator.
	* 50mg PO daily D2-D6.
	* Blocks central -ve feedback → ↑ FSH.

### Ovulation Induction (OI) - Second Line
* **Gonadotropins:** Subcutaneous FSH injections. Requires strict USG monitoring to prevent **Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS)** (卵巢過度刺激綜合症).

### Adjuvant Pharmacotherapy
* **Metformin:** Insulin sensitizer for PCOS with high BMI. Improves ovulatory response and ↓ miscarriage risk.
* **Dopamine Agonists:** Bromocriptine or Cabergoline for hyperprolactinaemia.
* **Progesterone Supplementation:** Vaginal pessaries used for luteal phase support during ART cycles.

## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm (Timing/Mode of Delivery OR Gynaecological Surgery)

### Artificial Insemination (IUI)
* **Indications:**
	* Mild male factor.
	* Unexplained subfertility.
	* Mild endometriosis.
* **Algorithm:** Ovarian stimulation → USG monitoring → trigger ovulation → washed/concentrated sperm injected directly into uterine cavity via catheter.

### In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) (體外受精)
* **Indications:**
	* Bilateral tubal blockage.
	* Severe endometriosis.
	* Prolonged unexplained subfertility.
* **Algorithm:** Controlled ovarian hyperstimulation → USG-guided transvaginal oocyte retrieval → in vitro fertilization → embryo transfer (ET) 3-5 days later.

### Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)
* **Indications:**
	* Severe male factor (oligoasthenoteratozoospermia).
	* Previous IVF fertilization failure.
* **Algorithm:** Selection of a single morphologically normal, motile sperm → direct microinjection into the oocyte cytoplasm.

### Gynaecological Surgery
* **Laparoscopic Adhesiolysis/Ablation:** Restores pelvic anatomy and destroys endometriotic implants.
* **Salpingectomy:** Removal of hydrosalpinx prior to IVF to ↑ implantation success (tubal fluid is embryotoxic).
* **Hysteroscopic Surgery:** Resection of submucosal fibroids/polyps or synechiolysis for Asherman syndrome.

## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up

### Post-ART Monitoring
* **Luteal Support:** Continue vaginal progesterone until 10-12 weeks GA if pregnancy is successfully achieved.
* **Early Viability USG:** Transvaginal USG at 6-7 weeks to confirm intrauterine gestation, fetal heart activity, and strictly exclude ectopic/heterotopic pregnancy.

### Post-Operative Care (Gynaecological Surgery)
* **Discharge Planning:** Same-day or overnight discharge depending on procedure complexity.
* **Analgesia:** Simple NSAIDs and Paracetamol.
* **Follow-up:** Review histology (endometrium, tubal specimens). Plan for natural conception attempt (usually 6-12 months) vs immediate ART depending on age and staging.

### Psychological Follow-up
* **Counselling:** Essential multidisciplinary support for couples undergoing ART. Address psychological stress, treatment fatigue, and relationship strain.

## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications

### Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS) (卵巢過度刺激綜合症)
* **Pathophysiology:** Exaggerated response to exogenous gonadotropins → ↑↑ VEGF → ↑ capillary permeability → massive fluid shift into third spaces.
* **Symptoms:** Severe ascites, pleural effusion, oliguria, hemoconcentration, thrombosis risk.
* **Management:** Immediate fluid restriction, prophylactic LMWH, strict I/O monitoring, supportive paracentesis.

### Pregnancy Complications (ART)
* **Multiple Pregnancy (多胎妊娠):**
	* Highest risk with OI and multi-embryo transfer.
	* Directly ↑ risk of preterm labour, gestational diabetes, and pre-eclampsia.
	* Mitigation via Single Embryo Transfer (SET).
* **Ectopic/Heterotopic Pregnancy (宮外孕):** ↑ risk post-IVF or with underlying tubal disease.

### Surgical & Procedure Risks
* **Oocyte Retrieval:**
	* Pelvic infection.
	* Massive intra-abdominal bleeding.
	* Bowel/bladder injury.
* **Pelvic Surgery:**
	* Post-operative adhesion formation.
	* Irreversible damage to ovarian reserve (specifically following endometrioma cystectomy).

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the most reliable biochemical predictor of ovarian reserve prior to IVF treatment? Answer: Anti-Mullerian Hormone (AMH).
* Trap: Testing Day 21 progesterone to confirm ovulation in a woman with a regular 35-day cycle. Pearl: Progesterone peaks precisely 7 days prior to the next menstruation; in a 35-day cycle, the test must be performed on Day 28.
* Pitfall: Performing IUI for severe male factor infertility or bilateral tubal blockage. IUI mandates at least one completely patent fallopian tube and an adequate post-wash motile sperm count; ICSI or IVF is strictly indicated respectively.
