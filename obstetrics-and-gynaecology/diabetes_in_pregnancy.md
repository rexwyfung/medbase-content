# Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (妊娠糖尿病)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology

### Definition of Gestational Diabetes
* **Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (妊娠糖尿病) (GDM)**: Hyperglycemia first detected at any time during pregnancy.
* **Diabetes Mellitus in Pregnancy (妊娠期糖尿病)**: Overt DM first recognized during pregnancy.

### Local Epidemiology & Context
* Prevalence: **10-14%** in Hong Kong.
* Prevalence correlates directly with population rates of Type 2 DM.
* Diagnosis typically via screening in late 2nd or early 3rd trimester.

## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy

### Maternal Pathophysiology
* Progressive ↑ **Insulin Resistance (胰島素抵抗)** (up to **50%** ↑ in late pregnancy).
* Driven by ↑ maternal adipose tissue & placental hormones (e.g., human placental lactogen, estrogen, progesterone, cortisol).

### Fetal Pathophysiology
* Maternal insulin does NOT cross the placenta.
* Maternal hyperglycemia crosses placenta → fetal hyperglycemia.
* Fetal pancreas responds from **11-12 weeks** GA → **Fetal Hyperinsulinemia (胎兒高胰島素血症)**.
* Hyperinsulinemia drives excessive fetal growth & fat deposition → **Macrosomia (巨大兒)**.

## 3. Clinical Assessment

### High-Risk Maternal History 
* Past Obstetric Hx: Previous **GDM (妊娠糖尿病)**, previous macrosomic baby, recurrent miscarriage, **Pre-eclampsia (先兆子癇)**, unexplained stillbirth/NND/congenital malformations.
* Medical Hx: **Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (多囊卵巢綜合症) (PCOS)**, long-term corticosteroid use.
* Family Hx: 1st-degree relative with DM.

### Current Pregnancy Assessment
* Maternal age $\ge$ **35 yrs**.
* Pre-pregnancy BMI $\ge$ **25**.
* Multiple pregnancy.
* Symptoms: Usually asymptomatic, but may present with polyuria, polydipsia, or recurrent vulvovaginitis.

### Clinical Examination Findings
* General: Obesity, hypertension.
* Abdominal: Symphyseal-fundal height (SFH) > expected for GA (suggestive of macrosomia or polyhydramnios).

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging

### Diagnostic Categorization Criteria
| Classification | Fasting Plasma Glucose | 1-hr Post 75g OGTT | 2-hr Post 75g OGTT | Random Plasma Glucose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overt DM in Pregnancy** | $\ge$ **7.0** mmol/L | N/A | $\ge$ **11.1** mmol/L | $\ge$ **11.1** mmol/L (+ symptoms) |
| **GDM (WHO 2013 Criteria)** | **5.1 - 6.9** mmol/L | $\ge$ **10.0** mmol/L | **8.5 - 11.0** mmol/L | N/A |

## 5. Key Investigations

### Biochemical Testing
* **Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (口服葡萄糖耐量測試) (OGTT)**: 75g glucose load at **24-28 weeks** GA (universal screening ideal; selective if resources limited).
* Urine: Glycosuria (2+ on 1 occasion OR 1+ on 2 occasions prompts OGTT).
* Blood: **HbA1c** (Target $\le$ **6.5%**).

### Specific Ultrasound Criteria
* Fetal biometry: Estimated Fetal Weight (EFW) $\ge$ 90th centile (**LGA**).
* Amniotic fluid: **Polyhydramnios (羊水過多)**.

### CTG Tracings
* Antepartum **CTG (胎心監護)** indicated for monitoring fetal well-being in cases of suboptimal control or suspected complications.

## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization

### Glycemic Target Protocols
* Fasting glucose: $\le$ **5.3 mmol/L**.
* 2-hour postprandial glucose: $\le$ **6.8 mmol/L**.

### Medical Nutrition Therapy
* Dietitian referral mandatory.
* Non-obese: **30-35 kcal/kg/day**.
* Obese: **25 kcal/kg/day**.
* Multi-fetal: Add **300 kcal/day** per fetus.
* Structure: 3 main meals + snacks.
* Bedtime snack crucial to prevent nocturnal hypoglycemia and starvation ketosis.
* Regular exercise (e.g., 30 mins post-meal walking).

### Pharmacological Interventions
* **Insulin (胰島素)**: 
  * Indicated if diet fails after **2 weeks**.
  * Start immediately at diagnosis if fasting $\ge$ **7.0 mmol/L** (or 6.0-6.9 mmol/L + macrosomia/polyhydramnios).
  * Safe in pregnancy. Regimen: Short-acting pre-meal + Medium-acting at bedtime.
* **Oral Hypoglycaemic Agents (OHA)**: 
  * **Metformin (二甲雙胍)** preferred.
  * Safe and effective alternative/adjunct to insulin in 2nd/3rd trimesters.

## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm

### Timing of Delivery Protocol
| Clinical Status | Recommended Timing of Delivery |
| :--- | :--- |
| Diet control ONLY + No complications | **~40 weeks** |
| **Insulin** control + No complications | **38-39 weeks** |
| Suboptimal control OR Fetal Complications | **< 38 weeks** (Individualized) |

### Mode of Delivery Principles
* Estimated Fetal Weight $\ge$ **4.0 - 4.5 kg**: Elective **LSCS (剖腹產)** to avoid **Shoulder Dystocia (肩難產)**.
* Otherwise, aim for vaginal delivery.

### Intrapartum Management
* Diet-controlled: No specific medical measures required during labour.
* Insulin-controlled:
  * Hourly capillary glucose monitoring.
  * IV Insulin + **10% Dextrose** + **KCl** infusion adjusted via strict sliding scale.
  * Continuous fetal CTG monitoring.

## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up

### Immediate Post-partum Glycemic Care
* Placental delivery → rapid ↓ placental hormones → immediate resolution of insulin resistance.
* Diet-controlled: Resume normal diet immediately.
* Insulin-controlled: Cease insulin. Monitor capillary glucose for **24-72 hours** to exclude pre-existing DM.

### Long-term Follow-up & Contraception
* Perform **OGTT** or fasting glucose at **6-8 weeks** postpartum to confirm resolution.
* Yearly fasting glucose/OGTT advised (↑ lifetime risk of Type 2 DM).
* Encourage **Breastfeeding (母乳餵哺)**: Improves maternal glucose metabolism and confers neonatal benefits.
* Contraception: No specific contraindications based on GDM status alone (consider co-morbidities like obesity).

## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications

### Maternal Complications
* **Pre-eclampsia (先兆子癇)** & Pregnancy-Induced Hypertension (PIH).
* Genitourinary infections (Vulvovaginal candidiasis, UTI).
* Cephalopelvic disproportion (CPD) → ↑ operative delivery risk.
* High risk of developing Type 2 DM in the future.

### Fetal & Neonatal Complications
* **Macrosomia (巨大兒)** → Birth trauma, **Shoulder Dystocia (肩難產)**.
* **Polyhydramnios (羊水過多)**.
* Prematurity.
* **Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS)**: Fetal hyperinsulinemia suppresses surfactant production.
* Neonatal metabolic crises: Hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, hypomagnesemia, polycythemia, jaundice.
* Stillbirth (associated with suboptimal glycemic control).
* ↑ risk of childhood obesity and DM.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: What is the underlying mechanism of respiratory distress syndrome in infants of diabetic mothers? Answer: Fetal hyperinsulinemia directly suppresses pulmonary surfactant synthesis, delaying fetal lung maturation despite advancing gestational age.
* Trap: Assuming oral hypoglycaemic agents are strictly contraindicated in all pregnancies. Pearl: Metformin crosses the placenta but is actively used and preferred in Hong Kong HA protocols as a safe alternative or adjunct to insulin in GDM management during the 2nd and 3rd trimesters.
* Pitfall: Continuing a patient's antenatal insulin dose into the immediate postpartum period. The delivery of the placenta causes an immediate and precipitous drop in anti-insulin hormones. Continuing the antenatal dose will cause severe maternal hypoglycemia; insulin must be stopped or drastically reduced, with strict capillary glucose monitoring for 24-72 hours.
