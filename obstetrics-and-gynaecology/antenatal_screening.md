# Antenatal Screening (產前篩查)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology

### Clinical Objectives & HA/MCHC Visit Schedule
* **Antenatal Screening (產前篩查)**: Surveillance from booking ($8–13\text{ wks}$) to delivery to detect asymptomatic maternal/fetal complications → ↓ morbidity/mortality.
* **HA & MCHC Shared Care**:
  * Booking–$28\text{ wks}$: q4w.
  * $28–36\text{ wks}$: q2–3w.
  * $\ge 36\text{ wks}$: Weekly.

### Hong Kong Local Epidemiology
* **Advanced Maternal Age (高齡妊娠 / AMA)**: $\ge 35\text{ yrs}$ at EDD → ↑ aneuploidy, **Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (妊娠糖尿病 / GDM)**, **Pre-eclampsia (先兆子癇 / PET)**, **Stillbirth (死產)**.
* **Group B Streptococcus (B型鏈球菌 / GBS)**: Universal culture screen since 2012; local colonization **21.8%**.
* **GDM**: Local prevalence **10–14%**.
* **Thalassaemia (地中海貧血)**: High Southern Chinese carrier rate (**SEA deletion** $\alpha$-trait, $\beta$-trait).
* **Rhesus (Rh) Negativity**: Rare in Chinese ($<1\%$).


## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy (Maternal-Fetal Physiology)

### Placental Aneuploidy & Pre-eclampsia Biomarkers
* **Cell-Free Fetal DNA (cffDNA)**: Apoptotic placental **Syncytiotrophoblasts (合胞體滋養層)** shed into maternal blood from $10\text{ wks}$ (**NIPT [無創產前DNA檢測]** basis; discovered by Prof. Dennis Lo).
* **$1^{\text{st}}$ Trimester ($11–13^{+6}\text{ wks}$)**:
	* **Free $\beta$-hCG** ($\uparrow$ in T21, $\downarrow$ in T18/T13).
	* **PAPP-A** ($\downarrow$ in T21/T18/T13 & placental insufficiency).
* **$2^{\text{nd}}$ Trimester ($14–19^{+6}\text{ wks}$)**:
	* **AFP** ($\downarrow$ in T21, $\uparrow$ in **Open Neural Tube Defects [開放性神經管缺陷 / NTD]** / abdominal wall defects).
	* **$uE_3$** ($\downarrow$ in T21/T18).
	* **Inhibin A** ($\uparrow$ in T21).
* **PET Pathophysiology**: Defective extravillous trophoblast invasion of **Spiral Arteries (螺旋動脈)** → high uteroplacental resistance ($\uparrow$ **UtA-PI**) & $\downarrow$ **PlGF** / **PAPP-A**.

### Thalassaemia & Gestational Metabolic Physiology
* **Homozygous $\alpha^0$-Thalassaemia (`--/--`, Hb Bart's Hydrops Fetalis [巴氏水腫胎])**:
	* Both parents carry `--/aa` (**SEA deletion**) → 25% risk.
	* $\gamma_4$ tetramers cannot release $\text{O}_2$ → severe fetal hypoxia, high-output heart failure, hydrops, intrauterine death + maternal **Ballantyne Syndrome (鏡像綜合徵)** (severe early PET from placentomegaly).
* **$\beta$-Thalassaemia Major**:
	* Normal in utero (predominant **HbF**, $\alpha_2\gamma_2$).
	* Manifests $3–6\text{ months}$ postnatally.
* **GDM Axis**:
	* Placental **hPL**, **Oestrogen**, **Progesterone**, **Cortisol** → $\uparrow$ insulin resistance.
	* Maternal glucose crosses placenta (insulin does not) → fetal hyperinsulinaemia from $11–12\text{ wks}$ → **Macrosomia (巨大兒)** & $\downarrow$ surfactant.


## 3. Clinical Assessment (History, Abdominal, Speculum & Bimanual Exam Findings ONLY)

### Booking History & Antenatal Enquiries
* **Booking ($8–13\text{ wks}$)**:
	* Age ($\ge 35\text{ yrs}$ = AMA).
	* LMP/cycle/contraception.
	* G/P.
	* Prior preterm birth/LSCS/PET/GDM/macrosomia/stillbirth/GBS baby.
* **Medical/Drug/Family**:
	* DM, HTN.
	* Renal/autoimmune (**SLE/APS**).
	* VTE.
	* Teratogens (**Warfarin, Sodium Valproate, MMF, Tetracycline, ACEI/ARB**).
	* **Penicillin** allergy.
	* Partner **Thalassaemia** status.
	* 3-generation pedigree.
* **Subsequent Visits**:
	* **Fetal Movement (胎動 / FM)** if $>20\text{ wks}$ (felt from $18–20\text{ wks}$).
	* PV bleeding/leaking.
	* Contractions.
	* PET symptoms (headache, visual disturbance, epigastric pain).

### Physical, Abdominal & Speculum Examination
* **Vitals & Anthropometry**:
	* Booking **BMI** ($\ge 25\text{ kg/m}^2$ = GDM risk; $\ge 35\text{ kg/m}^2$ = PET risk).
	* **BP** every visit ($\ge 140/90\text{ mmHg}$ = HTN; $\ge 160/110\text{ mmHg}$ = severe HTN).
* **Abdominal Palpation**:
	* Scars/masses.
	* **Symphyseal-Fundal Height (SFH / 宮底高度)** in $\text{cm}$ if $>24\text{ wks}$ ($\approx \text{GA} \pm 2\text{ cm}$).
	* **Leopold Manoeuvres** ($>30\text{ wks}$) for lie/presentation (**Breech [臀先露]**: hard round ballotable head at fundus).
	* **Doptone** FHR ($\ge 12\text{ wks}$).
* **Speculum & Swabs**:
	* Speculum at booking ONLY if **Cervical Smear (子宮頸抹片)** due or PV bleeding/discharge.
	* At $35–37\text{ wks}$, take **Low Vaginal Swab (LVS)** + **Rectal Swab** without speculum for GBS.


## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging (e.g., FIGO if applicable)

### Pre-eclampsia Risk Stratification (Aspirin Criteria)
| Risk Category | Maternal / Obstetric Factors | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Risk** ($\ge 1$) | Prior pregnancy HTN, **CKD**, **SLE/APS**, Type 1/2 DM, **Chronic HTN** | Start **Aspirin** |
| **Moderate Risk** ($\ge 2$) | **Primiparity (初產)**, Age $\ge 40\text{ yrs}$, interval $>10\text{ yrs}$, **BMI $\ge 35\text{ kg/m}^2$**, FHx PET, **Multiple Pregnancy (多胞胎)** | Start **Aspirin** |
| **1st Trimester Screen** | High-risk combined **MAP** + **UtA-PI** + **PlGF** / **PAPP-A** | Start **Aspirin** |

### Microcytic Hypochromic Anaemia (MCV <80 fL) & Hyperglycaemia (WHO Criteria)
| Condition                       | Key Diagnostic Criteria                                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Iron Deficiency Anaemia**     | $\text{MCV} <80\text{ fL}$ + **$\downarrow$ Serum Ferritin ($<30\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$)**                                   |
| **$\alpha$-Thalassaemia Trait** | $\text{MCV} <80\text{ fL}$ + normal ferritin + normal $\text{HbA}_2$ ($<3.5\%$) + **HbH inclusions** / **SEA deletion PCR** |
| **$\beta$-Thalassaemia Trait**  | $\text{MCV} <80\text{ fL}$ + normal ferritin + **$\uparrow \text{HbA}_2$ ($\ge 3.5\%$)**                                    |
| **GDM (妊娠糖尿病)**                 | **75g OGTT**: Fasting **$5.1–6.9$**, 1-hr **$\ge 10.0$**, or 2-hr **$8.5–11.0\text{ mmol/L}$**                              |
| **DM in Pregnancy**             | Fasting **$\ge 7.0$**, 2-hr **$\ge 11.1$**, or Random **$\ge 11.1\text{ mmol/L}$** + symptoms                               |

### Down Syndrome Screening Modalities
| Modality | Window | Components | T21 DR / FPR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$1^{\text{st}}$ Trimester Combined** | $11–13^{+6}\text{ wks}$ | Age + **NT** + **Free $\beta$-hCG** + **PAPP-A** | $\sim 85–90\%$ / $5\%$ |
| **$2^{\text{nd}}$ Trimester Quadruple** | $14–19^{+6}\text{ wks}$ | Age + **AFP** + **hCG** + **$uE_3$** + **Inhibin A** | $\sim 80\%$ / $5\%$ |
| **NIPT (cffDNA)** | $\ge 10\text{ wks}$ | Maternal plasma **cell-free placental DNA** | **$>99\%$** / **$<0.1\%$** |


## 5. Key Investigations (Labs, Specific Ultrasound Criteria & CTG Tracings)

### Antenatal Investigation Schedule in Hong Kong
| Gestation                       | Investigations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **$8–13\text{ wks}$ (Booking)** | - **CBP** ($\text{Hb}$, $\text{MCV} <80\text{ fL}$), **ABO/Rh & Antibody screen**<br>- **HBsAg** (if +ve $\rightarrow$ **HBeAg**, **HBV DNA**), **Syphilis (EIA/VDRL)**, **Anti-HIV**, **Rubella IgG**<br>- Urine **albumin/glucose** (every visit)<br>- **75g OGTT** (if high risk) or fasting glucose |
| **$11–13^{+6}\text{ wks}$**     | - **CRL** dating + **NT** + **$\beta$-hCG / PAPP-A** (or **NIPT** $\ge 10\text{ wks}$)<br>- Twin chorionicity (**$\lambda$-sign** = DCDA; **T-sign** = MCDA)                                                                                                                                            |
| **$18–22\text{ wks}$**          | **Fetal Anomaly Scan (結構性超聲波)** ($70\%$ major anomaly detection) + placental localization (repeat at $30–32\text{ wks}$ if low-lying)                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **$24–28\text{ wks}$**          | Repeat **CBP** + **75g OGTT**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **$35–37\text{ wks}$**          | **LVS + Rectal Swab** for **GBS** culture                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

### Thalassaemia Screening & Diagnostic Cascade
- Maternal $\text{MCV} <80\text{ fL}$ $\rightarrow$ check partner $\text{MCV}$ + maternal **Hb electrophoresis**, **HbH inclusion**, **Ferritin**.
- Normal ferritin + normal $\text{HbA}_2$ $\rightarrow$ presume $\alpha$-trait $\rightarrow$ DNA test for **SEA deletion**.
- If both parents carry same trait (25% major risk) $\rightarrow$ invasive prenatal diagnosis.

### Rhesus (RhD) Incompatibility & Alloimmunisation Cascade
- Non-invasive fetal **RHD genotyping** via maternal plasma cffDNA.
- If sensitized (+ve Anti-D) $\rightarrow$ serial titres + fetal **MCA-PSV Doppler** ($\text{MoM} >1.5$ = fetal anaemia).

### Invasive Prenatal Genetic Cascade (HKSAR 2022)
- **Invasive Testing**: **CVS (絨毛膜活檢)** at **$11–13^{+6}\text{ wks}$** (never $<10\text{ wks}$) or **Amniocentesis (抽羊水)** at **$\ge 15\text{ wks}$**.
* **HKSAR Genetic Lab Cascade**:
  * **QF-PCR (RAT)**: Rapid screen for T13, T18, T21, X/Y & **Maternal Cell Contamination (MCC)**.
  * If QF-PCR abnormal $\rightarrow$ **Traditional Karyotyping** (exclude **Robertsonian/Reciprocal Translocations**).
  * If QF-PCR normal $\rightarrow$ **Chromosomal Microarray (CMA / 基因晶片)** for microdeletions/duplications.
  * If CMA normal + structural anomalies $\rightarrow$ **WES / WGS (全外顯子/全基因組測序)**.


### Third-Trimester Ultrasound & CTG Indications
* **Transvaginal Cervical Length ($14–24\text{ wks}$)**: Screen if prior preterm birth/mid-trimester loss (**$<25\text{ mm}$** = short cervix).
* **FGR/PET Surveillance**: Serial USG (**EFW, AFI/DVP, Umbilical Artery Doppler** for **AEDF/REDF**).
* **CTG (胎心宮縮圖)**: Indicated $\ge 28\text{ wks}$ ONLY for high-risk (DFM, PET, FGR, GDM on insulin, APH, post-term).


## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization (Pharmacology, Steroids, Resuscitation)

### Supplements, Teratogen Switching & PET/VTE Prophylaxis
* **Folic Acid (葉酸)**:
	* **$400\text{ }\mu\text{g}$ PO daily** (pre-conception to $12\text{ wks}$).
	* **$5\text{ mg}$ PO daily** if high NTD risk (DM, AEDs, prior NTD) or **Thalassaemia trait** (continue throughout pregnancy).
* **Medication Switch**:
	* Switch **ACEI/ARB/Thiazides** to **Labetalol / Nifedipine / Methyldopa**.
	* Switch **Warfarin** to **LMWH**.
	* Avoid **Sodium Valproate**.
* **PET Prophylaxis**: **Low-dose Aspirin $75–150\text{ mg}$ PO ON** from **$12–16\text{ wks}$ to $36\text{ wks}$**.
* **VTE Prophylaxis**: **LMWH (Enoxaparin)** SC per risk score.

### Rhesus Anti-D & Maternal Infection Prophylaxis
* **Anti-D Ig (Unsensitized Rh-ve Mother)**:
	* Routine at **$28\text{ wks}$** (full dose) or **$28$ & $34\text{ wks}$** (half dose each) + within **$72\text{ hrs}$ postpartum**,
	* AND after sensitizing events (heavy $1^{\text{st}}$ trimester bleeding, APH, trauma, CVS/amniocentesis, **ECV**; use **Kleihauer test** $>20\text{ wks}$).
* **Hepatitis B (乙型肝炎)**: High viral load (**HBV DNA $>200,000\text{ IU/mL}$**) $\rightarrow$ maternal **Tenofovir $300\text{ mg}$ PO daily** from **$28\text{ wks}$** + neonatal **HBIG + HBV Vaccine** at birth.
* **Syphilis (梅毒) & HIV**:
	* IM **Penicillin** for syphilis.
	* **cART** for HIV.
* **GBS Intrapartum Antibiotic Prophylaxis (IAP)**:
  * **Give IAP Without Swab**: Prior baby with invasive GBS; **GBS bacteriuria** in current pregnancy.
  * **Give IAP if Swab +ve ($35–37\text{ wks}$)**: Except elective LSCS with intact membranes.
  * **Give IAP if GBS Unknown in Labour**: Preterm $<37\text{ wks}$, **ROM $>18\text{ hrs}$**, or intrapartum fever **$>38^\circ\text{C}$**.
  * **Regimen**: **IV Penicillin G**. If penicillin-allergic $\rightarrow$ check susceptibility for **Clindamycin / Erythromycin**; if resistant $\rightarrow$ **IV Vancomycin**.
* **Vaccination**:
	* **Pertussis (dTap)** at **$26–34\text{ wks}$** (passive fetal IgG).
	* **Influenza** & **COVID-19** at any GA.
	* **MMR** contraindicated (give postpartum if Rubella IgG -ve).


## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm (Timing/Mode of Delivery OR Gynaecological Surgery)

### Prenatal Diagnosis & Preterm Birth Prevention Algorithm
* **Positive NIPT**: Screening test ONLY $\rightarrow$ must confirm via **CVS / Amniocentesis** before **Termination of Pregnancy (人工流產 / TOP)** ($<24\text{ wks}$ legal limit in HK).
* **$\text{NT} \ge 3.5\text{ mm}$ or USG Structural Anomaly**: Bypass NIPT $\rightarrow$ direct **CVS / Amniocentesis** for **QF-PCR + CMA**.
* **Short Cervix ($<25\text{ mm}$) / Cervical Incompetence**: Vaginal **Progesterone** or **Cervical Cerclage (宮頸環扎)**.

### Delivery Timing
- **Post-term**:
	* Optional **Membrane Sweeping** at $40\text{ wks}$ (primip) / $41\text{ wks}$ (multip).
	* **IOL (催產)** at **$41\text{ wks}$**.
- **GDM**:
	* Diet-controlled $\rightarrow$ **$40\text{ wks}$**.
	* On Insulin $\rightarrow$ **$38–39\text{ wks}$**.
	* **Macrosomia ($\ge 4.0–4.5\text{ kg}$)** $\rightarrow$ **Elective LSCS**.
* **Twins**:
	* **DCDA** at **$37–38\text{ wks}$**.
	* **MCDA** at **$36–37\text{ wks}$**.
	* **MCMA** by **Elective LSCS at $32–34\text{ wks}$**.

### Malpresentation Algorithm (37 wks Review)
* **Persistent Breech ($\ge 37\text{ wks}$, 3% incidence)**:
	* Exclude placenta praevia/anomaly via USG $\rightarrow$ offer **External Cephalic Version (外倒轉術 / ECV)** at $37\text{ wks}$ vs **Elective LSCS**.
* **Persistent Transverse / Oblique Lie (Shoulder Presentation, 1 in 200)**:
	* **Admit at $37\text{ wks}$** (risk of **Cord Prolapse [臍帶脫垂]** if ROM).
	* At $39\text{ wks}$, offer **ECV $\pm$ Stabilizing IOL** (amniotomy + oxytocin); if failed $\rightarrow$ **Elective LSCS**.
* **Previous 1 LSCS**:
	* Counsel **VBAC** ($72–76\%$ success; $0.2–0.7\%$ scar rupture) vs **Elective Repeat LSCS**.
	* Previous **Classical CS** (1% rupture) $\rightarrow$ **Elective LSCS**.


## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up

### Immediate Postpartum Care & 6–8 Weeks Review
* **Immediate**:
	* Neonatal **HBIG + HBV vaccine** (if HBsAg +ve).
	* Maternal **Anti-D Ig** $<72\text{ hrs}$ (if Rh-ve).
	* **MMR vaccine** (if Rubella non-immune).
	* **LMWH** per VTE score.
	* Stop **Methyldopa** (switch to **Enalapril / Nifedipine / Labetalol**).
* **6–8 Weeks Visit**:
  * **GDM**:
	  * Stop insulin at birth.
	  * **Fasting glucose or 75g OGTT at $6–8\text{ wks}$** + **yearly** screen for Type 2 DM.
  * **PET / Gestational HTN**: Check BP & **Urine PCR at $6–8\text{ wks}$** (refer physician if persistent).
  * **Routine**:
	  * **Cervical Smear** (if due).
	  * **Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS)** screen (HK Chinese cutoff **$\ge 11$**).

### Postpartum Contraception & Genetic Counselling
* **Contraception**:
	* Start by **$3\text{ wks}$ postpartum** (earliest ovulation in non-breastfeeding).
	* **Lactation Amenorrhoea Method (LAM)** requires exclusive breastfeeding + amenorrhoea + $<6\text{ months}$.
	* Safe in breastfeeding: **POP, DMPA, Mirena, IUCD, barrier** (avoid **COCP**).
* **Future Pregnancy**: Offer **Pre-implantation Genetic Testing (植入前遺傳診斷 / PGT)** or early **CVS** for thalassaemia/genetic carrier couples.


## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications

### Procedure & Condition-Specific Complications
* **CVS / Amniocentesis**: **$0.5–1\%$ miscarriage**, PPROM, chorioamnionitis, Rh sensitization, **limb reduction defects** (if CVS $<10\text{ wks}$).
* **ECV**: Fetal distress/death, **Placental Abruption (胎盤早剝)**, PROM/preterm labour, PV bleeding.
* **Unscreened Conditions**:
  * **Hb Bart's Hydrops**: Fetal death, maternal **Ballantyne Syndrome (severe PET)**, polyhydramnios, **Primary PPH**.
  * **Infections**: Neonatal **GBS sepsis/meningitis** ($5–20\%$ mortality), chronic HBV, congenital syphilis/rubella.
  * **GDM**: PET, **Macrosomia**, **Shoulder Dystocia (肩難產)**, neonatal **RDS**, hypoglycaemia, stillbirth.


## 10. Examiner's Pearls (High-yield viva questions and common pitfalls)

* Viva Question: A pregnant woman at 10 weeks has MCV 72 fL, normal ferritin, and normal HbA2 (2.6%). What is the diagnosis and next step? Answer: Presumed alpha-thalassaemia trait. Check partner's MCV immediately and perform alpha-globin DNA testing for the SEA deletion if the partner is also microcytic (<80 fL) to exclude a 25% risk of Hb Bart's hydrops fetalis.
* Viva Question: Can a woman with a positive NIPT for Trisomy 21 proceed directly to termination of pregnancy? Answer: No. NIPT is a screening test measuring placental cell-free DNA and can have false positives (e.g., confined placental mosaicism); it must be confirmed via invasive testing (CVS or amniocentesis) before TOP.
* Viva Question: How do you manage a fetus with Nuchal Translucency (NT) of 4.2 mm at 12 weeks? Answer: Since NT is 3.5 mm or greater, bypass NIPT and proceed directly to invasive testing (CVS or amniocentesis) for QF-PCR and Chromosomal Microarray (CMA), plus a detailed anomaly scan at 18 to 22 weeks.
* Viva Question: Which women require intrapartum GBS antibiotics without needing a 35 to 37 week swab? Answer: Women with a previous baby affected by invasive GBS disease or women with GBS bacteriuria during the current pregnancy.
* Trap: Assuming a negative HbH inclusion test rules out alpha-thalassaemia trait when MCV is <80 fL and ferritin/HbA2 are normal. Pearl: Always assume alpha-thalassaemia trait and test for the SEA deletion if the partner also has low MCV.
* Trap: Giving the MMR vaccine antenatally to a Rubella non-immune mother. Pearl: MMR is a live vaccine contraindicated in pregnancy; administer it postpartum before discharge.
* Pitfall: Performing CVS before 10 weeks gestation. This causes fetal transverse limb reduction defects; always perform CVS between 11 and 13+6 weeks.
* Pitfall: Giving clindamycin or erythromycin for GBS prophylaxis in penicillin-allergic women without checking sensitivities. Due to high local macrolide resistance, check swab susceptibility first and use IV Vancomycin if resistant or unknown.
