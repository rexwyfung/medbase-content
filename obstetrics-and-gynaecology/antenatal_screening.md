# Antenatal Screening (產前篩查)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology

### Definition & Objectives of Antenatal Care
* **Antenatal Care (產前檢查)**: Systematic scheduled medical supervision of pregnant women to optimize maternal-fetal health.
* **Core goals**: Identify high-risk factors, prevent/detect complications early (e.g., FGR, Pre-eclampsia), provide reassurance & plan delivery.

### Hong Kong Hospital Authority (HA) Antenatal Schedule
* **Booking visit**: 8-13 wks GA.
* **Routine intervals**: 4-weekly to 28 wks → 2-3 weekly to 36 wks → weekly from 36 wks to term.
* **Reduced frequency**: Cost-effective for low-risk, but ↑ maternal anxiety.

## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy (Maternal-Fetal Physiology)

### Fetal Genetic Pathophysiology
* **Aneuploidy**: Abnormal chromosome number (e.g., Trisomy 21). Risk ↑ exponentially with **Advanced Maternal Age (高齡妊娠)** (≥35 yrs at delivery).
* **Neural Tube Defects**: Failure of embryonic neural tube closure; ↓ risk with periconceptional folate.

### Maternal-Fetal Isoimmunization
* **Pathogenesis**: Maternal Rh -ve / Fetal Rh +ve → transplacental fetal RBC hemorrhage → maternal **Anti-D** IgG production → crosses placenta → fetal haemolysis.

### Placental & Vascular Pathophysiology
* **Pre-eclampsia (先兆子癇)**: Defective trophoblast invasion → impaired spiral artery remodeling → placental ischemia → maternal systemic endothelial dysfunction.

## 3. Clinical Assessment (History, Abdominal, Speculum & Bimanual Exam Findings ONLY)

### First Visit (Booking) Maternal History
* **Demographics**: Age (note if ≥35 yrs).
* **Current Pregnancy**: LMP, EDD calculation, hyperemesis, vaginal bleeding.
* **Past Obstetric History**: G/P, previous FGR, macrosomia, GDM, pre-eclampsia, uterine scars, preterm birth.
* **Medical/Surgical History**: Chronic HTN, DM, autoimmune diseases (SLE).
* **Social History**: Smoking, alcohol, domestic violence.

### Routine Antenatal Physical Examination
* **Vitals**: BP (Normal <140/90 mmHg), HR.
* **General Assessment**: Pallor, edema, jaundice.
* **Anthropometry**: Height, Weight → BMI calculation.

### Obstetrical Abdominal Examination Findings
* **Inspection**: Linea nigra, striae gravidarum, surgical scars.
* **Palpation**: 
* **Fundal Height**: Palpable above symphysis at 12 wks, umbilicus at 22-24 wks, xiphoid at term.
* **Symphyseal Fundal Height (SFH)**: Measured in cm >24 wks.
* **Leopold Maneuvers (>30 wks)**: Fetal lie (longitudinal, transverse, oblique), presentation (vertex, breech), engagement.
* **Auscultation**: Fetal heart rate presence (Doptone).

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging

### Malpresentation Differentials at Term
| Presentation Type | Fetal Lie | Palpation Findings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breech (臀先露)** | Longitudinal | Hard round ballotable head at fundus; softer buttocks at pelvic brim. |
| **Shoulder (肩先露)** | Transverse / Oblique | Head in flank/iliac fossa; empty lower pole. |
| **Cephalic (Vertex)** | Longitudinal | Hard head at pelvic brim; soft buttocks at fundus. |

### Pre-Eclampsia Risk Stratification
| High Risk Factors (Requires 1) | Moderate Risk Factors (Requires ≥2) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Previous Pre-eclampsia | Primigravida |
| Chronic Kidney Disease | Age ≥40 yrs |
| Autoimmune disease (SLE, APS) | Pregnancy interval >10 yrs |
| Type 1 / Type 2 DM | Booking BMI ≥35 kg/m² |
| Chronic Hypertension | FHx of Pre-eclampsia / Multiple Pregnancy |

## 5. Key Investigations (Labs, Specific Ultrasound Criteria & CTG Tracings)

### First-Trimester Routine Bloods & Urinalysis
* **CBC**: Check Hb; if MCV <80 or 81 fL → check Hb electrophoresis for **Thalassaemia (地中海貧血)** (↑ HbA2 = Beta trait; HbH inclusion = Alpha trait).
* **Blood Group**: ABO & Rh status.
* **Infectious Screening**: HBsAg, anti-HIV, Syphilis, Rubella IgG.
* **Urine**: Dipstick for proteinuria (rule out pre-existing renal disease/PET) & glycosuria.

### Down Syndrome Screening (唐氏綜合症篩查)
* **First-Trimester Combined Screening (11-13 wks)**:
* **Ultrasound**: Nuchal Translucency (NT) (High risk if ≥3.5 mm).
* **Maternal Serum**: Free hCG & PAPP-A.
* **NIPT (無創產前DNA檢測)**: Maternal plasma cell-free fetal DNA; performed >10 wks (primary or secondary screen).
* **Invasive Diagnostics**: CVS or Amniocentesis indicated for high-risk screening or structural abnormalities.

### Second-Trimester Ultrasound Criteria
* **Fetal Anomaly USG (18-22 wks)**: Detects ~70% major congenital malformations.
* **Placental Localization**: Identifies low-lying placenta (repeat USG at 32 wks if overlapping os).

### Third-Trimester Investigations
* **Group B Streptococcus (B型鏈球菌) Screening**: Low vaginal + rectal swab at 35-37 wks (universal HA protocol).
* **OGTT (24-28 wks)**: Fasting plasma glucose ≥5.1 mmol/L; 1-hr ≥10.0 mmol/L; 2-hr 8.5-11.0 mmol/L.

## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization (Pharmacology, Steroids, Resuscitation)

### Pre-Pregnancy & Early Pregnancy Pharmacology
* **Folic Acid (葉酸)**: 400 mcg/day (5 mg/day if high risk for NTDs) until 12 wks.

### Prophylactic Antenatal Pharmacotherapy
* **Aspirin**: 75-150 mg/day PO from <16 wks to 36 wks for moderate/high pre-eclampsia risk.
* **LMWH**: Thromboprophylaxis based on VTE risk scoring.
* **Anti-D Immunoglobulin**: Administered to Rh -ve un-sensitized mothers at 28 & 34 wks (half dose) OR 28 wks (full dose), and for sensitizing events (e.g., APH, amnio, ECV).

### Antenatal Vaccinations
* **dTap Vaccine**: Given 26-34 wks for passive neonatal pertussis immunity.
* **Influenza & COVID-19 Vaccines**: Recommended during pregnancy.

### Antiviral & Antibiotic Prophylaxis
* **Tenofovir**: Given >28 wks for HBV carriers with high viral load to ↓ vertical transmission.
* **Intrapartum Antibiotics**: **IV Penicillin** (or Clindamycin/Erythromycin if allergic) for GBS +ve mothers, or GBS unknown with risk factors (PROM >18h, preterm, fever >38°C).

## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm (Timing/Mode of Delivery OR Gynaecological Surgery)

### Term Delivery Planning Algorithm
* **Timing of Discussion**: Mode of delivery discussed at 37 wks.
* **Uncomplicated Pregnancy**: Expectant management for spontaneous vertex vaginal delivery.
* **Prolonged Pregnancy**: Discuss **Induction of Labour (催產)** at 41 wks. 
* **Membrane Sweeping**: Offered at 40 wks (primiparous) or 41 wks (multiparous) to ↓ IOL rate.

### Malpresentation Management (37 wks)
* **Breech**: Offer **External Cephalic Version (ECV)** at 37 wks. If unsuccessful/declined → Elective LSCS.
* **Transverse/Oblique Lie**: Admit at 37 wks (risk of cord prolapse). ECV at 39 wks → Stabilizing induction if successful → LSCS if failed.

### Previous LSCS Management
* **Options**: Vaginal Birth After Caesarean (VBAC) vs. Repeat Elective LSCS. Discuss risks (VBAC: ~0.5% uterine rupture; LSCS: morbidly adherent placenta in future).

## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up

### Neonatal Immunoprophylaxis
* **HBV Post-Exposure**: HBIG + Hepatitis B vaccine administered to neonate immediately post-partum if mother is HBsAg +ve.

### Maternal Post-Partum Prophylaxis
* **Rh-Negative Mothers**: Check fetal cord blood Rh status. Give **Anti-D IgG** post-partum if baby is Rh +ve.

## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications

### Fetal & Neonatal Complications
* **Unscreened GBS**: Early-onset neonatal GBS sepsis, meningitis, pneumonia (high mortality).
* **Alloimmunization**: Fetal anaemia, hydrops fetalis.
* **Advanced Maternal Age**: ↑ Fetal Aneuploidy, **Fetal Growth Restriction (FGR)**, Stillbirth (死產).

### Maternal Complications
* **Missed Screening**: Undiagnosed severe pre-eclampsia leading to eclampsia, HELLP syndrome, placental abruption, or maternal mortality.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls

* Viva Question: When should NIPT be performed? Answer: From 10 weeks gestation onwards.
* Viva Question: What is the normal pH of the vagina during pregnancy? Answer: pH 3.5-6 (acidic environment protects against bacteria but increases susceptibility to candida).
* Trap: Assuming a normal MCV (<80 fL) with normal Ferritin rules out Thalassaemia. Pearl: Normal Ferritin with low MCV highly suggests Alpha Thalassaemia trait; test for SEA deletion of alpha globin gene.
* Pitfall: Performing a digital vaginal exam on a patient presenting with APH before ultrasound. Always confirm placental location via USG first to avoid provoking massive hemorrhage in Placenta Praevia.
