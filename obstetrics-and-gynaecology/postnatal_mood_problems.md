# Postnatal Mood Problems (產後情緒問題)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology
### Definition of Postpartum Psychiatric Disorders
* Spectrum of psychological and psychiatric conditions occurring in the puerperium (產褥期) following childbirth
* Encompasses three main categories: Postpartum Blues, Postpartum Depression, and Postpartum Psychosis

### Local Epidemiology of Postnatal Mood Problems
* **Postpartum Blues (產後心緒不良)**: ~50% of postpartum women
* **Postpartum Depression (產後抑鬱症)**: ~10% of postpartum women
* **Postpartum Psychosis (產後精神病)**: ~0.1% of postpartum women

## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy (Maternal-Fetal Physiology)
### Pathophysiology of Postnatal Mood Problems
* Rapid delivery of placenta → sudden ↓ in **Estrogen (雌激素)** and **Progesterone (孕酮)** triggering mood lability
* ↑ physiological stress + sleep deprivation + psychosocial adjustment to motherhood
* Disrupted circadian rhythm → altered serotonin/dopamine neurotransmission

### Endocrine Axis in Postpartum Period
* ↓ corticotropin-releasing hormone (CRH) post-placental delivery → transient maternal adrenal suppression
* Postpartum thyroid axis fluctuations → potential for postpartum thyroiditis mimicking depression symptoms

## 3. Clinical Assessment (History, Abdominal, Speculum & Bimanual Exam Findings ONLY)
### Maternal History in Postnatal Mood Problems
* Onset timing: <1 wk (Blues), within 6 mo (Depression/Psychosis)
* Symptoms: Tearfulness, insomnia, extreme fatigue, worthlessness, emotional instability
* **Red Flag Symptoms**: Ideas of self-harm, suicide, harming baby (infanticide)
* **Psychosis Symptoms**: Loss of insight, visual/auditory hallucinations, delusions regarding infant

### Clinical Exam Findings in Postnatal Mood Problems
* General appearance: Tearful, flat affect, poor eye contact, disheveled, distressed
* Behavior: Neglect of personal hygiene, impaired bonding/avoidance of baby, psychomotor retardation or agitation
* Abdominal/Pelvic Exam: Normal involution of uterus (子宮復舊) and normal lochia (惡露), unless concurrent puerperal infection exacerbates mental state

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging (e.g., FIGO if applicable)
### Differential Diagnoses of Postnatal Mood Problems
* Postpartum Thyroiditis (hyperthyroid or hypothyroid phases)
* Severe Postpartum Anemia (fatigue mimicking depression)
* Puerperal Sepsis (delirium mimicking psychosis)
* Pre-existing Bipolar Disorder or Schizophrenia

### Staging & Comparison of Postnatal Psychiatric Conditions
| Condition | Onset | Duration | Key Clinical Features | Self-Harm/Infanticide Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Postpartum Blues (產後心緒不良)** | 1st week | < 1 week | Transient tearfulness, emotional instability | Nil |
| **Postpartum Depression (產後抑鬱症)** | 1st 6 months | Prolonged | Low mood, anhedonia, poor sleep/appetite | Moderate to High |
| **Postpartum Psychosis (產後精神病)** | Days to weeks | Variable | Loss of insight, hallucinations, delusions | Extremely High |

## 5. Key Investigations (Labs, Specific Ultrasound Criteria & CTG Tracings)
### Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS)
* Self-administered screening questionnaire evaluating symptoms over past 7 days
* **HK / Chinese Cutoff**: $\ge$ 11 detects most cases (Standard Western cutoff is 13)
* Question 10 explicitly screens for self-harm ideation (immediate action required if +ve)

### Laboratory Investigations for Postnatal Mood Problems
* **TFT**: Exclude hypo/hyperthyroidism (Postpartum Thyroiditis)
* **CBC**: Exclude severe anemia (Hb < 8 g/dL) contributing to extreme fatigue and poor coping
* **Vit B12 / Folate / Iron studies**: Assess nutritional deficiencies linked to mood lability

## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization (Pharmacology, Steroids, Resuscitation)
### Pharmacological Management of Postpartum Depression
* **Antidepressants (抗抑鬱藥)**: SSRIs (e.g., Sertraline) as 1st-line therapy
* Monitor for infant side effects if breastfeeding (most SSRIs have safe Relative Infant Dose <10%)

### Acute Stabilization of Postpartum Psychosis
* **Antipsychotics (抗精神病藥)**: Atypical agents for acute stabilization
* Sedatives/Hypnotics for severe insomnia or extreme agitation

### Psychological Interventions
* **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**: 1st-line non-pharmacological management for depression
* Simple supportive therapy + reassurance: Sufficient for Postpartum Blues

## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm (Timing/Mode of Delivery OR Gynaecological Surgery)
### Definitive Psychiatric Triage Algorithm
* Mild (Blues) → Simple reassurance + family support + routine follow-up
* Moderate (Depression without self-harm) → CBT +/- SSRI via Outpatient Clinic / MCHC
* Severe (Depression with self-harm ideation OR Psychosis) → Urgent psychiatric consultation + Inpatient admission

### Infant Safety Algorithm
* If severe depression/psychosis identified → Immediately separate infant from mother if unsupervised
* Involve Medical Social Worker (MSW) + Paediatrician for child protection and social care

## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up
### Post-partum Follow-up & Support
* Frequent follow-up via **Maternal and Child Health Centre (MCHC)**
* Community support: Medical social worker, clinical psychologist, support groups
* Lactation support to ↓ maternal stress; safely suppress lactation if psychotropic drugs contraindicated

### Contraception Considerations
* Prevent immediate unplanned pregnancy to minimize compound stress
* **Progesterone-only pill (POP)** or **Levonorgestrel IUS (Mirena)**
* Avoid estrogen-containing methods initially to prevent further hormonal mood fluctuations and preserve lactation

## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications
### Maternal Complications of Postnatal Mood Problems
* Prolonged clinical depression
* High risk of recurrence of depression/psychosis in future pregnancies
* Self-harm or suicide (major cause of maternal mortality)

### Infant Complications of Postnatal Mood Problems
* Infanticide
* Impaired mother-child bonding
* Delayed infant cognitive and psychosocial development
* Early cessation of breastfeeding

## 10. Examiner's Pearls
* Viva Question: What is the EPDS cutoff score for Chinese women in Hong Kong? Answer: A cutoff score of 11 is used to detect most cases of postpartum depression in Chinese women, compared to 13 in Western studies.
* Trap: Assuming transient tearfulness at day 14 is just postpartum blues. Pearl: Postpartum blues resolve within the first week; symptoms persisting beyond 1 week or starting later strongly indicate postpartum depression.
* Pitfall: Discharging a mother with postpartum psychosis who promises to "not hurt the baby". Postpartum psychosis involves a complete loss of insight and hallucinations; urgent psychiatric admission is mandatory regardless of maternal reassurances.
