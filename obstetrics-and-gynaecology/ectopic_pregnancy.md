# Ectopic Pregnancy (宮外孕)

## 1. Clinical Definition, Gestational Context & Local Epidemiology
### Clinical Definition
* **Ectopic Pregnancy** (宮外孕): Implantation of fertilized ovum outside endometrial cavity.
### Local Epidemiology
* Incidence: ~1-2% of all pregnancies in Hong Kong.
* Leading cause of 1st-trimester maternal mortality.

## 2. Pathophysiology, Endocrine Axis & Pelvic Anatomy (Maternal-Fetal Physiology)
### Pathophysiology
* ↓ Tubal ciliary motility → delayed ovum transport → premature blastocyst hatching & implantation in tubal mucosa.
* Trophoblast invasion degrades tubal wall → risk of tubal rupture & massive intraperitoneal hemorrhage.
### Anatomical Distribution
* **Ampulla** (壺腹部): ~70-80%
* **Isthmus** (峽部): ~12%
* **Fimbrial** (傘端): ~5%
* **Interstitial/Cornual** (間質部/宮角): ~2-3% (Highest risk of massive, life-threatening hemorrhage).
* Ovarian, Cervical, Abdominal: <1%.
### Major Risk Factors
* **Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)** (盆腔炎)
* Previous ectopic pregnancy
* Previous tubal surgery/ligation
* **Endometriosis** (子宮內膜異位症)
* Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART/IVF)
* **Intrauterine Device (IUD)** (子宮環) in situ (if pregnancy occurs, ↑ chance of ectopic).

## 3. Clinical Assessment (History, Abdominal, Speculum & Bimanual Exam Findings ONLY)
### Maternal History & Symptoms
* **Amenorrhoea** (停經): Typically 6-8 weeks from LMP.
* Sudden sharp unilateral lower abdominal pain.
* Scant dark vaginal bleeding (decidual sloughing).
* Shoulder tip pain (diaphragmatic irritation from haemoperitoneum).
* Syncope/dizziness (hypovolemic shock).
### Abdominal Examination Findings
* Localized iliac fossa tenderness.
* Guarding & rebound tenderness (+ve if ruptured).
* Abdominal distension (haemoperitoneum).
### Speculum Examination Findings
* Closed cervical os.
* Scant dark blood in vaginal vault.
### Bimanual Examination Findings
* **Cervical Excitation** (子宮頸搖擺痛): Extreme pain on moving cervix.
* Palpable, tender adnexal mass.
* Boggy, bulging, tender **Pouch of Douglas** (德氏陷凹).

## 4. Critical Differential Diagnoses & Clinical Staging
### Key Differential Diagnoses
| Condition | Key Differentiating Feature |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Miscarriage** (流產) | Heavy bleeding, central cramping, open os. |
| **Ruptured Corpus Luteum** (黃體破裂) | Mid-luteal phase onset, sudden pain, -ve UPT (unless pregnant). |
| **Appendicitis** (盲腸炎) | Fever, migratory pain to RIF, GI symptoms, -ve UPT. |
| **PID** (盆腔炎) | Bilateral pain, purulent discharge, fever. |
| **Ovarian Torsion** (卵巢扭轉) | Ischemic colicky pain, vomiting, enlarged ovary on exam. |

## 5. Key Investigations (Labs, Specific Ultrasound Criteria & CTG Tracings)
### Laboratory Investigations
* Urine Pregnancy Test (UPT): +ve.
* Quantitative Serum **β-hCG**: Suboptimal doubling (<66% ↑ in 48 hrs).
* Complete Blood Picture (CBP): ↓ Hb (haemorrhage), normal/mild ↑ WBC.
* Group & Screen (G&S): Check Rh status for Anti-D.
* Clotting profile & LFT/RFT: Baseline needed for surgery/MTX.
### Transvaginal Ultrasound (TVUS) Criteria
* Empty uterine cavity.
* **Pseudogestational sac** (假孕囊): Central fluid collection without yolk sac/embryo.
* Adnexal mass separate from ovary (Blob sign, Bagel sign).
* **Ring of Fire** (火環徵) on colour Doppler.
* Free echogenic fluid in Pouch of Douglas (haemoperitoneum).

## 6. Medical Management & Acute Stabilization (Pharmacology, Steroids, Resuscitation)
### Acute Resuscitation Protocol
* ABCDE approach.
* Large-bore IV access x 2.
* Rapid IV crystalloid infusion.
* O2 supplementation.
* Group & Crossmatch (crossmatch 2-4 units if shocked).
### Pharmacological Management
* **Methotrexate (MTX)** (甲氨蝶呤): Folic acid antagonist, interrupts DNA synthesis of trophoblast.
* MTX Dose: 50 mg/m² IM single dose.
* **Anti-D Immunoglobulin**: Give to all Rh -ve, non-sensitized women.
* Analgesia: Paracetamol (Avoid NSAIDs if MTX planned due to renal clearance interaction).

## 7. Definitive Management Algorithm (Timing/Mode of Delivery OR Gynaecological Surgery)
### Management Algorithm Criteria
| Modality | Patient Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Expectant** | Clinically stable, pain-free, ↓ trend in hCG, initial hCG <1000 IU/L, adnexal mass <30mm, minimal free fluid. |
| **Medical (MTX)** | Haemodynamically stable, minimal pain, unruptured, adnexal mass <35mm, absent fetal heart, hCG <1500-5000 IU/L, compliant for follow-up. |
| **Surgical** | Haemodynamic instability, ruptured ectopic, severe pain, hCG >5000 IU/L, +ve fetal heart, MTX contraindicated/failed. |
### Gynaecological Surgical Principles
* **Laparoscopy** (腹腔鏡): Gold standard approach.
* **Salpingectomy** (輸卵管切除術): Preferred if tube severely damaged, contralateral tube healthy, ruptured tube, or no future fertility desire. ↓ risk of persistent trophoblast.
* **Salpingostomy** (輸卵管造口術): Linear incision to remove ectopic, tube left to heal by secondary intention. Indicated if contralateral tube damaged/absent & fertility highly desired.

## 8. Post-partum/Post-operative Care, Contraception & Follow-up
### Post-MTX Follow-up
* Check serum hCG on Day 4 & Day 7.
* Expect ≥15% ↓ between Day 4 & Day 7.
* If ↓ <15%, consider 2nd dose MTX or switch to surgery.
* Weekly hCG until <5 IU/L.
### Post-Operative Follow-up
* Routine wound care.
* Weekly hCG monitoring ONLY strictly required post-salpingostomy (rule out persistent trophoblast).
### Contraception & Future Pregnancy
* Delay pregnancy ≥3 months post-MTX (teratogenic risk).
* Progesterone-Only Pill (POP) or Combined Oral Contraceptive Pill (COCP) safe immediately post-op.
* Avoid IUD insertion until completely resolved.
* Early TVUS at 6 wks GA in future pregnancies to confirm intrauterine location.

## 9. Maternal, Fetal & Gynaecological Complications
### Maternal Complications
* Hypovolemic shock.
* Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC).
* Maternal death.
### Gynaecological Complications
* Infertility / subfertility.
* Recurrent ectopic pregnancy (10-15% risk).
* Persistent trophoblastic disease (if salpingostomy performed).
* Pelvic adhesion formation.

## 10. Examiner's Pearls
* Viva Question: What is the classic ultrasound triad for ectopic pregnancy? Answer: Empty uterus, adnexal mass, and free fluid in the Pouch of Douglas.
* Trap: Assuming a central fluid collection in the uterus is a normal early intrauterine pregnancy. Pearl: Identify the double decidual sign; a central collection without it is likely a pseudogestational sac of an ectopic pregnancy.
* Pitfall: Discharging a patient with an empty uterus on TVUS and a +ve pregnancy test without follow-up. Do serial serum hCG tests 48 hours apart to track doubling time and safely manage a Pregnancy of Unknown Location (PUL).
