# Osteoporosis

## General

**Examiner's Pearl**: DEXA scan T-score ≤ -2.5 defines osteoporosis. Osteopenia is -1.0 to -2.5.
**Answer**: DEXA掃描T評分 ≤ -2.5定義為骨質疏鬆症。骨質減少為 -1.0 至 -2.5。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Risk factors: Age, female, early menopause, smoking, alcohol, low BMI, long-term steroids, rheumatoid arthritis.
**Answer**: 風險因素：年齡、女性、提早絕經、吸煙、飲酒、低BMI、長期使用類固醇、類風濕性關節炎。

**Examiner's Pearl**: FRAX tool estimates 10-year probability of major osteoporotic fracture.
**Answer**: FRAX工具估計10年內發生重大骨質疏鬆性骨折的機率。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Treatment: Calcium & Vitamin D, Bisphosphonates (Alendronate) - take on empty stomach, sit upright for 30 mins.
**Answer**: 治療：鈣和維生素D、雙膦酸鹽（阿侖膦酸鈉）- 空腹服用，保持直坐30分鐘。

## Station 1: Station 1 — Management Interview

**Scenario**: A 65-year-old woman presents for a follow-up after a DEXA scan showed a T-score of -2.8 in her lumbar spine. She is asymptomatic but worried about breaking bones.

### Section 1 — Assessment (20%): 4P + BATHE + ICE
PHYSICAL (4P):
- Presenting complaint: Asymptomatic, abnormal DEXA scan (T-score -2.8).
- Precipitating factors: Post-menopausal, age.
- Pattern: Chronic condition.
- Palliation/Provocation: N/A.

BATHE:
- Background: "We have the results of your bone density scan. How have you been feeling since the test?"
- Affect: "How does the thought of having weak bones make you feel?"
- Trouble: "What worries you the most about this result?"
- Handling: "Have you been taking any calcium or vitamin D supplements?"
- Empathy: "It is very common to feel worried when you hear you might be at risk of breaking a bone."

ICE:
- Ideas: "What do you understand about osteoporosis?"
- Concerns: "You mentioned being worried about breaking bones. Has anyone in your family had a hip fracture?"
- Expectations: "What were you hoping we could do today to protect your bones?"

### Section 2 — Problem Identification (10%)
PHYSICAL:
- Osteoporosis (T-score ≤ -2.5). High risk of fragility fractures.

PSYCHOLOGICAL:
- Anxiety regarding potential falls and fractures.

SOCIAL:
- Need to assess home safety to prevent falls.

BARRIERS:
- Potential non-compliance with long-term bisphosphonate therapy due to side effects.

### Section 3 — RAPRIOP Management (50%)
REASSURE:
"Although your bone density is low, there are very effective medications and lifestyle changes that can strengthen your bones and significantly reduce your risk of a fracture."

ADVICE (EXACT SCRIPTS):
- "Ensure you have enough calcium in your diet from dairy products or fortified foods, and get some safe sun exposure for Vitamin D."
- "Weight-bearing exercises, like walking or Tai Chi, are excellent for strengthening bones and improving balance."
- "Make your home safe to prevent falls. Remove loose rugs, ensure good lighting, and use non-slip mats in the bathroom."

PRESCRIBE:
- "I will prescribe a medication called a bisphosphonate (e.g., Alendronate) to help rebuild your bone. You must take it once a week, on an empty stomach, with a full glass of water, and stay upright for 30 minutes afterwards."
- Prescribe Calcium and Vitamin D supplements.

REFER:
- Refer to a dietitian for calcium intake advice or physiotherapy for balance training if needed.

INVESTIGATE:
- Check baseline renal function, calcium, and vitamin D levels before starting bisphosphonates.

OBSERVE:
- Monitor for side effects of bisphosphonates (e.g., reflux, jaw pain).

PLAN:
- Review in 3-6 months to check medication compliance. Repeat DEXA scan in 1-2 years.

### Section 4 — CIPS Communication Skills (20%)
CHUNK AND CHECK:
Explain how to take the bisphosphonate medication, then ask: "This medication has very specific instructions. Just to make sure I explained it clearly, how will you take this pill every week?"

ICE:
Directly address her fear of breaking bones by explaining how the medication and fall prevention strategies work together.

PATIENT-CENTRED:
"I know taking a new medication can be daunting. What questions do you have about the treatment plan?"

SIGNPOSTING:
"First, let's talk about what the scan results mean, and then we will discuss the medication and lifestyle changes to protect your bones."
