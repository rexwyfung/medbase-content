# Chronic Hepatitis B

## General

**Examiner's Pearl**: Endemic in Hong Kong; major risk for cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
**Answer**: 在香港屬風土病；是肝硬化和肝細胞癌（HCC）的主要風險。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Screening: HBsAg, anti-HBs. Monitoring: ALT, HBV DNA, AFP, Ultrasound every 6 months.
**Answer**: 篩查：HBsAg、anti-HBs。監測：每6個月檢查ALT、HBV DNA、AFP、超聲波。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Treatment (Antivirals like Entecavir/Tenofovir) indicated if high viral load + elevated ALT or cirrhosis.
**Answer**: 如果病毒載量高 + ALT升高或肝硬化，則需要治療（抗病毒藥物如恩替卡韋/替諾福韋）。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Family screening and vaccination for non-immune contacts is essential.
**Answer**: 家庭篩查和為無免疫力的接觸者接種疫苗至關重要。

## Station 1: Station 1 — Management Interview

**Scenario**: A 35-year-old man recently found out he is a Hepatitis B carrier during a routine health check. He is asymptomatic but very anxious about liver cancer and transmitting it to his pregnant wife.

### Section 1 — Assessment (20%): 4P + BATHE + ICE
PHYSICAL (4P):
- Presenting complaint: Asymptomatic, incidental finding of HBsAg positive.
- Precipitating factors: Routine health check.
- Pattern: Chronic carrier state.
- Palliation/Provocation: N/A.

BATHE:
- Background: "You recently found out you are a Hepatitis B carrier. How have you been feeling since you got the results?"
- Affect: "How is this news affecting your mood and daily life?"
- Trouble: "What is the most difficult part of this diagnosis for you?"
- Handling: "How have you been coping with the anxiety?"
- Empathy: "It is completely normal to feel anxious when you receive unexpected health news."

ICE:
- Ideas: "What do you know about being a Hepatitis B carrier?"
- Concerns: "You mentioned your pregnant wife. Are you worried about passing it to her or the baby?"
- Expectations: "What were you hoping we could discuss today to help ease your mind?"

### Section 2 — Problem Identification (10%)
PHYSICAL:
- Chronic Hepatitis B infection (HBsAg positive). Need to assess viral load, liver function, and screen for HCC.

PSYCHOLOGICAL:
- Severe anxiety regarding liver cancer and transmission to family.

SOCIAL:
- Concerns about the health of his pregnant wife and unborn child.

BARRIERS:
- Misconceptions about the modes of transmission and the inevitability of liver cancer.

### Section 3 — RAPRIOP Management (50%)
REASSURE:
"Being a carrier does not mean you have liver cancer. Many carriers live long, healthy lives without any liver problems. We will monitor you closely."

ADVICE (EXACT SCRIPTS):
- "Hepatitis B is transmitted through blood and body fluids. It is not spread by sharing food or hugging."
- "Your wife should get a blood test to check her immunity. If she is not immune, she can get the Hepatitis B vaccine, which is safe during pregnancy."
- "The baby will receive the Hepatitis B vaccine and immunoglobulin immediately after birth to prevent transmission."
- "Avoid alcohol, as it can further damage your liver."

PRESCRIBE:
- No antiviral medication needed currently if liver function is normal and viral load is low (pending investigation results).

REFER:
- Refer to a hepatologist or gastroenterologist for long-term monitoring if indicated.

INVESTIGATE:
- Liver function tests (LFTs), Alpha-fetoprotein (AFP), HBV DNA viral load, HBeAg/anti-HBe.
- Ultrasound of the liver to screen for cirrhosis and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).

OBSERVE:
- Monitor for signs of liver decompensation (jaundice, ascites).

PLAN:
- Review investigation results in 2-4 weeks. Regular monitoring (blood tests and ultrasound) every 6 months.

### Section 4 — CIPS Communication Skills (20%)
CHUNK AND CHECK:
Explain the transmission and prevention for the baby, then ask: "Just to make sure I explained it clearly, what steps will we take to protect your baby?"

ICE:
Directly address his fear of liver cancer by explaining the purpose of the regular ultrasound and AFP monitoring.

PATIENT-CENTRED:
"I know this is a lot of information. How are you feeling about the plan to monitor your liver health?"

SIGNPOSTING:
"First, let's talk about what being a carrier means for your health, and then we will discuss how to protect your wife and baby."
