# DM / HTN Screening

## General

**Examiner's Pearl**: HTN Screening: All adults ≥18 years. Annually if high normal or risk factors; every 3-5 years if normal.
**Answer**: 高血壓篩查：所有≥18歲成人。如果處於正常高值或有風險因素，則每年一次；如果正常，則每3-5年一次。

**Examiner's Pearl**: DM Screening: Adults ≥45 years, or younger if overweight (BMI ≥23 in Asians) + 1 risk factor (e.g., family history, physical inactivity).
**Answer**: 糖尿病篩查：≥45歲成人，或如果超重（亞洲人BMI≥23）+ 1個風險因素（例如：家族史、缺乏運動），則提早篩查。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Screening tests for DM: Fasting plasma glucose (FPG) or HbA1c.
**Answer**: 糖尿病篩查測試：空腹血糖（FPG）或HbA1c。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Diagnosis requires 2 abnormal results on different days (unless symptomatic with random glucose ≥11.1).
**Answer**: 診斷需要在不同日子有2次異常結果（除非有症狀且隨機血糖≥11.1）。

## Station 1: Station 1 — Management Interview

**Scenario**: A 45-year-old man comes in for a general check-up. His father recently had a heart attack. He is overweight and smokes 10 cigarettes a day. He wants to know if he has diabetes or high blood pressure.

### Section 1 — Assessment (20%): 4P + BATHE + ICE
PHYSICAL (4P):
- Presenting complaint: Request for DM/HTN screening.
- Precipitating factors: Father's recent heart attack.
- Pattern: Preventive health check.
- Palliation/Provocation: N/A.

BATHE:
- Background: "You mentioned your father recently had a heart attack. How is he doing now?"
- Affect: "How has his heart attack affected you and your thoughts about your own health?"
- Trouble: "What is your biggest worry regarding your health right now?"
- Handling: "Have you made any changes to your lifestyle since your father's heart attack?"
- Empathy: "It's a very scary experience when a close family member has a heart attack. It's completely understandable that you want to get checked."

ICE:
- Ideas: "Do you have any symptoms like feeling very thirsty, urinating frequently, or having headaches?"
- Concerns: "Are you worried that you might already have diabetes or high blood pressure?"
- Expectations: "What tests were you hoping we would do today?"

### Section 2 — Problem Identification (10%)
PHYSICAL:
- Multiple cardiovascular risk factors: Age >40, male, family history of premature CAD, overweight, smoking. High risk for metabolic syndrome.

PSYCHOLOGICAL:
- Health anxiety triggered by a family event.

SOCIAL:
- Need to address lifestyle habits (smoking, diet, exercise).

BARRIERS:
- Difficulty in quitting smoking or changing long-term dietary habits.

### Section 3 — RAPRIOP Management (50%)
REASSURE:
"It is excellent that you are taking a proactive step to check your health. Finding out early means we can take action to prevent heart disease."

ADVICE (EXACT SCRIPTS):
- "The most important thing you can do for your heart is to stop smoking. I can help you with nicotine replacement therapy if you are ready to quit."
- "Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise, like brisk walking, every week."
- "Try to eat a balanced diet with more vegetables and less saturated fat and salt to help manage your weight and blood pressure."

PRESCRIBE:
- Offer smoking cessation aids (e.g., NRT) if the patient is willing to quit.

REFER:
- Refer to a smoking cessation clinic or dietitian if appropriate.

INVESTIGATE:
- Measure Blood Pressure and BMI.
- Fasting blood glucose (or HbA1c) and fasting lipid profile.

OBSERVE:
- Monitor BP, weight, and smoking status at follow-up.

PLAN:
- Review in 1-2 weeks to discuss blood test results and formulate a long-term cardiovascular risk reduction plan.

### Section 4 — CIPS Communication Skills (20%)
CHUNK AND CHECK:
Explain the cardiovascular risk factors, then ask: "Just to make sure I explained it clearly, what are the main things we need to work on to protect your heart?"

ICE:
Directly address his concern about his father's heart attack by explaining how screening and lifestyle changes can prevent the same thing happening to him.

PATIENT-CENTRED:
"I know quitting smoking is very hard. Are you interested in discussing ways to make it easier, or would you prefer to focus on diet and exercise first?"

SIGNPOSTING:
"First, let's do some physical checks like your blood pressure, and then we will discuss the blood tests and lifestyle changes."
