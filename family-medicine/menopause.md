# Menopause / Menstrual Irregularity

## General

**Examiner's Pearl**: Menopause: 12 consecutive months of amenorrhea. Average age 51.
**Answer**: 更年期：連續12個月閉經。平均年齡51歲。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Symptoms: Vasomotor (hot flushes, night sweats), urogenital atrophy, mood changes, sleep disturbance.
**Answer**: 症狀：血管舒縮（潮熱、盜汗）、泌尿生殖系統萎縮、情緒變化、睡眠障礙。

**Examiner's Pearl**: HRT indications: Relief of vasomotor symptoms, prevention of osteoporosis. Contraindications: Undiagnosed bleeding, breast cancer, VTE history, active liver disease.
**Answer**: HRT適應症：緩解血管舒縮症狀、預防骨質疏鬆。禁忌症：未確診的出血、乳腺癌、VTE病史、活躍的肝病。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Post-menopausal bleeding (PMB) is endometrial cancer until proven otherwise. Requires urgent ultrasound/biopsy.
**Answer**: 絕經後出血（PMB）在證實不是之前，應視為子宮內膜癌。需要緊急超聲波/活檢。

## Station 1: Station 1 — Management Interview

**Scenario**: A 50-year-old woman complains of hot flushes, night sweats, and irregular periods for the past 6 months. She is having trouble sleeping and feels irritable. She asks about Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT).

### Section 1 — Assessment (20%): 4P + BATHE + ICE
PHYSICAL (4P):
- Presenting complaint: Hot flushes, night sweats, irregular periods, insomnia, irritability.
- Precipitating factors: Perimenopause/Menopause transition.
- Pattern: Ongoing for 6 months.
- Palliation/Provocation: Heat, stress, caffeine may worsen symptoms.

BATHE:
- Background: "You've been having these hot flushes and sleep problems for 6 months. How are you managing day-to-day?"
- Affect: "How is this affecting your mood and your relationships?"
- Trouble: "What is the most bothersome symptom for you right now?"
- Handling: "What have you tried so far to help with the hot flushes or sleep?"
- Empathy: "It sounds like a very exhausting and frustrating time for you."

ICE:
- Ideas: "What do you know about the menopause transition?"
- Concerns: "Do you have any specific concerns about HRT, like breast cancer risks?"
- Expectations: "You mentioned HRT. Are you hoping to start it today?"

### Section 2 — Problem Identification (10%)
PHYSICAL:
- Perimenopausal/Menopausal symptoms significantly impacting quality of life. Need to assess suitability and contraindications for HRT.

PSYCHOLOGICAL:
- Irritability, mood swings, and insomnia.

SOCIAL:
- Impact on work performance and personal relationships.

BARRIERS:
- Potential fears or misconceptions about the risks of HRT.

### Section 3 — RAPRIOP Management (50%)
REASSURE:
"These symptoms are a normal part of the menopause transition. You are not alone, and there are effective ways to manage them."

ADVICE (EXACT SCRIPTS):
- "Try wearing layers of clothing so you can easily take some off when a hot flush starts."
- "Avoid triggers like spicy foods, caffeine, and alcohol, especially before bed."
- "Regular exercise and relaxation techniques can help with both mood and sleep."

PRESCRIBE:
- Discuss HRT: "HRT is very effective for hot flushes and night sweats. We need to check your medical history to ensure it's safe for you. Since you still have your womb, you would need a combined HRT (estrogen and progesterone) to protect the womb lining."
- Alternative: If HRT is contraindicated or declined, discuss non-hormonal options (e.g., SSRIs/SNRIs for hot flushes).

REFER:
- Refer for mammogram or pelvic ultrasound if indicated before starting HRT.

INVESTIGATE:
- Usually a clinical diagnosis. FSH/LH tests are not routinely needed for women over 45.
- Check BP and BMI before starting HRT.

OBSERVE:
- Monitor symptom improvement and any side effects of HRT (e.g., bleeding, breast tenderness).

PLAN:
- Review in 3 months if starting HRT to assess effectiveness and side effects.

### Section 4 — CIPS Communication Skills (20%)
CHUNK AND CHECK:
Explain the risks and benefits of HRT, then ask: "Just to make sure I explained it clearly, what are your thoughts on starting HRT after hearing this?"

ICE:
Directly address her concerns about HRT risks, providing balanced, evidence-based information.

PATIENT-CENTRED:
"We can tailor the treatment to what you feel most comfortable with. Would you like to try lifestyle changes first, or are you keen to consider medication?"

SIGNPOSTING:
"First, let's talk about some practical lifestyle tips, and then we will discuss HRT in detail."
