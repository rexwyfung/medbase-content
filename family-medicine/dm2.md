# Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (二型糖尿病)

## 1. Definition & Epidemiology (定義與流行病學)
- **Definition**: A chronic metabolic disorder characterized by hyperglycemia resulting from a combination of insulin resistance and relative insulin deficiency.
- **Epidemiology**: Global epidemic. Affects ~10% of the Hong Kong population. Strongly associated with obesity, sedentary lifestyle, and aging.

## 2. Pathophysiology (病理生理學)
- **Insulin Resistance**: Decreased tissue sensitivity to insulin (especially in muscle, liver, and adipose tissue), leading to reduced glucose uptake and increased hepatic glucose production.
- **Beta-cell Dysfunction**: Progressive decline in pancreatic beta-cell function and insulin secretion. Initially, hyperinsulinemia compensates for resistance, but eventually, beta-cells fail, leading to overt hyperglycemia.
- **Incretin Defect**: Reduced incretin effect (GLP-1, GIP), which normally stimulates insulin release after meals.

## 3. Clinical Features (臨床特徵)
- Often asymptomatic in early stages (diagnosed via screening).
- **Classic Symptoms**: Polyuria (多尿 - osmotic diuresis), Polydipsia (多飲 - secondary to dehydration), Polyphagia (多食), unexplained weight loss (more common in Type 1, but can occur in severe Type 2).
- **Other Symptoms**: Fatigue, blurred vision (osmotic changes in lens), recurrent infections (e.g., candidiasis, UTI, skin infections), poor wound healing, numbness/tingling in hands/feet (neuropathy).

## 4. Diagnosis & Screening (診斷與篩查)
### Diagnostic Criteria (WHO/ADA)
Requires TWO abnormal test results from the same sample or in two separate test samples (unless classic symptoms are present, then one is enough):
1. **Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG)**: ≥ 7.0 mmol/L (fasting for ≥ 8 hours).
2. **HbA1c**: ≥ 6.5% (48 mmol/mol).
3. **2-hour Plasma Glucose during 75g OGTT**: ≥ 11.1 mmol/L.
4. **Random Plasma Glucose**: ≥ 11.1 mmol/L *with* classic symptoms of hyperglycemia.

### Pre-diabetes (糖尿病前期)
High risk for developing overt DM and cardiovascular disease.
- **Impaired Fasting Glucose (IFG)**: FPG 6.1 - 6.9 mmol/L (WHO criteria).
- **Impaired Glucose Tolerance (IGT)**: 2-hour OGTT glucose 7.8 - 11.0 mmol/L.
- **HbA1c**: 5.7% - 6.4% (ADA criteria).

## 5. Management (治療與管理)
### Treatment Targets
- **HbA1c**: Generally < 7.0%. (Tighter < 6.5% for young, newly diagnosed; looser < 8.0% for elderly with comorbidities/hypoglycemia risk).
- **Blood Pressure**: < 130/80 mmHg.
- **Lipids**: LDL < 2.6 mmol/L (or < 1.8 mmol/L if established CVD).

### Lifestyle Modifications (First-line & Ongoing)
- **Medical Nutrition Therapy**: Low glycemic index (GI) foods, portion control, reduced refined sugars and saturated fats.
- **Weight Loss**: Target 5-10% weight loss if overweight/obese.
- **Exercise**: ≥ 150 mins/week of moderate aerobic exercise + resistance training.

### Pharmacological Therapy
- **First-line**: **Metformin (二甲雙胍)**
  - *Mechanism*: Decreases hepatic glucose production, increases insulin sensitivity.
  - *Benefits*: Weight neutral/loss, no hypoglycemia, cardiovascular benefits.
  - *Side effects*: GI upset (diarrhea, nausea - take with food, start low dose), Vitamin B12 deficiency.
  - *Contraindications*: eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m² (risk of lactic acidosis).
- **Second-line / Add-on Therapies**:
  - **SGLT2 Inhibitors** (e.g., Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin):
    - *Mechanism*: Inhibit renal glucose reabsorption, causing glycosuria.
    - *Benefits*: Weight loss, BP reduction, **proven cardiovascular and renal protection** (first choice if established ASCVD, HF, or CKD).
    - *Side effects*: Genital mycotic infections, UTIs, euglycemic DKA (rare but serious).
  - **GLP-1 Receptor Agonists** (e.g., Liraglutide, Semaglutide):
    - *Mechanism*: Stimulate insulin, inhibit glucagon, slow gastric emptying, promote satiety.
    - *Benefits*: Significant weight loss, **proven cardiovascular protection**.
    - *Side effects*: GI upset (nausea, vomiting), pancreatitis (rare). Injectable (mostly).
  - **DPP-4 Inhibitors** (e.g., Sitagliptin, Linagliptin):
    - *Mechanism*: Inhibit breakdown of endogenous incretins (GLP-1).
    - *Benefits*: Weight neutral, low hypoglycemia risk. Well-tolerated.
  - **Sulfonylureas (SU)** (e.g., Gliclazide, Glimepiride):
    - *Mechanism*: Stimulate insulin secretion from beta-cells.
    - *Side effects*: **Hypoglycemia**, weight gain. Use with caution in elderly.
  - **Insulin**: Required if HbA1c is very high (>10%), symptomatic, or oral agents fail. Basal insulin (e.g., Glargine) is usually started first.

## 6. Complications & Screening (併發症與篩查)
### Microvascular Complications
- **Diabetic Retinopathy (糖尿病視網膜病變)**: Leading cause of blindness.
  - *Screening*: Annual retinal photography.
- **Diabetic Nephropathy (糖尿病腎病)**: Leading cause of ESRD.
  - *Screening*: Annual urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (ACR) and serum creatinine (eGFR).
  - *Treatment*: ACEi or ARB if microalbuminuria is present, regardless of BP. SGLT2 inhibitors.
- **Diabetic Neuropathy (糖尿病神經病變)**:
  - *Peripheral*: "Glove and stocking" sensory loss, pain, risk of diabetic foot ulcers.
  - *Autonomic*: Gastroparesis, erectile dysfunction, postural hypotension.
  - *Screening*: Annual comprehensive foot exam (monofilament testing, pulses).

### Macrovascular Complications
- **Coronary Artery Disease (IHD/MI)**.
- **Cerebrovascular Disease (Stroke/TIA)**.
- **Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD)**.
- *Management*: Aggressive control of BP, lipids (Statins), and antiplatelet therapy if indicated.

### Acute Complications
- **Hypoglycemia**: Blood glucose < 3.9 mmol/L. Symptoms: sweating, tremor, palpitations, confusion, coma. Treatment: fast-acting carbohydrates (e.g., glucose tablets/drink).
- **Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)**: More common in Type 1, but can occur in Type 2 under severe stress.
- **Hyperosmolar Hyperglycemic State (HHS)**: Severe hyperglycemia, hyperosmolality, and dehydration without significant ketoacidosis. High mortality.
