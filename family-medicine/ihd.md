# Ischemic Heart Disease (IHD) (缺血性心臟病)

## 1. Definition & Epidemiology (定義與流行病學)
- **Definition**: A condition where there is an imbalance between myocardial oxygen supply and demand, most commonly due to atherosclerotic narrowing of the coronary arteries. Also known as Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) or Coronary Heart Disease (CHD).
- **Epidemiology**: A leading cause of death globally. Incidence increases with age. More common in men, but risk in women increases significantly post-menopause.

## 2. Pathophysiology (病理生理學)
- **Atherosclerosis (動脈粥樣硬化)**: The fundamental process. Endothelial injury allows LDL cholesterol to enter the intima, where it is oxidized and engulfed by macrophages, forming foam cells and fatty streaks. Over time, a fibrous cap forms over a lipid-rich necrotic core, creating an atheromatous plaque.
- **Stable Angina**: A stable plaque causes fixed narrowing (usually >70% stenosis) of a coronary artery. At rest, blood flow is adequate. During exertion or stress, oxygen demand increases, but flow cannot increase sufficiently, leading to transient ischemia and pain.
- **Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)**: Plaque rupture or erosion exposes thrombogenic material, leading to platelet aggregation and thrombus formation.
  - *Unstable Angina (UA) / NSTEMI*: Partial or intermittent occlusion.
  - *STEMI*: Complete, prolonged occlusion leading to transmural myocardial infarction (necrosis).

## 3. Clinical Features (臨床特徵)
### Stable Angina (穩定性心絞痛)
- **Character**: Central or retrosternal chest discomfort (often described as pressure, tightness, heaviness, or squeezing, rather than sharp pain).
- **Radiation**: May radiate to the left arm, neck, jaw, or back.
- **Precipitants**: Brought on by exertion, emotional stress, cold weather, or heavy meals.
- **Relief**: Relieved by rest or sublingual Nitroglycerin (GTN) within a few minutes.
- *Atypical presentations*: Dyspnea on exertion (angina equivalent), fatigue, or epigastric discomfort (more common in women, elderly, and diabetics).

### Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)
- Pain is similar in character to stable angina but is **more severe, lasts longer (>20 minutes), occurs at rest**, or is new-onset/rapidly worsening (crescendo angina).
- Not fully relieved by rest or GTN.
- Associated with autonomic symptoms: Diaphoresis (sweating), nausea, vomiting, pallor, sense of impending doom.

## 4. Assessment & Investigations (評估與檢查)
### For Suspected Stable Angina
- **Resting ECG**: Often normal. May show evidence of past MI (Q waves) or resting ischemia (T wave inversion, ST depression).
- **Blood Tests**: Fasting lipid profile, HbA1c/Fasting glucose, U&Es, CBC, TFTs (to identify risk factors and exacerbating conditions like anemia or thyrotoxicosis).
- **Non-Invasive Functional Testing (to provoke ischemia)**:
  - Exercise Tolerance Test (Treadmill ECG).
  - Stress Echocardiography (Dobutamine or Exercise).
  - Myocardial Perfusion Imaging (MPI - e.g., Thallium scan).
- **Anatomical Testing**:
  - CT Coronary Angiography (CTCA): Excellent negative predictive value to rule out CAD in low-intermediate risk patients.
- **Invasive Coronary Angiography**: The gold standard. Indicated if non-invasive tests are strongly positive, symptoms are refractory to medical therapy, or to plan revascularization.

### For Suspected ACS (Urgent)
- **12-Lead ECG (within 10 minutes)**: To differentiate STEMI (ST elevation, new LBBB) from NSTEMI/UA (ST depression, T wave inversion, or normal).
- **Cardiac Biomarkers (High-Sensitivity Troponin T or I)**: Elevated in NSTEMI and STEMI (indicating myocardial necrosis). Normal in Unstable Angina.

## 5. Management: Stable IHD (穩定性缺血性心臟病的治療)
Goals: Reduce symptoms (angina) and prevent cardiovascular events (MI and death).

### 1. Lifestyle Modifications & Risk Factor Control
- Smoking cessation (crucial).
- Diet (Mediterranean style), weight management, regular aerobic exercise.
- Strict control of Hypertension (<130/80 mmHg) and Diabetes.
- **Lipid Management**: High-intensity Statin (e.g., Atorvastatin 40-80mg) for all patients, regardless of baseline LDL, aiming for LDL-C < 1.4 mmol/L.

### 2. Pharmacological Therapy (Disease Modifying)
- **Antiplatelet Therapy**: Aspirin 75-100mg daily indefinitely. (Clopidogrel 75mg if aspirin allergic).
- **ACE Inhibitors / ARBs**: Indicated for all patients with IHD, especially if they have co-existing hypertension, diabetes, heart failure (LVEF ≤ 40%), or CKD.

### 3. Anti-Anginal Therapy (Symptom Relief)
- **Short-acting Nitrates**: Sublingual GTN spray or tablets for acute relief.
- **First-line Preventative**:
  - **Beta-blockers** (e.g., Bisoprolol, Metoprolol): Decrease heart rate and contractility, reducing myocardial oxygen demand.
  - **Calcium Channel Blockers (CCBs)**:
    - *Rate-limiting* (Diltiazem, Verapamil): Alternative to beta-blockers. Do not combine with beta-blockers (risk of heart block).
    - *Dihydropyridines* (Amlodipine): Cause vasodilation. Can be combined with beta-blockers.
- **Second-line Preventative**: Long-acting nitrates (Isosorbide mononitrate - requires a nitrate-free period to prevent tolerance), Ivabradine, Nicorandil, Trimetazidine.

### 4. Revascularization
- **Percutaneous Coronary Intervention (PCI - 俗稱「通波仔」)**: Angioplasty with stenting.
- **Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG - 俗稱「搭橋」)**: Surgical bypass.
- *Indications*: Unacceptable symptoms despite optimal medical therapy, or specific high-risk anatomy (e.g., Left Main disease, severe 3-vessel disease, especially with reduced LV function or diabetes) where CABG improves survival.

## 6. Complications (併發症)
- Acute Coronary Syndrome (MI).
- Heart Failure (Ischemic cardiomyopathy).
- Arrhythmias (Atrial fibrillation, Ventricular tachycardia/fibrillation leading to sudden cardiac death).
- Valvular dysfunction (e.g., ischemic mitral regurgitation due to papillary muscle dysfunction).
