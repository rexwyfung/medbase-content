# Stress / Adjustment Disorder

## General

**Examiner's Pearl**: Adjustment disorder: Emotional/behavioral symptoms within 3 months of an identifiable stressor.
**Answer**: 適應障礙：在可識別的壓力源出現後3個月內出現情緒/行為症狀。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Symptoms do not meet criteria for Major Depressive Disorder and resolve within 6 months after stressor ends.
**Answer**: 症狀不符合重度抑鬱症的標準，並在壓力源結束後6個月內消退。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Management: Psychological support, counseling, problem-solving. Avoid long-term benzodiazepines.
**Answer**: 管理：心理支持、諮詢、解決問題。避免長期使用苯二氮卓類藥物。

**Examiner's Pearl**: Always assess suicide risk.
**Answer**: 始終評估自殺風險。

## Station 1: Station 1 — Management Interview

**Scenario**: A 28-year-old woman complains of poor sleep, fatigue, and frequent crying spells for 3 weeks after losing her job. She feels overwhelmed but denies suicidal thoughts.

### Section 1 — Assessment (20%): 4P + BATHE + ICE
PHYSICAL (4P):
- Presenting complaint: Insomnia, fatigue, crying spells, feeling overwhelmed.
- Precipitating factors: Job loss 3 weeks ago.
- Pattern: Acute onset following the stressor.
- Palliation/Provocation: Thinking about finances worsens anxiety.

BATHE:
- Background: "You mentioned you lost your job 3 weeks ago. How have things been since then?"
- Affect: "How is this situation affecting your mood day-to-day?"
- Trouble: "What is the most difficult part of dealing with this job loss?"
- Handling: "How have you been coping with the stress and the sleep problems?"
- Empathy: "Losing a job is a major life event, and it's completely understandable that you are feeling overwhelmed right now."

ICE:
- Ideas: "What do you think is causing your sleep problems and fatigue?"
- Concerns: "Are you worried about your financial situation or finding a new job?"
- Expectations: "What were you hoping we could do today to help you cope?"

### Section 2 — Problem Identification (10%)
PHYSICAL:
- Adjustment disorder with depressed mood/anxiety. Need to rule out Major Depressive Disorder (MDD).

PSYCHOLOGICAL:
- Distress, tearfulness, insomnia related to acute psychosocial stressor.

SOCIAL:
- Unemployment, potential financial strain, loss of routine.

BARRIERS:
- Stigma associated with seeking mental health support.

### Section 3 — RAPRIOP Management (50%)
REASSURE:
"What you are experiencing is a normal reaction to a very stressful event. It is called an adjustment disorder, and with the right support, it will get better."

ADVICE (EXACT SCRIPTS):
- "Try to maintain a daily routine, even if you are not working. Wake up and go to bed at the same time every day."
- "Engage in activities you enjoy or find relaxing, like exercising, reading, or spending time with supportive friends."
- "Avoid using alcohol or sleeping pills to cope, as they can make the problem worse in the long run."

PRESCRIBE:
- Antidepressants are not usually the first-line treatment for adjustment disorder.
- "I can prescribe a short course of a mild sleeping aid (e.g., Melatonin or a low-dose antihistamine) to help you get back into a sleep routine for a few days."

REFER:
- Refer to a counselor, clinical psychologist, or social worker for supportive therapy and stress management techniques.

INVESTIGATE:
- No specific investigations needed unless an organic cause for fatigue is suspected (e.g., TFTs, FBC).

OBSERVE:
- Monitor mood, sleep, and assess for development of depressive symptoms or suicidal ideation.

PLAN:
- Review in 1-2 weeks to check on coping and sleep.

### Section 4 — CIPS Communication Skills (20%)
CHUNK AND CHECK:
Explain the concept of adjustment disorder, then ask: "Just to make sure I explained it clearly, how do you understand what you are going through right now?"

ICE:
Directly address her feeling of being overwhelmed by validating her experience and offering practical support (counseling).

PATIENT-CENTRED:
"I know it feels very dark right now. We will work together to help you through this difficult transition."

SIGNPOSTING:
"First, let's talk about some ways to help you sleep better naturally, and then we will discuss getting you some counseling support."
