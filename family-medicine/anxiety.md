# Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) (廣泛性焦慮症)

## 1. Definition & Epidemiology (定義與流行病學)
- **Definition**: Excessive, uncontrollable worry and anxiety about multiple everyday events or activities (e.g., work, health, finances, family), occurring more days than not for at least 6 months, accompanied by somatic symptoms.
- **Epidemiology**: Lifetime prevalence is ~5-6%. Twice as common in women. Often co-occurs with depression (high comorbidity) and other anxiety disorders (panic disorder, social phobia).

## 2. Pathophysiology (病理生理學)
- **Neurobiology**: Hyperactivity of the amygdala (fear center) and decreased inhibitory control from the prefrontal cortex. Dysregulation of neurotransmitters: GABA (decreased inhibitory tone), Serotonin (5-HT), and Norepinephrine (NE).
- **Genetics**: Moderate heritability (~30%).
- **Psychological**: Cognitive distortions (catastrophizing, overestimating threat), intolerance of uncertainty.

## 3. Clinical Features (臨床特徵)
Diagnosis (DSM-5) requires excessive anxiety and worry for ≥ 6 months, about a number of events, which the individual finds difficult to control.
- **Associated Symptoms (Requires ≥ 3 for adults, 1 for children)**:
  1. Restlessness or feeling keyed up or on edge.
  2. Being easily fatigued.
  3. Difficulty concentrating or mind going blank.
  4. Irritability.
  5. Muscle tension (e.g., neck/shoulder pain, headaches).
  6. Sleep disturbance (difficulty falling or staying asleep, or restless, unsatisfying sleep).
- **Somatic Presentations**: Patients frequently present to primary care with physical symptoms rather than complaining of "anxiety." Common somatic complaints include:
  - GI: Nausea, diarrhea, IBS-like symptoms.
  - CVS: Palpitations, chest tightness (must rule out cardiac causes).
  - Neuro: Tension headaches, dizziness, trembling.

## 4. Assessment & Investigations (評估與檢查)
- **Screening**: GAD-7 questionnaire. Score 5-9 (Mild), 10-14 (Moderate), 15-21 (Severe). Score ≥ 10 warrants further evaluation and possible treatment.
- **Rule out organic causes (Medical mimics of anxiety)**:
  - **Endocrine**: Hyperthyroidism (check TFTs), Pheochromocytoma, Hypoglycemia.
  - **Cardiovascular**: Arrhythmias (e.g., SVT, AF - check ECG), Angina.
  - **Respiratory**: Asthma, COPD, Pulmonary Embolism.
  - **Neurological**: Vestibular dysfunction, seizures.
- **Substance/Medication-induced**:
  - Intoxication: Caffeine (high intake), Stimulants (amphetamines, cocaine), Salbutamol, Decongestants.
  - Withdrawal: Alcohol, Benzodiazepines, Opiates.
- **Assess Comorbidities**: Screen for Major Depressive Disorder (PHQ-9) and suicide risk.

## 5. Management (治療與管理)
Treatment involves a combination of psychological and pharmacological interventions.

### Psychological Therapy (First-line)
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT - 認知行為治療)**: The most effective psychological treatment. Involves:
  - *Cognitive restructuring*: Identifying and challenging catastrophic thinking and intolerance of uncertainty.
  - *Relaxation training*: Progressive muscle relaxation, deep breathing.
  - *Worry exposure*: Scheduling "worry time" and confronting feared scenarios.
- **Applied Relaxation**: Focuses on recognizing early signs of tension and using relaxation techniques to counteract them.

### Pharmacological Therapy
- **First-line**: Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs) or Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors (SNRIs).
  - *Examples*: Escitalopram (艾司西酞普蘭), Sertraline (舍曲林), Venlafaxine (文拉法辛), Duloxetine.
  - *Counseling*: Start at a lower dose than used for depression (e.g., Escitalopram 5mg) to minimize initial activation/jitteriness, then titrate up. Takes 2-4 weeks to work. Continue for at least 6-12 months after remission before considering tapering.
- **Second-line**:
  - Pregabalin (普瑞巴林): Binds to voltage-gated calcium channels. Effective for somatic symptoms of anxiety.
  - Buspirone: 5-HT1A partial agonist. Non-sedating, no abuse potential, but takes weeks to work.
- **Short-term relief (Use with extreme caution)**:
  - **Benzodiazepines** (e.g., Lorazepam, Diazepam): Provide rapid relief of severe acute anxiety. **Must be limited to 2-4 weeks maximum** due to the high risk of tolerance, dependence, and withdrawal. Use only during the initiation phase of SSRIs or for acute crises.

### Lifestyle & Social Interventions
- **Caffeine and Alcohol Reduction**: Both can exacerbate anxiety symptoms.
- **Exercise**: Regular aerobic exercise reduces baseline anxiety levels.
- **Sleep Hygiene**: Essential, as insomnia exacerbates anxiety.
- **Stress Management**: Mindfulness, meditation, yoga.

## 6. Complications (併發症)
- Chronic impairment in occupational and social functioning.
- Increased risk of developing Major Depressive Disorder.
- Substance abuse (self-medication with alcohol or sedatives).
- Increased risk of cardiovascular disease (due to chronic autonomic hyperarousal).
